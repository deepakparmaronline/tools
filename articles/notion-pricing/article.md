# Notion Pricing: Plans and AI Credits

**Short answer:** Notion lists Free at US$0 per member/month, Plus at US$10, Business at US$20 and Enterprise at a custom price. The pricing page has a monthly and yearly selector. Check the selected billing mode, taxes and extra AI credits before you compare the total cost.

## Notion plan prices

Plan | Published price | Best fit | Important limits or features
Free | US$0 per member/month | One person or a small trial | Collaborative blocks are limited for 2+ members; 5 MB per file; 7-day page history; limited AI trial; 10 external guests.
Plus | US$10 per member/month | Small teams and professionals | Up to 5 GB per file; 30-day page history; custom forms and sites, charts and basic connections; limited AI trial.
Business | US$20 per member/month | Growing teams | Up to 5 GB per file; 90-day history; Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search beta, SAML SSO, private teamspaces and granular database permissions.
Enterprise | Custom pricing | Organizations with advanced controls | Custom page history, SCIM, audit logs, domain management, advanced security and zero data retention with Notion AI providers.

These are the prices displayed on Notion's US pricing page when checked on November 8, 2026. Notion lets you select monthly or yearly billing and says yearly billing can save up to 20%. The displayed per-member price is not a full invoice estimate. Add the number of paid seats, any paid guest or domain needs, taxes and AI credit use. The [official pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is the source of truth at checkout.

## What the Free plan includes

Free is useful for an individual who wants notes, pages, databases and a personal workspace. The pricing page says the plan limits collaborative blocks for workspaces with two or more members. That means a team may reach the limit while it is still building its first shared processes. Free file uploads are limited to 5 MB each, and page history goes back seven days. The external guest limit is 10.

Free includes a trial of Notion AI, basic forms, basic sites, Notion Calendar and databases with subtasks, dependencies and custom properties. It is a good way to learn the editor and check whether the team likes the page and database model. It should not be treated as a promise that every team workflow will remain free as membership and content grow.

## What Plus adds

Plus is listed at US$10 per member/month. It adds collaborative blocks, custom forms, custom sites, charts and basic connections. File uploads can reach 5 GB each, and page history is 30 days. The plan still lists Notion AI as a limited trial rather than the full Business AI package.

For a team that mainly needs more room for shared pages, custom forms and charts, Plus may be enough. It is not the plan to choose only because you want Notion Agent or AI Meeting Notes. Those are listed under Business. If you publish a Notion site on a custom domain, the pricing page lists a separate domain charge of US$8 per month per domain with annual billing or US$10 per month per domain with monthly billing.

## What Business adds

Business is listed at US$20 per member/month. It includes Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search beta. It also adds SAML single sign-on, private teamspaces, granular database permissions and premium connections. The pricing comparison lists 90 days of page history and 5 GB per-file uploads.

Business is the plan to review when Notion is becoming a shared operating space rather than a personal notebook. Private teamspaces help separate sensitive areas, and granular database permissions can limit a collaborator to rows assigned to them. The extra controls matter only if the workspace owner defines a clear access policy and checks it as teams change.

## What Enterprise adds

Enterprise uses custom pricing. Notion lists zero data retention with its AI model providers, SCIM user provisioning, advanced security and controls, audit logs, domain management, security and compliance connections, workspace consolidation and a customer success manager. This plan is aimed at organizations that need admin controls across a larger workspace, not a small team that only wants more pages.

## Notion AI and Custom Agent costs

Free and Plus list a limited Notion AI trial. Business and Enterprise include Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search beta. Custom Agents are a separate usage item: Notion's pricing page says they are free to try, then cost US$10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits. The credit dashboard is available to owners on Business and Enterprise plans.

Notion announced that new Business and Enterprise workspaces would move to the new credit system on October 19, 2026, and existing workspaces on November 2, 2026. As of November 8, 2026, teams should budget with the current system rather than an older AI add-on description. Check the [Notion credits dashboard guide](https://www.notion.com/help/track-notion-credits-usage) for usage and the [release notes](https://www.notion.com/releases) for the change announcement. Do not assume agent use is unlimited just because a plan includes Notion AI.

## How to estimate a Notion budget

Start with paid members, not the number of pages. For 10 seats, the displayed Plus rate produces a base monthly equivalent of US$100, while Business produces US$200. These examples use the listed per-member prices and exclude taxes, custom domains, extra credits and any regional billing changes. The actual charge depends on the billing selector and contract terms.

Then list the work that may create extra costs. A team may need custom domains for public sites, Custom Agent credits, additional tools connected through a paid service, or a higher plan for private teamspaces and admin controls. Guests are external collaborators, but members are paid seats. Check the member and guest definitions before inviting a contractor with a company email address.

## Which Notion plan should you choose?

- **Choose Free** to learn the editor alone or run a small personal knowledge base.
- **Choose Plus** when a small team needs more collaborative content, charts, custom forms and longer history.
- **Choose Business** when Notion AI, private teamspaces, granular database permissions or SAML SSO are required.
- **Talk to Enterprise sales** when you need SCIM, audit logs, domain management, compliance controls or a custom retention policy.

Do not buy Business only because the word AI appears in the plan description. Write down which AI features the team will use, who can access them and how much monthly credit use is acceptable. ## Common questions

### How much does Notion Plus cost?

The Notion pricing page lists Plus at US$10 per member per month. It has a monthly and yearly payment selector, so check the selected billing mode and checkout total before buying.

### Does Notion Business include AI?

Business includes Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search beta. Custom Agents are priced separately at US$10 per 1,000 monthly Notion credits after the free trial.

### Is the Notion Free plan enough for a team?

It can work for a small trial, but collaborative blocks are limited for workspaces with two or more members. Free also has a 5 MB file upload limit and seven days of page history.

### How long does Notion keep page history?

The pricing page lists seven days on Free, 30 days on Plus, 90 days on Business and a custom period on Enterprise.

### Do unused Notion credits roll over?

Notion uses a monthly credit system for Custom Agents. Check the workspace usage dashboard and current billing terms for the applicable reset and rollover rules before setting an agent budget.
