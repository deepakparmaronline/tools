# Freshdesk vs Help Scout: Pricing, AI and Fit

Canonical: https://toolboxkart.tech/compare/freshdesk-vs-help-scout/

## Quick verdict

Freshdesk and Help Scout both organize customer requests, but they emphasize different support models. Freshdesk is a conventional email-first help desk with ticket fields, groups, customer portal, knowledge base and tiered routing/reporting. Help Scout centers on shared conversations, a shared inbox and Docs, with a free plan for a small team. If your main reason for switching is seat cost, compare the exact plan and usage meter. If the issue is workflow, first decide whether you need a help desk or a simpler shared inbox.

## What the prices actually mean

Freshdesk Growth is listed at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month billed annually. These prices were checked November 11, 2026 on the [official Freshdesk pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/). The table also lists extra Freddy AI Agent sessions at US$49 per 100 and Freddy AI Copilot at US$29 per agent/month on Pro and Enterprise. Freshworks says the first 500 sessions are a one-time allowance for new customers on Growth, Pro or Enterprise. Extra packs cost US$49 per 100 and are valid for the current billing cycle. See the [official AI add-on guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011515), checked November 11, 2026.

Help Scout's official pricing page displayed Standard at US$25, Plus at US$45 and Pro at US$75 per user/month on November 11, 2026, along with a Monthly/Annual selector and an annual savings marker. Verify the selected billing period and checkout amount before comparing it with Freshdesk's annual rates. The Free plan lists up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Sources: [Help Scout pricing](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) and [Freshdesk pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), both checked November 11, 2026.

A simple seat comparison misses AI and usage costs. Freshdesk's page lists day passes, connector app tasks and MCP actions as separate add-ons. Help Scout lists Envoy at US$0.75 per resolution. The meters are different: a session allowance and a resolved conversation are not the same unit. Estimate both from your actual support volume rather than comparing the numbers directly.

## Which workflow fits Freshdesk?

Freshdesk is a stronger candidate when requests need explicit ticket categories, assignment groups, priorities, service targets and structured reporting. Growth includes ticketing, shared inbox, customer portal, knowledge base and standard analytics. Pro adds custom dashboards, intelligent routing, multiple SLA policies and external collaborators. Enterprise adds features such as skill-based routing, sandbox and audit logs. Plan facts were checked November 11, 2026 on the [official pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/).

Freshdesk is also a better fit when your team wants the support request, internal thread, task and customer self-service content tied to the same ticket workflow. It is less compelling if you only need a small team to answer email with a few shared views. Freshworks also separates Freshdesk (email-first) from Freshdesk Omni (multichannel), so verify which product matches your channel requirements.

## Which workflow fits Help Scout?

Help Scout is worth evaluating when the team thinks in conversations and shared inboxes rather than a large ticket-routing system. Its product and pricing pages focus on shared inbox, Docs, workflows and AI assistance. The Free plan can be a useful starting point for a small team, but the 100-contacts-per-month allowance is a real limit; check the current page if contact volume grows. See the [official Help Scout product overview](https://www.helpscout.com/) and [pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 11, 2026.

Help Scout's paid tiers add more inboxes and reporting/workflow capacity. The right plan depends on how many people need access, how many contacts you handle and whether AI assistance or advanced reports are required. Do not assume a lower-complexity product removes the need to define ownership, escalation and data retention.

## AI and self-service are not interchangeable

Freshdesk's AI Agent sessions are metered separately from the agent seat price, and Copilot is a separate add-on on Pro and Enterprise. Help Scout's pricing page lists Envoy by resolution, while AI drafts and other features vary by plan. Ask each vendor for a sample invoice using your monthly volume, the expected number of AI sessions or resolutions and the plan you would buy. A free plan can still have a paid AI meter, and a one-time allowance should not be forecast as a monthly reset.

For self-service, Freshdesk's pricing page lists a customer portal and knowledge base across Growth, Pro and Enterprise. Help Scout's product centers on Docs and a shared inbox. Compare the customer experience you need: a portal that fits a ticket workflow, or a help center closely connected to email conversations.

## Ease of use and administration

Freshdesk's ticket fields, groups and priorities suit teams that want to manage a queue with explicit ownership and service targets. Help Scout organizes work around shared conversations and a common inbox. That model may feel lighter for email-first teams, but it still needs clear rules for assignment, escalation and customer follow-up. Choose based on the workflow your agents will use every day, not on a shorter feature list.

## Integrations and customer context

Both vendors offer integrations, but the relevant question is whether the exact apps your team relies on are supported on the plan you will buy. Freshdesk can be a fit when ticket workflow and customer self-service sit together. Help Scout emphasizes shared conversations, Docs and connected customer context. Review the [Freshdesk product overview](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/) and [Help Scout product page](https://www.helpscout.com/inbox/), checked November 11, 2026, then verify your CRM, ecommerce, identity and reporting integrations before migration.

## Support and rollout risk

Do not assume support response times or onboarding help are identical across plan tiers. Ask each vendor which support channels, migration assistance and service commitments apply to the specific subscription. For Help Scout, the import guide identifies the account roles and paid tiers that can use the import tool; for Freshdesk, verify export scope before moving. A small pilot and written migration plan are more useful than a general promise that a platform is easy to adopt.

## Migration: the detail that can decide the project

Help Scout's [official import guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1495-import-emails-or-tickets-into-help-scout), checked November 11, 2026, lists Freshdesk as a supported source. The import tool is available to Account Owners and Administrators on Standard, Plus and Pro. The guide describes importing emails or tickets with customer profiles and tags. This is a useful path, but it is not proof that every field, trigger, report, attachment and SLA setting transfers without work.

Freshdesk's [ticket export guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158), checked November 11, 2026, describes filtered ticket exports and limitations. Before switching, inventory tickets, comments, attachments, users, organizations, custom fields, macros, rules, knowledge articles and reports. Export a sample, inspect the fields and attachments, compare record counts and record what needs to be rebuilt. Keep the source system available until the new inbox has passed a realistic test.

- Create the target inboxes and user accounts first.
- Map Freshdesk groups, ticket fields, statuses and tags to Help Scout's conversation model.
- Run a sample import and review older, reopened and attachment-heavy cases.
- Confirm customer profiles, tags and conversation history before moving email forwarding.
- Switch forwarding only after a small group has tested replies and assignment.
- Keep an export and rollback plan until the new workflow is stable.

## Decision guide

### Choose Freshdesk when

- You need ticket categories, groups, priorities and SLA controls.
- A customer portal and knowledge base should sit beside the ticket workflow.
- Custom dashboards or intelligent routing justify Pro, or Enterprise controls justify its higher price.
- You will monitor AI and integration usage as part of the total cost.

### Choose Help Scout when

- You want a shared inbox and Docs-first workflow.
- The Free plan's five-user and 100-contact limits fit your actual volume.
- You prefer a direct Freshdesk import path and can validate the fields that transfer.
- Ticket-routing complexity is modest compared with the value of simple shared conversations.

## Freshdesk vs Help Scout FAQ

### Is Freshdesk cheaper than Help Scout?

Freshdesk Growth is listed at US$19 per agent/month billed annually, while Help Scout pricing displayed Standard at US$25 per user/month on November 11, 2026. Confirm billing selection, seats and usage before comparing total cost.

### Does Help Scout have a free plan?

Help Scout lists a free plan for up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Freshdesk public pricing lists a 14-day Enterprise trial but does not show a Free plan; some support docs still reference a Free program, so confirm new-account eligibility.

### Which tool is better for AI support?

Freshworks says new customers on Growth, Pro or Enterprise receive a one-time complimentary 500-session allowance, with extra packs at US$49 per 100 for the current billing cycle. Help Scout lists Envoy at US$0.75 per resolution. These meters are not directly comparable without expected volume.

### Can I import Freshdesk tickets into Help Scout?

Help Scout official import tool lists Freshdesk as a supported source on Standard, Plus and Pro. Test a sample and map fields before changing email forwarding.
