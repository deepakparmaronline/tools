# 6 Vanta Alternatives for Compliance and Trust Management

For Vanta alternatives, compare Drata for a broad trust-management programme, Secureframe for clearly described compliance packages, and Sprinto for ongoing evidence and control workflows. Scrut suits a risk-and-compliance evaluation across entities; Thoropass is relevant when you want software and an auditor-led service together. Hyperproof deserves a look for shared controls and risk programmes managed across teams. There is no universal cheapest choice: most equivalent scopes require quotes, and audit fees can be separate. Keep Vanta if its existing integrations and evidence history work well. Validate the required framework, entities, controls and services before switching.

## Compare the whole programme, not just a dashboard

Vanta alternatives are usually bought to collect evidence, coordinate controls, support audits or answer customer security reviews. Those are connected jobs, but the contracts do not always bundle them in the same way. One proposal might include a single framework and a trust centre; another might add auditor services, questionnaire allowances or multiple entities.

The six options below were selected for overlapping compliance and trust-management work, with different scopes worth evaluating. Official pages were checked on October 14, 2026. This is research-based editorial guidance, not a performed audit, legal opinion or measured product-security test. Recommendations describe where to investigate a fit rather than promise a compliance outcome.

## When Vanta is worth keeping

[Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/) supports compliance, risk and trust workflows. If your present deployment has useful integrations, clear ownership of failed checks and a history your auditor can trace, moving to a visually different dashboard can create more work than it removes.

The [current pricing page](https://www.vanta.com/pricing) requests personalized pricing and presents Essentials, Plus, Professional and Enterprise. Essentials describes one compliance framework, automated evidence collection, continuous monitoring, basic audit workflows, a Trust Center and access to expert partners. Plus includes 25 automated questionnaires per year and access management. Professional increases the listed questionnaire allowance to 144 and adds broader risk, reporting and customization features. Enterprise is tailored.

Those package boundaries offer useful reasons to review your scope before migrating. If the problem is a missing questionnaire allowance or custom monitoring function, compare an appropriate Vanta upgrade with a fully scoped rival quote. Do not assume a rival's entry price includes the module you need.

Keep it when the controls, evidence and review process are working and the next renewal quote remains acceptable. Consider switching when a specific workflow has a poor fit: evidence for your stack needs too much manual handling, entity boundaries are hard to govern, or audit/service coordination is the primary procurement problem. Those are evaluation questions, not generalized claims about defects in Vanta.

The [AICPA describes SOC offerings](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/landing/system-and-organization-controls-soc-suite-of-services) as assurance reporting services CPAs may provide. Buying an automation tool does not itself issue a SOC 2 report. Agree the engagement and evidence requirements with the responsible auditor; do not treat a product's readiness percentage as an audit opinion.

## Drata for a broad trust-management comparison

[Drata's current product overview](https://drata.com/products) combines compliance automation, enterprise GRC and customer assurance. It describes evidence collection and control monitoring across frameworks, internal/third-party risk and a Trust Center through which customers can review posture and request documents. Its positioning is broader than a checklist for one initial audit.

Shortlist Drata when you want to evaluate one programme connecting audit work to customer assurance and risk. A useful proposed scenario is a newly discovered control issue that affects a risk record and an answer in the trust centre. Ask the vendor to show who owns the issue, how evidence is linked and what prevents an outdated external answer from being reused.

The potential advantage over your present Vanta setup is the particular fit of these connected workflows and modules. That is a reason for a representative demonstration, rather than proof that Drata's integrations are deeper or its automation is more accurate for every stack.

The attempted [pricing route](https://drata.com/pricing) redirected to the main site. No current numeric public tariff was verified. Request a quote naming frameworks, entities, integrations, trust-centre/questionnaire scope, users, service assistance and contract terms. Do not substitute marketplace medians or an old entry estimate for your proposal.

The tradeoff is breadth and implementation. A platform covering more of your programme still requires control definitions and accountable owners. Skip it if your sole aim is a guaranteed cheap replacement for a small checklist. First demonstrate the needed module and obtain a migration plan for history, attachments and mappings rather than assume the whole programme imports automatically.

## Secureframe for a package-led evaluation

[Secureframe](https://secureframe.com/) provides compliance automation and security/trust work. Its [package page](https://secureframe.com/pricing) is unusually useful for an initial buying discussion: Fundamentals starts at $7,500 per year and lists infrastructure monitoring, evidence, personnel, policies, risk and a Trust Center. A final quote is still required; the starting figure is not a complete all-framework audit price.

Complete adds advanced third-party risk, risk management, user access reviews, Trust Center and questionnaire functions, plus SSO/SCIM and custom integrations. Additional workspaces are listed as an add-on. The comparison shows one compliance framework in both base package columns and distinguishes one custom automated test in Fundamentals from unlimited in Complete. Defense is a separately scoped offer for requirements including SSP and POA&M work.

Evaluate Secureframe when you want the procurement conversation to identify those package boundaries early. If your Vanta issue is access review or a custom integration, request Complete's scope instead of comparing Fundamentals' starting number with a richer incumbent contract.

The potential benefit is a package structure that may align with the functions your team needs. The tradeoff is that price transparency at the entry point does not remove additional framework, workspace or service questions. Ask what an additional entity means commercially and how evidence is separated between entities.

Skip the entry package if its limits contradict your intended programme. Also avoid treating a Defense-related feature as a general promise that the purchase satisfies a regulatory requirement. Use your required controls and delivery scope to select the package, with responsible technical and audit review.

## Sprinto for continuous controls and approval workflows

[Sprinto's current platform](https://sprinto.com/) presents continuous compliance, vendor risk, risk management, AI governance and trust/questionnaire functions. It describes collecting evidence, detecting control changes, refreshing evidence and routing approvals. The important buying question is how those functions operate in your actual environment and who remains responsible for decisions.

Shortlist Sprinto when the work between audits matters more than collecting a one-time folder of screenshots. For a proposed evaluation, change a representative test resource in a sandbox and trace the issue through its owner, remediation task, evidence refresh and approval. Then check what happens when the required integration loses access.

The advantage to investigate over your existing Vanta configuration is whether that control lifecycle is easier to govern for your team. Marketing descriptions of autonomous operations are not proof that a vendor can make every security decision correctly without human review.

The attempted public pricing path was unavailable, and no numeric current tariff was verified. The [official site](https://sprinto.com/) invites a demo. Obtain a written scope for frameworks, entities, connections, personnel/device coverage, AI usage, approval boundaries and service assistance. Separate audit engagement fees and any security testing from the subscription unless the contract explicitly includes them.

Its breadth also brings a limitation: a well-run programme still needs people to define acceptable evidence and approve changes. Skip it if the buying rationale is an unverified promise of an instant audit or zero internal effort. Ask for a workflow demonstration with failure cases and the actual permissions the product needs.

## Scrut for common controls and multi-entity operations

[Scrut's platform](https://www.scrut.io/platform/scrut-platform) describes a common control framework, collaborative tasks, dashboards, integrations, access control and multi-entity management. Its [current site](https://www.scrut.io/) also describes AI Teammates that draft policies, gather evidence, assess risk and prepare audit material, with people reviewing and approving work.

Evaluate it if your company is trying to reuse controls across programmes while keeping entity ownership understandable. For example, two entities may share an identity provider but have different systems in scope. Ask the vendor to demonstrate that shared evidence can be used where appropriate without making excluded systems look covered.

The potential gain over your current setup is the fit of common-control operations and entity governance. This is not a claim that every supported framework is included at a single flat price. Published framework counts and marketing descriptions do not establish licence entitlement.

The attempted pricing route did not provide a usable current tariff. Request a quote defining entity count, frameworks, integrations, AI functions, risk/vendor modules, services and audit access. Do not use the conflicting annual figures in third-party alternatives lists as proof of Scrut's price or of savings over Vanta.

The tradeoff is mapping and ownership work. Even when a platform suggests that one control maps to several frameworks, an owner needs to confirm that the evidence is appropriate to each scoped requirement. Skip it if the team wants to import a risk register and never revisit its assumptions. Require a demonstrable review process, versioned evidence and clear entity permissions.

## Thoropass for auditor-led delivery with software

[Thoropass](https://www.thoropass.com/) focuses on bringing evidence, progress and auditors together. Its [solutions page](https://www.thoropass.com/solutions) presents automation, audit delivery, integrations, expertise and a risk register linking risks to controls. This is a relevant alternative when coordinating the audit service is a larger problem than choosing another evidence dashboard.

Shortlist it for a discussion covering both the software workflow and the engagement. Ask which party performs the audit, what services precede it, what deliverable you receive and which entities and systems are covered. For an existing programme, ask how the provider will use earlier evidence and what remains outside the new engagement.

The potential advantage is one coordinated operating experience for evidence and auditor requests. The limitation is that you still need the exact contractual scope. The platform offering does not prove that every framework assessment, penetration test or future audit is bundled into an unspecified subscription.

A current public numeric price was not verified; the attempted pricing path was unavailable. Obtain an itemized proposal separating platform access, readiness/support work, the audit or assessment, other security services and renewal terms. Compare that combined proposal with Vanta plus your current auditor, rather than compare two software-only headlines.

Skip it if you need to keep an existing auditor but have not confirmed that arrangement fits the proposed service. An auditor-led model can be attractive, yet the responsible engagement still needs appropriate independence, scope and professional standards. Do not buy it on a guaranteed pass or an unverified audit-speed claim.

## Hyperproof for shared control and risk programmes

[Hyperproof's product page](https://hyperproof.io/product/) describes a compliance module built around common controls across frameworks and a risk module connecting control health to multiple risk registers. It also presents vendor records, documents, renewal dates, dashboards and roles/permissions for work across an organisation.

Shortlist it when compliance owners need to coordinate a wider programme of controls and risk, rather than only prepare a startup's first report. An evaluation could take one control used in two programmes, attach an updated evidence item and ask both owners to explain what changed. Then trace a related risk through its treatment and reporting.

The potential benefit is the fit of common-control operations and shared reporting. The limitation is that automation and coordination are different. Ask which evidence your exact stack supplies automatically, which needs a human upload and how stale material is flagged. A dashboard covering many programmes does not make every collection task disappear.

The attempted [pricing route](https://hyperproof.io/pricing/) redirected to the product page and did not expose a tariff. Request a scope for programmes, users, modules, entity boundaries, connectors, services and contract terms. No annual directory estimate is used here as a current official price.

Skip Hyperproof if the buying requirement is a verified fixed-price starter package with a bundled audit. That scope was not established in this research. It is a more useful candidate when you can demonstrate why common controls, multiple risk registers and accountable collaboration solve a problem in your existing programme.

## A fair quote and migration checklist

Give every vendor the same written scope: legal entities, systems and locations, personnel/devices, required frameworks, existing auditor, expected customer reviews and the services you need. List mandatory modules separately from optional ones. Request a fixed explanation of how growth, additional frameworks and renewal affect the price.

Use a proposed evidence pilot with three cases: a fully automated control from a supported integration, a manually maintained control and a shared control used across programmes. For each, identify the original source, collection time, owner, review, mapping and auditor access. Include a stale item and a failed integration to see how missing coverage is reported.

Before leaving Vanta, inventory policies and acceptance records, evidence and attachments, control/test mappings, risk treatments, vendor assessments, access reviews and questionnaire answers. Obtain a sample export and confirm the destination's import capability for each object. A CSV export of a risk list is not proof that linked evidence, historical decisions and audit context transfer with it.

Agree migration timing with programme owners and the auditor. Preserve evidence for the relevant periods and avoid disabling the current collectors before the new collection is reconciled. The appropriate retention and engagement requirements depend on your organisation; resolve them with the responsible professionals rather than infer them from a software comparison.

Compare the full buying cost: platform, extra frameworks/entities, questionnaires, AI allowances, audit/assessment, implementation, security services, overlap and internal staff work. A lower software price can still accompany a more expensive overall programme.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Vanta alternative is cheapest?

This research cannot establish a cheapest equivalent scope. Secureframe publishes Fundamentals from $7,500 annually, but other vendors and richer scopes require proposals. Compare the same entities, frameworks, modules and services before declaring savings.

### Does a compliance tool give us a SOC 2 certification?

SOC offerings involve assurance reporting by CPAs, as described by the AICPA. An automation subscription supports the work; it does not itself issue the report or guarantee the auditor's opinion. Ask the responsible auditor about the actual engagement.

### Is an audit included with every alternative?

Do not assume so. Thoropass explicitly presents an auditor-led service alongside software, but the exact engagement still needs a proposal. For every vendor, distinguish subscription access, readiness assistance and audit/assessment fees.

### Can we keep our existing auditor?

Confirm this with the platform and auditor before purchase. Ask about auditor access, evidence format, exports and the engagement's scope. A familiar vendor logo or an auditor network does not establish compatibility with every arrangement.

### Will all evidence collection be automatic?

No such universal capability was established. Automation depends on the integration, permissions, scoped resource and control. Pilot both automated and manual evidence, including stale data and a disconnected source.

### Can AI answer customer questionnaires without review?

Use draft answers as inputs to a controlled review. Verify that the answer matches the current system, entity and evidence, and that an authorized owner approves externally shared statements. Questionnaire allowances and approval functions can also depend on the package.

## Choose around the control work you need to improve

Start with two alternatives matched to the problem: Drata for broad trust operations, Secureframe for package-led procurement, Sprinto for a continuous-control evaluation, Scrut for shared entity/control work, Thoropass for service coordination or Hyperproof for cross-programme risk operations. Keep Vanta if its programme is working. Require a scoped quote, a representative evidence demonstration and an agreed migration plan before making a change.
