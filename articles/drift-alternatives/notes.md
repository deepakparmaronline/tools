# Drift alternatives editorial notes

## Selection rationale
This page treats Drift as a sunset product, not a new purchase recommendation. The seven options cover distinct jobs: named successor (1mind), Salesforce-centric inbound sales (Qualified), existing CRM chat (HubSpot), routing and scheduling (Chili Piper), support-led messaging (Intercom), smaller-team chat and automation (Tidio), and a shared inbox (Crisp). The order reflects workflow fit and the official successor relationship, not paid placement.

## Limitations and editorial boundaries
- Drift is scheduled for retirement on 2027-01-31. Access can end earlier at contract end. Billing for contracts extending beyond that date is handled case by case, according to the official FAQ.
- No current public Drift price is shown by Salesloft. Historic third-party price figures were not presented as current prices.
- The 1mind, Qualified and other vendors' quotes may depend on traffic, seats, AI usage, CRM and contract terms. Qualified plan capacity was not independently confirmed from the accessible public pricing text, so the article asks readers to verify it in writing.
- Product facts, prices and usage limits are volatile. Every stated price or limit is linked to the official page checked on 2026-10-30.
