# 6 Gainsight Alternatives for Customer Success Teams

For Gainsight alternatives, shortlist ChurnZero for repeatable customer journeys, Vitally for customer data alongside collaborative work, and Planhat for a broader customer operations platform. Custify suits teams wanting guided setup around health scores and playbooks; ClientSuccess offers packages with explicit account and licence limits. Odie, the current identity of Totango, deserves a separate look if you want customer intelligence and an agent-led workspace. This comparison covers Gainsight Customer Success, rather than Gainsight PX or community software. Choose around your account model, renewal process and data ownership, then request equivalent quotes before switching.

## Choose the customer workflow before the vendor

A customer success platform has to connect signals to an accountable action. A dashboard showing declining usage is useful only if the right owner can decide what happens next, record the decision, and see whether the customer reached the intended outcome.

These six options represent different ways of doing that. Their order is a reading guide, rather than a ranking based on performance tests. Pricing and product pages were checked on October 14, 2026. Most vendors require a scoped quote, so this article does not invent annual contract totals or promise that a replacement will be cheaper.

## When keeping Gainsight is the better choice

[Gainsight's pricing page](https://www.gainsight.com/pricing/) lists Customer 360, playbooks and success plans, health scorecards, surveys, digital journeys and renewal/expansion forecasting for Customer Success. Essentials includes 10 full users and 100 customers per user; Enterprise lists 20 and 200 respectively. Both list unlimited viewer licences. Pricing requires a request, and the page says its comparison is not inclusive of all features.

That is a reason to compare your actual contract with a replacement quote, rather than compare a rumoured Gainsight price with a rival's starting package. A working implementation may already contain years of useful customer history, approved reporting definitions and routing rules. Keeping it can be sensible when those processes are used consistently and the operations team can maintain them.

Also distinguish the broader suite. [Gainsight Customer Success](https://www.gainsight.com/customer-success/) is the scope here. PX provides a different product experience workflow, while communities and education are separate offerings. Replacing CS with a rival does not automatically replace in-app guidance, a customer community or a training academy purchased elsewhere in the suite.

Consider switching when a specific requirement is hard to deliver in your present setup: a customer-facing plan that people actually update, simpler ownership of onboarding work, an account structure that reflects multiple products, or a commercial model better matched to your coverage. These are evaluation triggers, rather than claims that Gainsight universally fails at them.

## ChurnZero for repeatable lifecycle journeys

[ChurnZero's customer journeys](https://churnzero.com/features/customer-journeys/) support stages, progress tracking, conditional logic and entry based on segment criteria. The page describes automatic CSM notifications when accounts enter a journey and customer-facing milestone sharing. It also describes using journey status to run emails, in-app announcements and surveys.

This makes ChurnZero a relevant candidate when your main problem is turning an onboarding or adoption policy into repeatable work. For example, an account could enter an onboarding journey after a defined event, receive a sequence of tasks, and require a human review if the planned milestone is overdue. That is an evaluation scenario, not a claim about an outcome achieved in testing.

The advantage to investigate over your Gainsight deployment is whether this journey model is easier for the people who own the process to inspect and change. Ask a CSM to explain an account's current stage without help from an administrator. Then change an entry criterion and trace the effect on existing accounts.

The [official site](https://churnzero.com/) directs buyers to a demo. A public numeric price was not verified, and the attempted pricing route did not provide a usable tariff. Request terms covering accounts, licences, event ingestion, messaging, implementation and any AI entitlement. Do not assume every feature on a marketing page is in the base quote.

Skip this option if your only need is a small contact list and renewal reminders. Before selecting it for event-driven work, prove that your data sources can provide stable account identifiers and the signals the journey actually needs. A new journey builder will not repair inconsistent product instrumentation.

## Vitally for customer data alongside collaborative work

[Vitally's product overview](https://www.vitally.io/why-vitally) connects customer profiles with Hubs, views, projects, tasks, goals and Docs. Projects can be embedded into Docs for customer collaboration, while playbooks automate account and work changes. Health scoring can incorporate usage, surveys and support activity.

Shortlist it when your CSMs move repeatedly between a customer database and a separate document or project tool. The potential gain is a shared place for an account plan and the work needed to deliver it. A useful pilot would put the onboarding milestones, customer responsibilities and current account context into one customer-facing document, then ask both sides to update it.

[Vitally pricing](https://www.vitally.io/pricing) presents Tech-Touch, Hybrid-Touch and High-Touch plans, each requiring pricing requests. The page says all plans include core features, unlimited automations and observer seats, single sign-on, the integration library and unlimited Docs. Those inclusions do not establish a price for active users, a customer capacity limit or every possible contract condition.

The tradeoff is the work involved in translating your present account process into the new workspace. A document can be pleasant to read while still failing a permission requirement. Validate what an external customer sees, which fields they can update, and whether that update writes back to the system your sales or support team treats as authoritative.

Skip Vitally if you are seeking a guaranteed free plan or a verified fixed public price. Also avoid assuming that an observer licence permits the same actions as a working CSM. Compare roles with your own workflow, rather than counting all licences as interchangeable seats.

## Planhat for broader customer operations

[Planhat's customer success offering](https://www.planhat.com/applications/csp?swcfpc=1) describes bringing together CRM, database, support, email and meeting information. Its positioning now extends across a broader commercial platform. That matters when evaluating it: a new homepage emphasis on AI does not mean the CS application has disappeared, but old descriptions of a narrow CS-only product may be incomplete.

Planhat is worth a scoped demonstration when account management, customer success and service delivery need to act on related information. The practical advantage to look for is less duplication between teams. For example, a renewal owner should be able to understand whether an implementation project is late without someone copying an internal spreadsheet into a renewal note.

The [current pricing page](https://www.planhat.com/pricing) uses enquiries rather than a numeric self-service tariff. It separates CRM, CSP and PSA application contexts and lists add-ons such as upgraded AI, advanced service, email marketing and advanced portals. Managed and professional services are also presented separately. Ask for a CSP quote that names the applications, user roles, data scope, add-ons and implementation work you need.

This breadth creates a procurement question as well as an opportunity. A vendor demonstration across the whole platform can conceal which parts your contract includes. Request a written entitlement map and make the demo use that scope.

Skip Planhat if the attraction is simply a promise of an instant, inexpensive Gainsight migration. No such result was established in this research. It is a more useful candidate when you can name the cross-team workflows worth rebuilding and provide people who own those definitions.

## Custify for health scores and guided setup

[Custify](https://www.custify.com/) presents customer success work around a unified account view, segmentation, health scores, lifecycle management, tasks and playbooks. Its [pricing and onboarding page](https://www.custify.com/pricing) describes a guided process that defines the success use case, assists the development team, verifies implementation and trains users. It says customers are not charged setup fees.

Choose it for evaluation when a smaller CS operation wants help establishing a usable process, rather than buying a large collection of dashboards and deciding the operating model afterward. A proposed pilot might start with one product's adoption signal and one renewal risk rule, then verify that an alert creates useful work for the correct owner.

The commercial page does not publish a numeric tariff. Obtain a custom quote for your account volume, working users and integrations. The no-setup-fee statement does not mean your own developers or operations team spend no time on implementation. Request the actual division of work, including how historical usage is loaded and how a broken integration is detected.

Its potential advantage over your present Gainsight setup is the fit of the guided implementation and the amount of process you need to manage. That is a reason to test, not evidence that every company implements it faster. Avoid unsupported claims that Custify cannot handle a particular account hierarchy; have the vendor demonstrate yours.

Skip it if you expect onboarding assistance to define your product's value metrics for you. Your team still needs to decide which activity represents adoption, which support signals matter, and what intervention is appropriate. A neat health score has little value when its ingredients do not match customer outcomes.

## ClientSuccess for explicit package boundaries

[ClientSuccess](https://www.clientsuccess.com/) combines account visibility, customer journeys, health information and customer onboarding/portals. Its published integration inventory includes CRM, support and communication systems such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk and Microsoft Dynamics. Validate each required connector's objects and direction of sync, rather than treating a logo as proof of complete compatibility.

The [pricing page](https://www.clientsuccess.com/pricing) provides useful package boundaries despite withholding prices. Startup lists up to 500 customers and 10 full licences. Growth lists 5,000 customers and 50 full licences; Enterprise lists unlimited for those rows. Customer portals and custom reports have package dependencies or add-on treatment. Its FAQ says quotes depend on CSM count, modules, contract length and required integrations, and it does not offer a self-service free trial.

Shortlist ClientSuccess if you want the buying discussion to begin with those account and user boundaries. Ask whether a parent company and its subsidiaries count separately and what happens when an account is archived or reactivated. This prevents a quote sized to sales logos from being mistaken for one sized to operational customer records.

The potential improvement over an existing deployment is a package and workflow that match a clearly defined CS team. The limitation is that modules still affect the final scope. The page's comparative savings claims were not independently verified and are not used as proof of a lower price here.

Skip it if you need unrestricted use of every portal or reporting feature at a presumed entry price. Use the guided proof-of-concept process to validate the full renewal and onboarding workflow, including customer access, before treating the quote as equivalent to your Gainsight contract.

## Odie for an intelligence-led alternative to Totango's old model

[Totango's official website](https://www.totango.com/) now announces that Totango is Odie and directs visitors to the new platform. A comparison built around old Totango plans or SuccessBLOC descriptions would therefore need current confirmation. [Odie](https://www.heyodie.ai/) describes a Customer Knowledge Engine that combines customer data with team knowledge, plus an optional Customer Operating System for guided post-sales work.

The distinction is essential. Buying the intelligence engine alone is not the same purchase as replacing a CSM's daily workspace. Shortlist Odie when you want to evaluate contextual intelligence for internal agents, or an agent-led alternative to a conventional CS operating model. Ask it to explain the evidence behind an account recommendation and show what a human can correct.

[Odie pricing](https://www.heyodie.ai/pricing) lists the Knowledge Engine from $500 per month and the Customer Operating System from $1,500 per month. Terms start at one year; these are not month-to-month subscription offers. The engine is sized around knowledge maintenance and access, while the operating system depends on portfolio size, analysis depth/frequency and an AI Success allocation pool. Request a complete quote explaining how the engine and application are charged together.

The tradeoff is a different operating model, with usage and scope questions that a seat-price comparison misses. Establish which work consumes the allocation, what happens when it runs out, and which underlying enterprise AI bills remain yours. Do not assume a historic Totango configuration migrates unchanged.

Skip it if your requirement is specifically the old Totango product or a verified like-for-like migration of its modules. Confirm availability and migration terms directly. The current pricing page also contained an unresolved support-threshold placeholder, so no guaranteed dedicated-support threshold is claimed here.

## A migration pilot that exposes hidden differences

Before a full export, write down who owns account identity, renewal value and product usage. Then use three deliberately different accounts in a proposed evaluation:

1. A parent company with two subsidiaries and separate product renewals.
2. A recently onboarded account whose usage data arrives late.
3. An established customer with strong usage but an unresolved support issue.

For each, ask a CSM to find the next action, explain the health score, identify the renewal owner and update a shared plan. Ask an administrator to change the rule and show the resulting audit/history. The purpose is to reveal assumptions about hierarchy, freshness, permissions and workflow, rather than reward a polished demo.

Create an export inventory before cancelling Gainsight: accounts, contacts, notes, tasks, success plans, score definitions, historical scores and reports. Do not assume a CSV can preserve relationships, attachments or automation rules. Agree with each vendor which objects are importable and which must be rebuilt; obtain a sample export and import reconciliation.

Estimate the full first-year cost from comparable written scopes. Include implementation, overlap subscriptions, data work, user training, messaging, add-ons and the staff responsible for integration maintenance. A lower licence quote is not automatically a lower switching cost.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Gainsight alternative should a small CS team consider first?

Custify and ClientSuccess are reasonable initial discussions for a team wanting a defined CS workflow and guided setup. The right choice depends on account structure and required modules. Neither is labelled cheapest here because public equivalent quotes were not available.

### Does this comparison replace Gainsight PX?

No. It covers Gainsight Customer Success. If you use PX analytics, in-app guides or product surveys, inventory those separately and confirm how they would be provided after a CS switch.

### Is Totango still the same alternative listed in older articles?

Its official site now points to Odie. Evaluate the current Knowledge Engine and optional Customer Operating System rather than rely on older Totango packaging. Ask the vendor about any legacy service you specifically need.

### Are there verified free Gainsight replacements in this shortlist?

No permanent free plan was established for a comparable CS setup in these six options. A product tour, demo, sandbox or proof of concept is not a free production plan. Request evaluation access and its limitations in writing.

### Can health scores transfer unchanged?

Do not assume so. A health score depends on source fields, update timing, weights and account relationships. Rebuild a small sample, compare the explanations and preserve historical exports before switching the live process.

### How should a team compare prices when most are custom?

Give every vendor the same customer count, hierarchy, active/viewer roles, integration list, communication volume and required modules. Separate annual commitments from monthly billing and include overlap and implementation effort. Compare the resulting contracts, rather than headline estimates from directories.

## Make the next decision small and concrete

Start with two candidates that address your actual operating problem. ChurnZero fits a journey-led evaluation; Vitally fits collaborative work around customer data; Planhat fits broader customer operations. Custify and ClientSuccess deserve scoped discussions for a defined CS programme, while Odie requires deliberate evaluation of its new intelligence-led model. Keep Gainsight if its present setup already supports the work well. Require an account-level demonstration, a reconciled import and a complete quote before authorizing a migration.
