# Help Scout review

Canonical: https://toolboxkart.tech/helpscout/review/

**Verdict:** Help Scout is a good fit when a support team wants a shared inbox, a connected knowledge base and clear customer context without building a complex ticket system. Its strongest value is the link between Inbox, Docs and Beacon. The main cautions are the Free plan's 100-contact limit, per-user paid pricing, separate AI resolution costs and the work needed to move old help-desk settings.

## What is Help Scout?

Help Scout is customer support software for teams that answer customer questions through shared conversations. The Inbox gives agents a place to assign messages, add private notes, use saved replies and see customer context. Docs provides a hosted knowledge base, while Beacon connects those answers to the place on a website where customers need help. Workflows and reporting help managers organize the queue.

This is a conversation-first product. It can suit a team that mainly works through email and wants the support history to feel personal. It is not a simple mailbox with a team password. It has rules, service targets, reporting, multiple inboxes and AI features. The important question is whether that model fits the work your agents do every day.

## Where Help Scout is strong

### 1. Shared conversations with customer context

The Inbox gives a team a shared view of customer messages. Agents can assign ownership, add an internal note, use a saved reply and review past conversations. This reduces the chance that two people answer the same email or that a customer has to repeat the same detail. A small team can start with one inbox and add another when a real ownership boundary appears.

Views and tags can make a busy inbox easier to manage. A team can keep a short list for unassigned conversations, waiting replies or high-priority issues. The trade-off is that a team still needs naming rules. Too many tags or inboxes make it harder to see which queue needs attention.

### 2. Docs and Beacon connect support to self-service

Docs is the knowledge base side of Help Scout. It gives customers a place to find answers and gives agents a common link to share. Beacon can surface Docs and offer a contact route on a website. This can reduce repeat questions when the articles are current and easy to find. It also gives the support team a clear place to send a customer before opening a new conversation.

Self-service needs maintenance. An old article can create more work than no article because the customer follows the wrong steps. Give every important article an owner, a review date and a clear route to contact support when the answer does not solve the problem. The same rule applies to saved replies.

### 3. Workflows and service targets

Help Scout has workflow rules that can apply actions when a conversation matches conditions. This can help with tagging, assignment, status changes and notifications. Its public plan table lists different workflow limits by tier. Standard has basic workflow capacity and one basic SLA policy; Plus adds advanced workflows and two advanced SLA policies; Pro lists higher limits. Check the exact allowance before you design a large set of rules.

SLAs are useful when the team has a clear promise to customers. They are less useful when working hours, ownership and escalation are not defined. Start with one service target that the team can meet. Review missed targets and adjust the process before adding several policies.

### 4. Channels and customer portal

Help Scout product pages describe support across email and website messaging, with SMS, social channels and other connections available through plan or integration choices. The Fall 2026 release notes describe changes to SMS, Topics, Company Profiles and the Customer Portal. Do not assume every channel is included in every plan. Check the exact channel and any provider charge before you add it to the support promise.

### 5. AI assistance with a separate usage meter

The pricing page lists an AI Inbox assistant and AI drafting features on paid plans. It also lists Envoy at US$0.75 per resolution. That charge is separate from user seats. Help Scout setup documentation uses the Scout name for the agent configuration flow. Check the current product label and the definition of a resolution in your account. A team should measure how many conversations the agent resolves before making a monthly budget.

AI quality depends on the source content and handoff rules. Keep Docs current, limit the source material to approved content and send account-specific decisions to a person. A support agent should not make a promise about a refund, account change or security issue unless the workflow and source data support it.

## Where Help Scout needs care

- **The Free plan has a real contact cap.** Five users and one inbox can look generous, but 100 contacts per month is low for a busy public support address.
- **Paid plans are per user.** A growing team should include agents, managers and extra seats in the estimate.
- **Inbox and Docs limits vary by plan.** A team with separate brands or departments may need more inboxes or Docs sites.
- **AI is not one flat fee.** Envoy is priced by resolution. Do not treat a resolution charge as part of the seat price.
- **Migration is selective.** The official import guide does not promise to recreate every user setting, inbox, knowledge article, saved reply, macro, rule, automation, custom field or custom status.
- **Complex ticketing needs should be checked.** Teams that rely on large ticket taxonomies, custom fields and advanced routing should compare a ticket-first product before moving.

## Who should use Help Scout?

Help Scout is worth shortlisting for a small or mid-sized support team that answers a high share of questions by email and wants a shared history. It can work for software companies, service businesses and teams that publish a help centre. It is also worth considering when the team values personal replies and does not need a large set of ticket states or departments.

## Who should compare alternatives?

Compare Front if the main need is collaborative inbox work across several channels. Compare Freshdesk when ticket fields, a customer portal and structured reporting matter. Compare Zendesk when routing and service operations are more complex. Intercom suits chat-first software support and AI outcomes. Zoho Desk is worth checking for a small team already using Zoho. Gorgias fits ecommerce support, and Missive fits team email rather than a full help desk. The [Help Scout alternatives guide](/helpscout/alternatives/) explains these differences.

## Migration and data portability

Help Scout has an official import tool for several help desks, including Freshdesk and Zendesk. The guide says it can bring selected emails or tickets with customer profiles and tags. It is available to Account Owners and Administrators on Standard, Plus and Pro. The guide does not describe a full import of users, inbox settings, Docs articles, saved replies, macros, rules, automations, custom fields or custom statuses.

That does not make migration impossible, but it makes a checklist important. List the objects you need to keep, ask the destination vendor which ones it imports, then export and inspect a sample. Compare record counts, timestamps, attachments and customer profiles. Keep the source system available until the new workflow has been checked by the people who use it.

## Common questions

### Is Help Scout worth using?

Help Scout is worth considering when a team wants a shared inbox, Docs and customer context in one support workspace. It is less suitable when the main need is a large ticket taxonomy, advanced routing or a self-hosted knowledge base.

### Does Help Scout have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan lists up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Check the article and saved-reply limits before using it for a busy public support address.

### How much does Help Scout cost?

The pricing page displayed Standard at US$25, Plus at US$45 and Pro at US$75 per user/month on November 11, 2026. It has a monthly and annual selector, so confirm the billing mode and seat minimum before purchase.

### Does Help Scout include an AI agent?

The pricing page lists Envoy at US$0.75 per resolution. AI Inbox assistance and drafting features vary by plan. Confirm the current product label and resolution definition before estimating usage.

### Can I migrate from another help desk?

Help Scout lists supported import sources, including Freshdesk and Zendesk. The import does not recreate every user setting, knowledge article, saved reply, automation or custom field, so map those items separately.
