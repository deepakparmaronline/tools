## Alternatives
- [Zapier product](https://zapier.com/) and [pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing/) — app connections, tasks and public plans.
- [Zapier task usage help](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496181445389-How-is-task-usage-measured-in-Zapier) — what counts as a task.
- [Make product](https://www.make.com/) and [pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing) — visual scenarios, credits and plan selector.
- [Make credits help](https://help.make.com/credits) — credit counting and exceptions.
- [Activepieces product](https://www.activepieces.com/), [pricing](https://www.activepieces.com/pricing/) and [installation docs](https://www.activepieces.com/docs/install/overview) — flows, credits, Cloud/on-prem hosting.
- [Activepieces repository licence](https://github.com/activepieces/activepieces/blob/main/LICENSE) — MIT-licensed core and separately licensed enterprise directories.
- [Power Automate product](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-automate), [pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-automate/pricing) and [limits](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/limits-and-config) — cloud flows, desktop flows and US plan price.
- [Power Automate solution export](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/export-flow-solution) — migration and ALM considerations.
- [Node-RED about](https://nodered.org/about/), [user guide](https://nodered.org/docs/user-guide/) and [Apache 2.0 licence](https://github.com/node-red/node-red/blob/master/LICENSE) — runtime, editor and licence.
- [Windmill product](https://www.windmill.dev/), [pricing](https://www.windmill.dev/pricing/) and [docs](https://www.windmill.dev/docs) — self-hosted scripts, flows, seats and compute.
- [Windmill licence](https://github.com/windmill-labs/windmill/blob/main/LICENSE) — AGPL/Apache components and Community Edition terms.
- [Kestra product](https://kestra.io/), [pricing](https://kestra.io/pricing.html) and [quickstart](https://kestra.io/docs/quickstart) — orchestration model, plugins and managed/self-hosted options.
- [Pipedream Workflows sunset notice](https://pipedream.com/docs/workflows) — March 31, 2027 shutdown of Workflows and String; Connect is not affected.
