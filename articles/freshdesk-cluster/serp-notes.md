## Result types and search intent

Public searches for "Freshdesk alternatives", "Freshdesk vs Zendesk", and related customer-support software queries returned list-style alternatives guides, direct product comparisons and vendor pricing pages. The dominant task is vendor selection, not a definition of help desk software. Pages compete by presenting a short verdict, a quick comparison table, per-product use cases, plan costs and migration considerations.

The article covers:
- Direct help desk competitors for teams that need ticketing and routing.
- Shared inbox products for teams that want lighter collaboration.
- CRM-first support for companies that already run their customer records in a CRM.
- Ecommerce support where order context and store integrations matter.
- AI-led customer service where usage charges can sit beside seat fees.
- Free and low-seat options for very small teams.
- Data export and migration checks before cancelling a subscription.

## Editorial choices

1. Put the answer and top three use cases first.
2. Use a real HTML comparison table with pricing model and free access.
3. Explain when Freshdesk is still the better choice, instead of assuming every searcher should switch.
4. Explain the difference between standalone Freshdesk and Freshdesk Omni.
5. Use current official product and pricing pages for product claims. Prices are time-sensitive and carry a checked date and source link.
6. Do not quote competitor article copy, ratings, customer counts or unverified performance claims.
7. FAQs answer practical selection questions and are not labelled as exact People Also Ask questions because no exact PAA list was committed for this keyword.
