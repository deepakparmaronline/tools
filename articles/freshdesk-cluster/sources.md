# Freshdesk cluster source ledger

Checked on 2026-10-28. Prices below are US public-page values where the vendor page exposed them. They may change after this date.

## Freshdesk

1. Product overview: https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/
   - Describes Freshdesk as an AI-supported customer service product.
   - The page separates Freshdesk from Freshdesk Omni.
2. Standalone Freshdesk pricing: https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/
   - Growth: US$19 per agent/month billed annually.
   - Pro: US$55 per agent/month billed annually.
   - Enterprise: US$89 per agent/month billed annually.
   - First 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions included; additional sessions US$49 per 100.
   - Freddy AI Copilot: US$29 per agent/month on Pro and Enterprise.
   - Day passes: US$2 on Growth, US$7 on Pro and US$12 on Enterprise.
   - Connector app tasks: US$80 per 5,000 tasks. MCP actions: US$15 per 1,000 actions.
3. Product features: https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/helpdesk-ticketing/
   - Lists ticketing, shared inbox, customer portal, knowledge base, threads and tasks, reporting, and plan-dependent AI and routing features.
4. Freshdesk support email setup: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/37541-setting-up-your-support-email
   - Updated 2026-07-03. Requires admin access and mailbox access; incoming email to a configured support address becomes a ticket.
5. Email channel overview: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000009268-overview-of-email-channel
   - Updated 2026-01-21. Documents default support email, multiple support addresses, and plan-dependent email setup.
6. Ticket-update automation: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/99047-setting-up-automation-rules-to-run-on-ticket-updates
   - Updated 2026-05-11. Documents event/condition/action rules and the Admin > Workflows > Automation Rules > Ticket Updates location.
7. Advanced email settings: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000009270
   - Updated 2026-08-21. Documents Admin > Channels > Email > Advanced Settings.
8. Subscription and Free Program rules: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011778
   - Updated 2026-01-28. New accounts start with a 14-day trial. The standalone Freshdesk Free Program lasts six months and allows two agents; it does not apply to Freshdesk Omni. Downgrading can remove features and configurations.
9. Free Program detail: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-program-freshdesk-only-
   - Updated 2025-12-18. Confirms the two-agent cap, six-month period and activation through Plans and Billing.
10. Ticket export: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158
    - Used to describe filtered ticket-list export to CSV/Excel and the limits of that export compared with a full account export.
11. Account data export: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225487-how-do-i-export-my-helpdesk-data-
    - Used as the account-level export reference. Verify current account settings before relying on it.
12. Freshdesk product updates: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011682-freshdesk-upcoming-changes-december-2025-march-2026-
    - Reviewed for product changes. No time-sensitive release claim is made in the article.

## Alternatives and comparison products

1. Zendesk pricing: https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/
   - Checked 2026-10-28. Support Team US$19/agent/month and Suite Team US$55/agent/month billed yearly; Suite Professional US$115/agent/month billed yearly.
2. Zendesk service overview: https://www.zendesk.com/service/
   - Describes AI-supported customer service, agent workflows, knowledge and multiple channels.
3. Zoho Desk pricing: https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html
   - The page shows a Free edition for three users and region-specific paid pricing. Exact US paid rates were not captured in the public page text, so the article does not invent them.
4. Zoho Desk features: https://www.zoho.com/desk/features.html
   - Documents AI assistance, omnichannel support, agent productivity, self-service, automation, customization, analytics, integrations and mobile app.
5. Help Scout pricing: https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/
   - Checked 2026-10-28. Free plan includes up to five users, one inbox and one Docs site. Standard US$25/user/month, Plus US$45 and Pro US$75 on the pricing page's annual view.
6. Intercom pricing: https://www.intercom.com/pricing
   - Checked 2026-10-28. The page showed a new-customer Essential offer at US$19/seat/month (regular US$29) and US$0.99 per Fin outcome. The offer and usage rates can change.
7. HubSpot Service Hub catalog: https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog/products
   - Checked 2026-10-28. Starter US$20/core seat/month, Professional US$100/service seat/month and Enterprise US$150/service seat/month (annual billing stated for Enterprise). HubSpot also offers select functionality in its free tools.
8. HubSpot Service Hub product page: https://www.hubspot.com/products/service
   - Describes CRM-connected customer service tools.
9. Front pricing: https://front.com/pricing/
   - Checked 2026-10-28. Starter US$25/seat/month, up to 10 seats; Professional US$65/seat/month, up to 50 seats; Enterprise US$105/seat/month. The page shows annual billing and 24% savings.
10. Gorgias pricing: https://www.gorgias.com/pricing
    - Checked 2026-10-28. The page states that the help desk is priced by monthly ticket volume rather than per agent and describes a 50 to 5,000 ticket range. Exact prices were not captured in the rendered page text, so the article links to the official page without inventing a starting price.
11. LiveAgent pricing: https://www.liveagent.com/pricing/
    - Checked 2026-10-28. The page showed promotional Starter pricing of US$15/agent/month monthly or US$11/agent/month annually, for up to five agents; regular prices and promotions can change.
