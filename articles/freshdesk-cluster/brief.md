## Product boundaries

This cluster covers standalone Freshdesk, the email-first help desk. Freshdesk Omni is a separate product with different channel coverage and pricing. Do not mix its plan features or prices into standalone Freshdesk claims.

## Page-specific angle

- Hub: map Freshdesk's ticketing, customer portal, knowledge base, routing and AI model to team needs.
- Alternatives: compare eight alternatives by use case, pricing model and trade-offs.
- Review: explain strengths and constraints without star ratings or hands-on claims.
- Pricing: separate seat prices from AI sessions, Copilot and other usage-based charges.
- How-to: use official support instructions for email, automations, knowledge content, reporting and exports.
- Comparison: compare equivalent plans and the cost and data implications of switching between Freshdesk and Zendesk.
