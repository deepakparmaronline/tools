# Help Scout vs Zendesk: Features and Pricing

Help Scout is a better fit when a team wants a shared inbox, a connected knowledge base and a straightforward way to manage customer conversations. Zendesk is a stronger option when support needs structured ticket routing, broader service channels and more formal administration. Compare the right plans: Zendesk Support Team and Suite Team do not include the same features, just as Help Scout Standard and Plus differ.

The entry prices differ by billing period. Help Scout Standard is US$30 per user/month month-to-month or US$25 with annual billing; Zendesk Support Team is US$25 per agent/month month-to-month or US$19 with annual billing. The plans include different features, so compare the full workflow as well as the seat rate.

## The main difference is the support model

Help Scout starts with a shared Inbox. Teammates can assign a conversation, add internal notes and use saved replies. Docs gives the team a searchable help center, while Beacon can bring help content and contact options into a website or app. This model works when most requests arrive through email or a small set of customer channels.

Zendesk starts with ticketing and service operations. Support Team includes email ticketing, routing, customer context, analytics dashboards, prewritten responses, automations and triggers. Suite plans add a knowledge base, AI Agents, action building, omnichannel routing, messaging, live chat and telephony.

Neither model is automatically better. A team that wants a shared workspace may prefer Help Scout. A team that needs queue rules, multiple service channels and formal ticket operations should compare Zendesk Suite.

## Pricing: compare the same billing period

Help Scout's [official pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists Standard at US$25 per user/month with annual billing or US$30 month-to-month, Plus at US$45 annual-billed or US$54 monthly, and Pro at US$75 annual-billed or US$90 monthly. Free includes up to five users, one Inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Envoy is a separate AI add-on at US$0.75 per resolution. Extra Inboxes and Docs sites can also add cost.

Zendesk's [official pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/?so=1), checked November 22, 2026, lists Support Team at US$19 per agent/month billed yearly or US$25 monthly. Suite Team is US$55 yearly or US$69 monthly. Suite Professional is US$115 yearly or US$149 monthly. The page does not list a permanent free support plan. Suite Team adds AI Agents, a knowledge base, action builder, omnichannel routing, messaging, live chat and telephony.

These examples use monthly prices checked November 22, 2026. They are simple seat calculations, not quotes. Taxes, extra Inboxes, Docs sites, AI usage, channel charges, onboarding and contract terms can change the final bill. Help Scout Pro has a ten-user minimum, so its five-seat figure is only a rate comparison, not an eligible five-user order.

The entry price alone does not settle the decision. With annual billing, Help Scout Standard is US$25 per user/month and Zendesk Support Team is US$19 per agent/month. Month-to-month rates are US$30 and US$25 respectively, and the feature lists differ. If you need Zendesk's knowledge base, messaging and telephony, price Suite Team. If you need Help Scout's advanced workflows, secure portal and round-robin routing, compare Plus.

## Shared inbox and ticket handling

Help Scout's Inbox is designed for collaboration around a conversation. Teammates can assign a request, add private notes and use saved replies. The [pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists multiple Inboxes, customer channels and workflows on paid plans. It separates the number of Inboxes included from the maximum that can be added. Standard includes two, Plus five and Pro ten. Extra Inboxes have a separate monthly price.

Zendesk Support Team is more explicitly ticket-focused. The [Zendesk plan comparison](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/?so=1), checked November 22, 2026, lists email ticketing, routing, prebuilt analytics, customer context and automations. Suite adds messaging, live chat and telephony. A small team that works mainly from a shared email address may prefer Help Scout's conversation view. A support operation with several queues and more detailed routing should test Zendesk with real examples.

## Knowledge base and self-service

Help Scout Docs connects a searchable knowledge base with the Inbox and Beacon. The [pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists one site on Free, two on Standard, three on Plus and five on Pro. Extra sites have a separate charge. Free lists ten articles, while paid plans list unlimited articles.

Zendesk includes a Knowledge Base on Suite Team and higher Suite plans. Support Team does not list that feature in the current [pricing comparison](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/?so=1), checked November 22, 2026. If help content is central to the customer experience, list the number of sites, languages, brands, permissions and article owners you need, then price the plan that includes them.

## Automation, routing and AI

Help Scout lists basic workflows on Standard, advanced workflows on Plus and unlimited workflows on Pro. Plus adds two advanced SLA policies and round-robin routing. Pro adds unlimited SLA policies and multiple routing types. The [plan comparison](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists AI Assist, AI Drafts and AI Summarize, while Envoy is priced separately at US$0.75 per resolution.

Zendesk Support Team includes automations and triggers. Suite Team adds AI Agents and Action Builder. Suite Professional adds skills-based routing and an IVR phone tree. The [Zendesk pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/?so=1), checked November 22, 2026, lists features by tier but does not show a per-outcome AI price in the plan table. Confirm any usage or add-on charges in the quote.

If the goal is to assign and organize email conversations, Help Scout may cover the workflow. If you need routing across channels, phone menus or more formal service operations, Zendesk Suite deserves a closer review.

## Ease of use and team setup

Help Scout's core work areas can be easier to explain to a team moving from shared email. An owner can start with one Inbox, add users, create a Docs site and connect Beacon. The team still needs rules for ownership, tags, saved replies and escalation.

Zendesk gives administrators a larger set of service tools. That can help when several support teams and channels share one process, but the plan and configuration need more attention. Define ticket fields, groups, routing rules and permissions before moving a large backlog. Ask agents to test a normal request, an escalation and a reporting task in both products.

## Integrations and connected tools

Help Scout says it connects with more than 100 apps and provides API access and webhooks. Its [pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists integrations such as Salesforce, Jira and HubSpot on Plus. Check the integration directory and plan access for each system your team depends on.

Zendesk has a marketplace of apps and integrations. For a critical connection, check the exact app, data direction, permissions and plan requirements before moving. Make a checklist for CRM records, ecommerce orders, team chat, issue tracking, analytics and identity management. A listing in a marketplace does not prove that every workflow will transfer.

## Support and administration

Help Scout's [pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists standard email support on Standard and priority support on Plus and Pro. Pro includes a dedicated onboarding specialist and strategic account manager. It also lists SSO/SAML and HIPAA compliance on Pro.

Zendesk's plan structure supports different levels of service operation. Suite Professional adds skills-based routing and IVR, while Suite Enterprise lists further governance and security features. If SSO, audit records, data location or contractual support commitments are mandatory, confirm them in the quote.

## Migration and data portability

Help Scout's [Import2 guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1495-import-emails-or-tickets-into-help-scout), checked November 22, 2026, says it can import emails or tickets with customer profiles and tags from listed providers, including Zendesk. It is available to Account Owners and Administrators on Standard, Plus and Pro. The standard import does not include users, Inbox settings, knowledge base articles, saved replies, macros, workflow rules or some custom fields and statuses.

Zendesk documents API import for tickets, users, organizations and Help Center articles. Its [migration guide](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6696005837082-How-can-I-migrate-information-from-another-platform-into-Zendesk), checked November 22, 2026, says first reply and first resolution metrics are not imported through the ticket import process, and triggers do not run on imported tickets.

Treat migration as a separate project. Export a sample, map users and fields, test attachments, check ticket history and rebuild automation that does not transfer. Do not turn off the old system until the destination has passed the sample review.

## Choose Help Scout if

- Your team wants a shared Inbox and customer conversations are mostly email-led.
- Docs and Beacon are central to how customers find help.
- You prefer a smaller set of support work areas.
- The included plan limits match your user, Inbox and knowledge base needs.
- You want Envoy with a visible per-resolution rate and monthly spending cap.

## Choose Zendesk if

- You need formal ticket routing, analytics and automation.
- Your support spans messaging, live chat or telephony.
- You need a knowledge base and AI Agents within a broader service suite.
- You need advanced routing, IVR or service administration.
- Your team can manage a more detailed ticket configuration.

## Common questions about Help Scout vs Zendesk

### Is Help Scout cheaper than Zendesk?

On November 22, 2026, Help Scout Standard was US$25 per user/month with annual billing or US$30 month-to-month. Zendesk Support Team was US$19 per agent/month with annual billing or US$25 month-to-month. Zendesk Suite Team was US$55 annual-billed or US$69 monthly; Help Scout Plus was US$45 annual-billed or US$54 monthly. Compare features and add-ons, not only the first price.

### Does Zendesk have a free plan?

Zendesk's [public pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/?so=1), checked November 22, 2026, advertises a free trial and lists paid Support and Suite plans. It does not show a permanent free support plan.

### Does Help Scout include a knowledge base?

Yes. Help Scout calls its knowledge base Docs. The [plan page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/), checked November 22, 2026, lists one site on Free, two on Standard, three on Plus and five on Pro. Free also lists ten articles.

### Can I move Zendesk tickets into Help Scout?

Help Scout's [Import2 guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1495-import-emails-or-tickets-into-help-scout), checked November 22, 2026, lists Zendesk as a supported source. It can import emails or tickets with customer profiles and tags, but the standard import does not include every user, Inbox setting, article or automation. Test a sample and confirm what needs to be rebuilt.

### Does Zendesk include AI?

Zendesk's [current pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/?so=1), checked November 22, 2026, lists AI Agents on Suite Team and higher Suite plans. Check the plan comparison and your quote for exact features and any usage charges.

### Which tool is better for a small support team?

Help Scout is a sensible starting point when a small team wants a shared Inbox and Docs. Zendesk Support Team is worth comparing when the team needs ticket routing and reporting. If you need omnichannel support or a knowledge base in Zendesk, compare Suite Team.

## Research method

This comparison uses official Help Scout and Zendesk pricing, product and migration documentation checked November 22, 2026. Monthly and annual prices are labeled separately. Five-seat examples are simple multiplication and exclude taxes, usage, add-ons and contract terms. This is documentation-based research, not hands-on testing, a customer survey or a measured performance review.
