## Research limits and conflicts

Checked October 8, 2026. This is an official-document research comparison, not hands-on app or agent testing. Taskade and Notion pages display numeric paid plan prices but the extracted billing selector is ambiguous, so their base paid prices are not quoted. ClickUp AI add-on numeric rates are omitted for the same reason. Taskade's Free one-time credit grant and paid usage allowances are directly stated. Asana Personal means the current two-user plan, not older legacy allowances. Asana's subscription help page renders a loading/CSS error when opened; indexed official help text establishes the two-seat paid minimum, while the current pricing page establishes plans and included AI products. That help evidence is recorded separately, not represented as a fully loaded page. Airtable AI credit pooling was verified in official billing documentation updated October 2, 2026. n8n is described as self-hostable Community software, without an unsupported open-source license claim. Retool's Free/Team exclusion of external users means its low-cost plans are not equivalent to a customer portal. Prices are displayed in USD except n8n in EUR; no currency conversion, tax estimate or total-cost guarantee. No universal import, whole-workspace export, automatic Genesis migration or code ownership claim.
