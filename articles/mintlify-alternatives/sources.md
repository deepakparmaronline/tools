## Research limits and conflicts

Mintlify Pro selected annual/monthly context was not established from animated pricing text; no numeric quote used. GitBook currently mixes Essential card naming with Premium text; describes the $65/site/month annual display and $12/user/month component without asserting a final team quote. Redocly page uses product selectors; base seat price is not treated as total cost. Open-source licensing is separate from hosting/support. Custom-component and access-control parity require a representative build.
