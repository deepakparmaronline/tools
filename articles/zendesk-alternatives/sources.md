# Evidence ledger

Checked October 28, 2026 unless noted. Prices are volatile and must be checked again before a future update.

## Zendesk
- Product: https://www.zendesk.com/
- Pricing: https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/ — Support Team US$19, Suite Team US$55 and Suite Professional US$115 per agent/month billed yearly. Current official page opened October 28, 2026.
- Export guide: https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408886165402-Exporting-ticket-user-or-organization-data-from-your-account — export formats and access conditions.
- Migration: https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/6696005837082-How-can-I-migrate-information-from-another-platform-into-Zendesk — API import guidance; some ticket metrics are not imported.

## Freshdesk
- Product and pricing: https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/helpdesk-ticketing/ — Growth US$19, Pro US$55 and Enterprise US$89 per agent/month billed annually; first 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions included, additional sessions US$49 per 100, Copilot US$29 per agent/month on Pro and Enterprise.
- Product updates: https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/new-features/ — current 2026 updates include contact/company views, analytics time units and agent field controls. The page states switching to the Free Program is no longer self-serve and should be arranged through Customer Success.
- Free Program: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-program-freshdesk-only- — six months, up to two agents, Freshdesk only.
- Ticket export: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158 — CSV/Excel ticket-list export and its limits.
- Account export: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225487-how-do-i-export-my-helpdesk-data- — broader data export.
- Automation: https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/240670-automation-rules-that-run-on-ticket-creation — ticket creation rules.

## Other alternatives
- Help Scout: https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/ — free plan up to five users; Standard from US$25/user/month.
- Zoho Desk: https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html — free edition for three users; paid prices vary by region.
- Intercom: https://www.intercom.com/pricing — seat pricing plus Fin from US$0.99 per outcome.
- Front: https://front.com/pricing — Starter US$25, Professional US$65, Enterprise US$105 per seat/month billed annually.
- HubSpot Service Hub: https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/service — free tools for up to two users; paid plans use seat and credit arrangements.
- Gorgias: https://www.gorgias.com/pricing/choose-your-plan — ticket-volume pricing; Starter at US$10/month for 50 billable tickets.
- Salesforce Service Cloud: https://www.salesforce.com/service/pricing/ — multiple editions; Starter Suite displayed from US$25/user/month.
