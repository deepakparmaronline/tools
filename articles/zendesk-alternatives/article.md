**Short answer:** Freshdesk is the closest like-for-like alternative to compare first. Help Scout suits teams that prefer a shared inbox, Zoho Desk is worth checking for small teams, and Intercom fits businesses that prioritize customer messaging and outcome-priced AI. The best choice depends on your channels, agent count, automation needs and historical data.

## Why teams look for a Zendesk alternative

Zendesk is not a poor fit by default. Its official pricing page lists ticketing, routing, customer context, analytics and automation on Support Team, with Suite plans adding broader channels and knowledge features. Those capabilities are useful when the workflow needs them, but can be more than a small team needs if it mainly handles email and simple assignments.

Price is another reason to compare. Seat count, annual commitment, AI usage, channel add-ons, migration effort and the plan required for a particular feature all affect total cost. Compare the workflow and plan you will actually use, not only a headline starting price.

## 1. Freshdesk: best first like-for-like comparison

Freshdesk is the first product to compare for a conventional help desk. Freshworks lists ticketing, shared inbox, customer portal, knowledge base and analytics in Growth. Its official page checked October 28, 2026 lists Growth at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month billed annually. The listed plans include the first 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions, with additional sessions at US$49 per 100; Freddy AI Copilot is listed at US$29 per agent/month on Pro and Enterprise. The public table does not label the 500-session allowance as a recurring monthly amount.

**Advantages:** familiar ticketing, explicit tiers, customer portal and knowledge base. **Trade-offs:** verify AI usage, routing and dashboards by plan. **Skip it if:** you need a CRM-first or specialized ecommerce workflow.

## 2. Help Scout: best for shared inbox support

Help Scout focuses on shared conversations, inboxes, help content and workflows. Its official pricing page lists a free plan for up to five users with one inbox and one Docs site; Standard was displayed at US$25 per user/month when checked October 28, 2026.

**Advantages:** a focused option for email-first teams. **Trade-offs:** compare inbox, reporting, SLA and workflow limits. **Skip it if:** your process requires advanced routing beyond the plan.

## 3. Zoho Desk: best for small teams

Zoho Desk offers a free edition for up to three users, with ticketing, a support email address and a help center described in its help content. Paid prices depend on region and billing. **Advantages:** low-friction start and a logical candidate for existing Zoho users. **Trade-offs:** check automation, reporting, AI and department limits. **Skip it if:** a required feature is only in an unaffordable edition.

## 4. Intercom: best for messaging and AI

Intercom combines a shared inbox and ticketing with Messenger, a help center and Fin AI Agent. Its current pricing page shows seat-based plans and Fin pricing from US$0.99 per outcome. **Advantages:** worth evaluating for product-led teams focused on messaging. **Trade-offs:** outcome charges affect total cost. **Skip it if:** you need predictable seat-only pricing.

## 5. Front: best for collaborative inboxes

Front centers on shared inboxes, assignments, internal comments, templates and rules. Its page checked October 28, 2026 showed Starter at US$25, Professional at US$65 and Enterprise at US$105 per seat/month billed annually. **Advantages:** useful for teams coordinating customer conversations. **Trade-offs:** check channel, automation and reporting limits.

## 6. HubSpot Service Hub: best for CRM-connected service

HubSpot Service Hub connects support workflows with HubSpot CRM. Its pricing page lists free tools for up to two users and seat-based paid plans. **Advantages:** service context alongside the wider CRM. **Trade-offs:** confirm seats, credits, onboarding and setup costs. **Skip it if:** you need a standalone help desk without CRM scope.

## 7. Gorgias: best for ecommerce support

Gorgias is built around ecommerce support. Its billing documentation says the help desk fee is based on ticket volume, and AI Agent may add outcome-based fees. **Advantages:** evaluate it when support is closely tied to ecommerce operations. **Trade-offs:** forecast ticket volume and AI outcomes before comparing costs.

## 8. Salesforce Service Cloud: best for CRM-led operations

Salesforce Service Cloud is worth evaluating when service workflows need to connect with Salesforce CRM and enterprise processes. Its official pricing page lists multiple editions with substantial differences in functionality and price. **Advantages:** CRM context and a wider service operation. **Trade-offs:** implementation, training and add-ons can increase total cost.

## How to switch safely

Zendesk documents API-based ticket imports and notes that first reply time and first resolution time are not imported through its documented ticket import path. Export availability can depend on account settings and plan. Before cancelling, inventory tickets, comments, attachments, users, organizations, custom fields, macros, triggers, help articles and reports. Export a sample, map fields, test import and compare record counts. Rebuild routing and reporting instead of assuming they transfer.

## How to choose

- List the channels and workflow features you must keep.
- Calculate total cost for your seat count and likely usage.
- Decide whether you need a help desk, shared inbox, CRM suite or ecommerce platform.
- Verify export, import and retention options.
- Trial high-risk workflows with representative data before switching.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest alternative to Zendesk?

Freshdesk is a sensible first comparison for conventional ticketing. Read the [Freshdesk hub](/freshdesk/) and [Freshdesk review](/freshdesk/review/) before shortlisting it. The best fit depends on plan, channels and migration needs.

### Which alternatives have a free plan?

Help Scout lists a free plan for up to five users, Zoho Desk a free edition for three users, and HubSpot free service tools for up to two users. Check current limits with each vendor.

### Is Freshdesk cheaper?

The entry annual prices displayed October 28, 2026 were US$19 per agent/month for Freshdesk Growth and Zendesk Support Team. Suite features, add-ons and AI usage change the total.

### Can Zendesk tickets be migrated?

Options depend on destination and data type. Review both vendors' documentation and test a sample before committing.

### Should I switch only to save money?

Compare seats, usage charges, implementation and migration effort. A lower seat price may not lower total cost if you need extra products or must rebuild workflows.
