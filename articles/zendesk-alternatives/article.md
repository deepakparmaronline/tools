## Why compare alternatives?

Zendesk offers structured ticketing, routing, customer context, analytics and automation. Suite plans add broader channels and knowledge capabilities. These can be useful for a multi-agent support operation, but a smaller team may only need email, assignment and searchable answers. Compare the plan you need, not only an entry price. Seat count, annual commitment, AI usage, add-ons, implementation and migration affect total cost.

## Freshdesk

Freshdesk is a practical first comparison for conventional help desk workflows. Freshworks lists ticketing, shared inbox, customer portal, knowledge base and analytics in Growth. Its [official pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/), checked October 28, 2026, lists Growth at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month billed annually. Listed plans include 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions, with extra sessions charged separately. Freshworks also documents a six-month Free Program for eligible Freshdesk customers, limited to two agents and not available in Freshdesk Omni; it is not a permanent free tier. Check the [Freshdesk pricing guide](https://toolboxkart.tech/freshdesk/pricing/) and [official Free Program guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-program-freshdesk-only-) before choosing. It is less suitable if your main need is CRM-first service or a specialized ecommerce workflow.

## Help Scout

Help Scout focuses on shared conversations, inboxes, help content and workflows. Its pricing page lists a free plan for up to five users with one inbox and one Docs site. Standard was displayed at US$25 per user/month on October 28, 2026. Compare inbox, reporting, SLA and workflow limits before switching.

## Zoho Desk

Zoho Desk has a free edition for up to three users, with ticketing, support email and a help center described in its help content. Paid pricing depends on region and billing. It is worth considering for small teams and existing Zoho users, but check automation, reporting, AI and department limits.

## Intercom

Intercom combines a shared inbox and ticketing with Messenger, help center and Fin AI Agent. Its pricing page shows seat-based plans and Fin pricing from US$0.99 per outcome. Outcome charges can change total cost, so model usage before switching. It is less suited to teams that want seat-only predictable pricing.

## Front

Front centers on shared inboxes, assignments, internal comments, templates and rules. Its page checked October 28, 2026 showed Starter at US$25, Professional at US$65 and Enterprise at US$105 per seat/month billed annually. Compare channel, automation and reporting limits with the workflow you need.

## HubSpot Service Hub

HubSpot Service Hub connects support workflows with HubSpot CRM. Its pricing page lists free tools for up to two users and seat-based paid plans. Confirm seats, credits, onboarding and setup fees in the total. It is most relevant when support data already belongs in HubSpot.

## Gorgias

Gorgias is designed around ecommerce support. Its billing documentation says help desk fees depend on ticket volume, and AI Agent may add outcome-based fees. Forecast ticket volume and AI outcomes. It is less relevant to a general service desk unrelated to ecommerce.

## Salesforce Service Cloud

Salesforce Service Cloud suits organizations that need service workflows connected to Salesforce CRM. Its official pricing page lists multiple editions with different capabilities and costs. Implementation, training and add-ons can increase the total, so it may be excessive for a simple support inbox.

## Switching safely

Zendesk documents API-based ticket imports and notes that first reply time and first resolution time are not imported through its documented import path. Export access may depend on plan and account settings. Inventory tickets, comments, attachments, users, organizations, custom fields, macros, triggers, help articles and reports. Export a sample, map fields, test import, compare record counts and rebuild routing instead of assuming it transfers.

## How to choose

1. List channels and workflow features you must keep.
2. Calculate total cost for your actual seats and likely usage.
3. Decide whether you need a help desk, shared inbox, CRM suite or ecommerce platform.
4. Verify export, import and retention options.
5. Trial high-risk workflows with representative data before switching.

## FAQs

### What is the closest alternative to Zendesk?
Freshdesk is a sensible first comparison for conventional ticketing. The best fit depends on plan, channels and migration needs.

### Which alternatives have a free plan?
Help Scout lists a free plan for up to five users, Zoho Desk a free edition for three users, and HubSpot free tools for up to two users. Confirm current limits on the vendor pages.

### Is Freshdesk cheaper?
The entry annual prices displayed on October 28, 2026 were US$19 per agent/month for Freshdesk Growth and Zendesk Support Team. Suite features and usage can change the total. Freshdesk's separate six-month Free Program is limited to eligible customers and two agents, so confirm current eligibility rather than treating it as a permanent free plan.

### Can Zendesk tickets be migrated?
Options depend on destination and data type. Review both vendors' documentation and test a sample.

### Should I switch only to save money?
Compare seats, usage charges, implementation and migration effort. A lower seat price may not lower total cost if extra products or workflow rebuilds are needed.
