# 6 MobiLoud Alternatives for Turning a Website into an App

Median is the first MobiLoud alternative to examine if you want to reuse an existing website and manage more of the app configuration yourself. AppMySite offers another website-conversion route. GoodBarber and Tapcart fit teams willing to shape a separate app experience, with Tapcart limited to Shopify. FlutterFlow and Draftbit are rebuild options for greater control over the application. Keep MobiLoud when managed launch and ongoing maintenance are the services you need. Comparing a builder subscription with a managed-service fee is incomplete unless you also account for implementation, support and app-store work.

## Website reuse is different from rebuilding the storefront

[MobiLoud](https://www.mobiloud.com/) converts existing website experiences into mobile apps and provides launch and maintenance services. Its central advantage is reusing the web work you already maintain.

The [Business offer](https://www.mobiloud.com/pricing/) is $1,499 per month, or $1,274 per month billed annually, plus a one-time development and submission fee. It includes up to 10,000 monthly active users; the page lists additional usage charges. OneSignal campaigns use your own account and billing.

Keep MobiLoud when your mobile website works well and you want a service handling the native layer. Consider alternatives when you want a separate app design, can handle release work internally, or need source-level control. Do not assume that changing vendors fixes a slow or confusing underlying website.

This research comparison uses official product, plan and relevant platform documentation. It includes no performed app builds, store-submission tests or claimed conversion-rate improvements. Rebuild tools are explicitly broader alternatives because they require a different implementation project.

## Median: retain the website and configure the native layer

[Median](https://median.co/), formerly GoNative, packages web content in iOS and Android apps. Its [documentation](https://docs.median.co/docs/overview) describes the web/native integration model, making it relevant when your team wants to keep the website as the main content system.

The [personal pricing](https://median.co/pricing) includes a branded free option. Starter lists US$229 for activation and US$179 a year beginning after 12 months, with solo-developer scope. Business plans and optional services require their own comparison. Do not describe this as an unrestricted lifetime commercial package.

Choose Median if you can own configuration and want to retain existing web flows. The advantage is greater involvement in the native layer without rebuilding every page.

Skip the personal offer as a substitute for a managed business service with a team and custom support. Check native plugin needs, white labeling, identifiers and publishing help. Test external authentication and links with the actual site; a page loading inside an app does not prove checkout, uploads and login redirects work correctly.

## AppMySite: choose the right conversion product

[AppMySite](https://www.appmysite.com/) separates website conversion, WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify and custom-app products. Its [web-to-app offering](https://www.appmysite.com/webtoapp/) is the direct candidate when you have an existing website.

That distinction matters more than an undifferentiated app-builder label. A custom app without a website and a web-view conversion do not give you the same maintenance or data model.

The [public pricing page](https://www.appmysite.com/pricing/) separates those products, but reliable numeric plan amounts were not available in this research. Confirm Android and iOS publishing, branding, app count, push features and renewal terms in the quote for your chosen product.

Choose it if the self-service conversion workflow matches your site and you want to manage the project more directly. Skip it if you expect all MobiLoud launch and ongoing maintenance services to be included automatically. Evaluate one complete customer journey and ask how a live app is updated after a website or platform change. Source-code portability was not established.

## GoodBarber: accept a separate app workspace

[GoodBarber](https://www.goodbarber.com/) offers content and ecommerce app building, including native apps and progressive web apps. It is useful when an app-specific structure matters more than reproducing every part of the website.

The [category pricing](https://www.goodbarber.com/pricing/) starts at $30 per month billed yearly for content and $40 per month billed yearly for ecommerce. Those are entry offers. The [content plan details](https://www.goodbarber.com/pricing/content/) should be checked for the exact Android/iOS tier, extensions and publication services.

Choose GoodBarber for a publisher, membership project or store that can maintain an app experience intentionally. Its advantage is shaping the app around that use case.

Skip it when preserving every custom website feature without rebuilding is the core requirement. Inventory how articles, customer accounts, product options and transactions will be populated. A lower starting price can still involve migration and separate content operations. The advertised trial is for evaluation, not permanent free production hosting.

## Tapcart: design a Shopify storefront for the app channel

[Tapcart](https://www.tapcart.com/) is a Shopify mobile-app platform. Its app workspace is relevant if your commerce operation runs on Shopify and marketing staff want to design screens and campaigns specifically for mobile customers.

The [pricing page](https://www.tapcart.com/pricing) states that Shopify is required and separates Growth, Scale and Enterprise scope, with paid add-ons. The extracted page contained inconsistent dynamic annual figures. A numeric rate is withheld here until the actual plan and commitment can be confirmed.

Choose Tapcart when app-specific merchandising and layouts matter more than exact parity with the website. A useful example is evaluating a product collection, loyalty integration and targeted campaign as one app experience.

Skip it for a non-Shopify website or when a custom web feature must appear unchanged. Verify every indispensable store app and checkout behavior rather than assuming an integration logo means the whole workflow is included. Campaign tooling and implementation help can affect total cost; request a complete quote.

## FlutterFlow: build the app you want instead of converting pages

[FlutterFlow](https://www.flutterflow.io/) is a visual app-development environment. It fits a team that wants new screens, backend connections and application logic rather than a repackaged website.

Its [Free plan](https://flutterflow.io/pricing) supports building and testing. The displayed Basic offer is $39 per month and adds source-code download and app-store deployment features; confirm the selected billing cycle. Collaboration and other development features have higher-tier requirements. Backend services and store accounts remain separate considerations.

Choose it if a named developer or capable builder will own authentication, data access and release testing. The advantage is control over the application model, with code export available on a paid plan.

Skip it when you need a managed website conversion with little implementation work. Reusing an API is different from reusing the website interface. Prototype one real authenticated flow and an error state before estimating the rebuild. Exporting source code helps portability, but you still need a working build environment and knowledge of its dependencies.

## Draftbit: prioritize exportable code and continuing ownership

[Draftbit](https://draftbit.com/) combines app creation with visual editing. It is another option for teams prepared to build a separate application and maintain its code.

The current [Standard plan](https://draftbit.com/pricing/) includes code editing/export and native publishing, with a displayed $12 monthly figure. Confirm the monthly/yearly selector and editor-seat costs. AI usage can be supplied through a separate subscription or credits, so a builder price is not the full operating budget.

Choose Draftbit when code ownership and ongoing development matter. A useful trial exercise is creating an authenticated page against an existing API, exporting the project and confirming your team can build it outside the editor.

Skip it as a direct substitute for outsourced MobiLoud maintenance if nobody will own releases after export. Paid publishing access does not eliminate configuration or store review. Check what remains accessible after downgrading and what help is included for submission. An exported project is a starting point for maintenance, not the end of it.

## Evaluate login, checkout and deep links before migrating

Use a real acceptance checklist rather than judging a generated home screen:

1. Sign in, close the app and reopen it. Verify session behavior and logout.
2. Follow an external login redirect and return to the intended screen.
3. Open a product or article through a deep link.
4. Complete the applicable checkout flow and inspect error handling.
5. Test file selection, notifications and any native feature the website depends on.
6. Inspect a weak-network state and the behavior after a website update.

These are proposed tests, not work reported as completed in this article. Ask who fixes a failed test and whether that service is part of the subscription or a separate engagement.

Apple’s [review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) include minimum-functionality requirements. A website-conversion feature alone is not proof of approval. Judge the actual app experience and the current platform requirements rather than assuming any vendor’s general claim covers your app.

## Keep app identity and account ownership in the migration plan

Replacing a vendor may mean updating an existing app within your developer account, rebuilding it, or transferring an account-owned app. Those are different operations.

Apple’s [app-transfer guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer/) explains continuity subject to transfer criteria and the separate exchange of code and build assets. It does not promise that moving a builder project automatically moves everything an app uses.

Record developer-account ownership, bundle/package identifiers, signing arrangements, push configuration, deep-link domains, analytics and third-party service accounts. Ask the destination provider whether it can continue the existing listing and how it handles users already running the old build. Do not cancel the previous service until the release and support transition is clear.

Compare the full budget: initial implementation, the subscription, required integrations, platform accounts and continuing maintenance. A self-service builder can be appropriate when your team owns those tasks. A managed service can be appropriate when it does not.

## Questions about leaving MobiLoud

### Which option most directly reuses an existing website?

Median and AppMySite’s website-conversion product are the closest routes in this selection. Verify the site’s authentication, payment and native-feature requirements.

### Is Tapcart suitable for any website?

No. Its current pricing page states Shopify support. Consider a different route for a non-Shopify application.

### Does a free builder plan include a production iOS app?

Do not assume so. Some plans are for building or web publishing; others include branded app output. Verify the specific platform and paid publishing requirements.

### Will my existing store listing and users be preserved?

This depends on account ownership, app identity, platform rules and whether the destination can update the existing app. Obtain a concrete migration plan.

### Are FlutterFlow and Draftbit simple conversions?

They are application-development alternatives. Expect screen, backend and release work rather than automatic reproduction of the existing website.

### Can an app-builder guarantee store approval?

Store approval depends on the app and the current platform rules. Inspect the scope and conditions of any provider commitment instead of treating it as a general technical guarantee.

Start with Median when website reuse and internal ownership are the goals. Consider GoodBarber or Tapcart when designing a separate app channel is acceptable. Use a code-oriented builder when the app itself should be rebuilt, and keep MobiLoud when managed maintenance is the service you value.
