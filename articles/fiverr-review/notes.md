# Editorial notes: Fiverr review

- Primary keyword: Fiverr review.
- Audience: buyers evaluating a service marketplace and freelancers deciding whether the Gig model fits their work.
- Review method: documentation-based comparison of the product model, official fee disclosures, Gig setup requirements, payout details, review rules and dispute workflow.
- No hands-on testing, numerical score, testimonial, customer outcome or AggregateRating markup is claimed.
- The seller share and buyer fee are kept distinct. The buyer Help Center page and Payment Terms page describe different fee elements; the page directs readers to the checkout total for their region rather than treating one number as universal.
- Payout methods vary by location. The review links to the current withdrawal guide and does not promise that every method is available in every country.
- No official bulk migration path for reviews or seller levels was confirmed.
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-31.
