## SERP notes
Public SERP searches on 2026-10-31 returned current alternatives roundups and official pricing pages. Dominant intent is mixed: buyers want to hire a freelancer and compare total project cost; freelancers want to compare commission, discovery, contract type and payout. This article splits the buyer and seller decision paths instead of treating one fee number as the only decision factor. The saved Semrush snapshot includes PAA as a feature but no exact question text, so the FAQ copy is not presented as exact PAA wording.

## Limits
- No official bulk transfer path for Fiverr Gigs, reviews, account level or buyer history was confirmed.
- Fee schedules vary by contract, country, payment method, billing currency and provider plan. Readers are directed to official checkout/pricing pages.
- No hands-on testing, rating, customer outcome, affiliate link or sponsored placement is claimed.
