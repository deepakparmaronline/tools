# Editorial and publication notes: Gmail alternatives

## Selection rationale

Seven alternatives cover distinct needs: Outlook.com for Microsoft integration; Proton Mail and Tuta Mail for privacy-focused email; Zoho Mail and Fastmail for custom-domain hosting; iCloud Mail for Apple users; Yahoo Mail for a familiar consumer inbox. The list avoids generic mail clients as direct provider replacements and explains that a client change does not move the mailbox.

Public SERP discovery on 2026-11-01 showed mixed intent across Gmail competitors, privacy-first email, Outlook comparisons, custom-domain hosting and email-client questions. ## Evidence and limits

Official product, pricing, help and migration sources checked 2026-11-01 Asia/Kolkata. Fastmail and Microsoft prices are labelled with billing terms. Tuta prices are labelled as the EU selector. Zoho and Yahoo details are qualified where regional or account-specific information prevents one universal number. No user ratings, deliverability benchmarks, customer quotes, hands-on tests or unsupported encryption promises are used.
