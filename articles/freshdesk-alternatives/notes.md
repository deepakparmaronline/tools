# Editorial notes: Freshdesk alternatives

## Search intent and content gap
The saved target is commercial: “Freshdesk alternative” (US volume 480, KD 16, CPC $46.98, intent C, rising trend). Saved SERP features are SL, AIO, Rev, Vid and Rel. Do not change these metrics and do not invent a PAA list.

Current public results tend to list 8 to 16 products with price tables and short “best for” blurbs. This page uses eight products but gives a different decision path: identify the support model first, then compare per-seat vs per-ticket vs outcome pricing, free-plan limits, AI charges and migration work.

## Why these eight
- Zendesk: closest broad help desk comparison and existing review on ToolboxKart.
- Zoho Desk: free edition for three users and a broader Zoho ecosystem.
- Help Scout: shared inbox model and a documented free-plan contact limit.
- Intercom: in-app messaging and Fin outcome pricing.
- HubSpot Service Hub: support connected to CRM records.
- Gorgias: ecommerce-focused, ticket-volume pricing.
- Front: collaborative inbox with explicit channel and automation limits.
- LiveAgent: ticketing with chat and call-center options.
