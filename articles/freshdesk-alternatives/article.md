# 8 Freshdesk Alternatives for Support Teams in 2026

> Editorial source of truth for the public page at https://toolboxkart.tech/freshdesk/alternatives/. Content and source dates checked on October 28, 2026. This is documentation-based research, not hands-on testing.

**Short answer:** Zendesk is the closest broad help desk to compare first, while Zoho Desk is worth checking for a lower-cost route, Help Scout for email-led teams, and Gorgias for ecommerce support. Do not switch only because of a headline price. Compare the plan you need, AI usage fees, reporting, channels and the data you can move.

Freshdesk itself may still be the right choice if its ticketing, customer portal and knowledge base cover your workflow. This guide is for teams that have a specific reason to reconsider it, not for anyone who simply wants a different logo.

## Why teams look for a Freshdesk alternative

Freshdesk's paid plans cover core ticketing, a shared inbox, a customer portal and a knowledge base. The reason to move usually comes from a mismatch in the next layer: a team needs a different routing model, more flexible reporting, a CRM-led service record, ecommerce order context, or a different way to pay for AI and support volume.

Cost also deserves a full calculation. The current Freshdesk pricing page lists Growth at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month with annual billing. It also lists the first 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions as included and additional sessions at US$49 per 100. Freddy AI Copilot is a separate US$29 per agent/month add-on on Pro and Enterprise. These details were checked October 28, 2026 on the [official Freshdesk pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/helpdesk-ticketing/). A low seat price can be less attractive if the features or AI allowance you need sit outside it.

Do not assume Freshdesk Omni and Freshdesk are the same package. Freshworks describes Freshdesk as email-first and Freshdesk Omni as its multichannel product. If your team relies on WhatsApp, social messaging or a unified channel view, confirm the exact product and plan before comparing prices.

## 1. Zendesk: compare it when workflow depth matters

Zendesk is the most direct broad comparison for a team that still wants a structured help desk. Its official pricing page lists ticketing, routing, customer context, analytics and automation in Support Team, with broader channels and knowledge capabilities in Suite plans. Freshdesk and Zendesk both list entry plans at US$19 per agent/month with annual billing on the pages checked October 28, 2026, so the decision is not simply that one has a lower entry price.

**Advantages:** worth evaluating when routing rules, reporting, customer context and a wider support suite are central to the process. **Trade-offs:** feature access changes by plan, and AI or channel costs should be priced separately where applicable. **Who should skip it:** a small team that only needs a shared mailbox and basic ticket ownership may not need a broader suite.

Use the [Zendesk review](/zendesk/review/) and [Zendesk vs Freshdesk comparison](/compare/zendesk-vs-freshdesk/) to compare specific workflows. Check [Zendesk's official pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/) before buying.

## 2. Zoho Desk: check it for a cost-conscious support team

Zoho Desk is a sensible candidate when you want ticket management and customer support inside the wider Zoho ecosystem. Its official plan comparison shows a Free edition limited to three users, while paid plan prices and taxes depend on the region shown by Zoho. Checked October 28, 2026: the [official plan comparison](https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html) lists Free, Express, Standard, Professional and Enterprise editions and describes the user limits by tier.

**Advantages:** the free edition gives a small team a route to begin, and the wider Zoho stack may reduce friction if you already use Zoho CRM or related apps. **Trade-offs:** compare the actual edition available in your country, including automation, SLAs, reporting, AI and role permissions. **Who should skip it:** a team that depends on a feature available only in a higher tier should cost that tier before assuming the switch saves money.

Zoho Desk is not a drop-in replacement by name alone. Map ticket fields, assignment rules and customer records to the Zoho model, then test a sample import before moving the whole help desk.

## 3. Help Scout: a better match for email-led support

Help Scout is built around shared customer conversations, a shared inbox and help content. That model may suit a team that finds a traditional ticket queue too heavy but still needs ownership, saved replies and customer history. The official pricing page checked October 28, 2026 lists a free plan for up to five users and Standard from US$25 per user/month. Its [official free-plan guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1597-free-plan) says the free tier is limited to 100 contacts per month, one inbox and five users.

**Advantages:** the free tier can be enough to evaluate a small email support workflow, and the shared inbox model is easy to compare with a team mailbox. **Trade-offs:** contact limits matter more than the user count if your team handles many unique customers. Paid tiers add more capacity, channels and controls. **Who should skip it:** a team that needs complex ticket routing, several operational queues or highly structured SLA workflows should test those requirements before switching.

Compare the number of people answering support and the number of customers they help. These are different limits, and both affect whether the free plan is useful.

## 4. Intercom: choose it for conversational support, not just ticket storage

Intercom combines a customer-facing Messenger, shared inbox, ticketing and its Fin AI Agent. The official pricing page checked October 28, 2026 lists Essential from US$29 per seat/month with annual billing and Fin at US$0.99 per outcome. Advanced and Expert cost more and add workflow, collaboration or security features. See [Intercom's official pricing](https://www.intercom.com/pricing) for current terms.

**Advantages:** it is worth considering when support starts inside a product or website and the team wants messaging and AI resolution in the same workflow. **Trade-offs:** the budget needs both seat costs and expected Fin outcomes, plus any usage-based messaging channels. **Who should skip it:** a team that wants predictable per-agent pricing and mainly handles email may prefer a simpler ticketing product.

Estimate a realistic month of customer conversations before comparing Intercom with Freshdesk. A small seat count does not guarantee a small bill if many AI outcomes or paid channels are involved.

## 5. HubSpot Service Hub: connect service with the CRM

HubSpot Service Hub makes sense when support needs to sit alongside CRM contacts, deals and customer activity. The official pricing page checked October 28, 2026 lists free tools for up to two users, plus paid Starter, Professional and Enterprise options. Paid plans use different seat and credit arrangements, and Professional and Enterprise list one-time onboarding fees. Review [HubSpot's official pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/service) instead of comparing a single starter number with a Freshdesk seat price.

**Advantages:** service conversations can be evaluated in the context of the customer record and the wider HubSpot platform. **Trade-offs:** the total depends on seat type, plan, credits, onboarding and the other HubSpot products your team uses. **Who should skip it:** a support team that does not need CRM integration may be paying for a wider platform than it uses.

Before migrating, decide whether your source of truth is the help desk or the CRM. Test how contacts, companies, tickets and activity history appear in HubSpot and which reporting needs rebuilding.

## 6. Gorgias: a specialist for ecommerce support

Gorgias is designed for ecommerce support and connects customer service conversations with store context. That makes it worth evaluating when agents need to understand orders, returns and other retail questions while replying. Its official plan guide checked October 28, 2026 lists Starter at US$10/month for 50 billable tickets, with higher plans based on larger monthly ticket allowances. See the [official Gorgias plan guide](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing/choose-your-plan).

**Advantages:** its ticket-volume model may be a better fit than per-agent billing for a retail operation with a predictable number of customer conversations. **Trade-offs:** ticket allowances and AI usage fees must be modeled, and the pricing is not directly comparable with a per-seat help desk. **Who should skip it:** a non-retail support team may not benefit enough from ecommerce-specific workflows.

Estimate both monthly tickets and the number of agents who need access. Check whether the plan includes the channels and integrations your store uses before moving records.

## 7. Front: shared inbox collaboration across teams

Front is a candidate when customer conversations are handled by several teams and collaboration matters as much as ticket assignment. The official pricing page checked October 28, 2026 lists Starter at US$25 per seat/month billed annually for up to 10 seats, Professional at US$65 for up to 50 seats and Enterprise at US$105. It describes channel, automation and analytics differences by plan. See [Front's official pricing](https://front.com/pricing).

**Advantages:** the shared inbox approach can suit teams that work together on email and other conversations. **Trade-offs:** Starter has a single-channel type and a limit on automation rules; omnichannel support and advanced reporting require a higher tier. **Who should skip it:** teams that depend on a classic ticket lifecycle, complex SLA rules or help-desk-specific reporting should confirm those needs against the plan details.

Front also has a 14-day trial of Professional with documented limits on teammates, workspaces, channels and messages. Those limits were checked October 28, 2026 in the [official trial and plan guide](https://help.front.com/en/articles/2178).

## 8. LiveAgent: include phone and live chat in the same comparison

LiveAgent is worth shortlisting when a support team wants ticketing, live chat and call-center features from one vendor. Its official pricing page checked October 28, 2026 lists Small Business at US$15 per agent/month billed annually or US$19 monthly, Medium Business at US$29 annually or US$35 monthly, and Large Business at US$49 annually or US$59 monthly. The tiers differ in call-center, SLA, reporting and other features. Source: [LiveAgent official pricing](https://www.liveagent.com/pricing/).

**Advantages:** the published pricing gives a clear starting point for comparing a help desk that includes chat and phone features. **Trade-offs:** verify which tier includes the call center, IVR, SLAs, SSO and reporting your team needs. **Who should skip it:** a business whose main requirement is deep CRM or ecommerce context should compare a product built around that workflow.

Do not compare only the entry plan. Make sure the same phone, chat, automation and reporting requirements are included in the cost on both sides.

## Moving away from Freshdesk: what to export first

Freshdesk documents ticket-list exports to CSV or Excel for users with the required Admin or Supervisor access. The official guide checked October 28, 2026 also warns that a ticket-list export does not include the entire conversation or archived ticket history. A broader account export is available through account administration. Read [How to export tickets](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158) and [How to export help desk data](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225487-how-do-i-export-my-helpdesk-data-) before planning a switch.

Make a migration inventory that includes ticket IDs, replies, attachments, contacts, companies, custom fields, tags, macros, ticket forms, automation rules, SLAs, knowledge articles, integrations and reports. Some destination products will map these items differently, and workflow rules usually need to be rebuilt rather than copied as-is.

1. Export a small, representative date range and a sample of older tickets.
2. Check that conversations, attachments, customer links and custom fields are present.
3. Map source fields to the destination and record fields that have no equivalent.
4. Import the sample into a test environment and compare record counts.
5. Rebuild assignment, notification, SLA and knowledge workflows, then test them with real scenarios.
6. Keep the source account until stakeholders approve the imported history and the new support queue.

## How to choose the right Freshdesk alternative

- **Start with the problem:** name the workflow Freshdesk cannot support at an acceptable cost. Avoid switching just because another plan table looks shorter.
- **Compare the exact product:** Freshdesk and Freshdesk Omni have different channel scope. Apply the same level of detail to every alternative.
- **Calculate the full bill:** include agents, AI outcomes or sessions, channels, onboarding, taxes and any ticket-volume charges.
- **Test the hardest workflow:** try a complex ticket, escalation, handoff or customer-history lookup rather than only a simple email.
- **Prove data portability:** confirm the records and history that will move, then test the import before you cancel.

## Freshdesk alternatives FAQ

### What is the closest alternative to Freshdesk?

Zendesk is the closest broad help desk comparison in this list. Zoho Desk is worth checking for price-sensitive teams, Help Scout for email-led support, and Gorgias for ecommerce. The right choice depends on channels, workflow depth and total cost.

### Does Freshdesk have a free plan?

Freshdesk has a Free Program for up to two agents for six months. Freshworks says this program applies to Freshdesk, not Freshdesk Omni. Its current 2026 product updates say switching to the Free Program is no longer self-serve and should be arranged through a Customer Success Manager. Check the [official Free Program guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000010099-explore-freshdesk-free-program-freshdesk-only-) and [product updates](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/new-features/) for current eligibility and limits.

### Is Zendesk better than Freshdesk?

Neither is better for every team. The entry prices match on the official annual-billing pages checked October 28, 2026, but plan features, AI usage, channels and migration requirements differ. Compare the exact workflows you need.

### Can I export tickets from Freshdesk before switching?

Freshdesk documents ticket exports to CSV or Excel for admins and supervisors. Its support article says a ticket-list export does not include the full conversation or archived ticket history; account export is needed for broader data.

### What should I check before moving away from Freshdesk?

Inventory tickets, full conversations, archived records, contacts, companies, attachments, knowledge articles, fields, automations, SLAs, reports and integrations. Export a sample and test the destination import before cancelling.
