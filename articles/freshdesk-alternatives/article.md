# 8 Freshdesk Alternatives for Support Teams in 2026

Canonical: https://toolboxkart.tech/freshdesk/alternatives/

<p><strong>Short answer:</strong> Start with Zendesk if you need a broad ticketing replacement, Help Scout if you want a smaller shared inbox, and Zoho Desk if a lower-cost starting point is important. The right Freshdesk alternative depends on what you are trying to fix: plan cost, AI usage, reporting limits, channel coverage or data portability.</p>
<p>This list compares eight different product models rather than naming one universal winner. It uses Freshdesk's email-first product and public pricing page, not an assumed Freshdesk Omni bundle. Official prices and feature details below were checked October 29, 2026; confirm the current product, billing selector and usage terms before purchase.</p>

<h2 id="why-switch">First decide why you want to leave Freshdesk</h2><p>A price increase, a missing feature and a poor workflow are different problems. If the issue is the number of agents, calculate the total seat cost. If it is AI usage, compare how each vendor counts sessions, outcomes or resolutions. If it is reporting, check the exact plan that includes the dashboard or SLA control. If the issue is portability, test export and import before choosing a destination.</p><p>Freshdesk's current pricing page lists Growth at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month billed annually. It lists the first 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions as included and additional sessions at US$49 per 100; the table does not clearly state the renewal period for that included allowance. Pro and Enterprise list Freddy AI Copilot at US$29 per agent/month as an add-on. These figures were checked October 29, 2026 on the <a href="https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/">official Freshdesk pricing page</a>. Include add-ons and the correct product SKU in your cost comparison.</p>
<h2 id="zendesk">1. Zendesk: closest broad help-desk comparison</h2><p>Zendesk is the first alternative to evaluate when the team still wants a conventional ticketing platform with clear routing, customer context, automation and reporting. Its pricing page lists Support Team at US$19 and Suite Team at US$55 per agent/month billed yearly, with Suite Professional at US$115. Those prices and plan details were checked October 29, 2026 on the <a href="https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/">official Zendesk pricing page</a>.</p><p>Choose Zendesk when its plan covers the channels, workflows and reporting you need. It is not automatically cheaper than Freshdesk, and Suite features can change the total. Before switching, compare equivalent channels and plan limits rather than comparing only the lowest number. For the product-side review, see the <a href="/zendesk/">Zendesk hub</a> and <a href="/zendesk/review/">Zendesk review</a>.</p>
<h2 id="helpscout">2. Help Scout: shared inbox with a free entry plan</h2><p>Help Scout is worth a close look if the team mainly answers email and values shared conversations, a knowledge base and workflows more than advanced ticket-routing controls. Its pricing page lists a Free plan for up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Standard was displayed at US$25 per user/month on October 29, 2026; the page includes a Monthly/Annual selector and an annual savings marker, so verify the selected billing period. <a href="https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/">Official pricing, checked October 29, 2026.</a></p><p>Help Scout has a practical migration advantage: the <a href="https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1495-import-emails-or-tickets-into-help-scout">official import guide</a>, checked October 29, 2026, lists Freshdesk as a supported source. The tool is available to account owners and administrators on Standard, Plus and Pro. That does not mean every automation or report transfers; test a sample import and map the fields you rely on.</p>
<h2 id="zoho">3. Zoho Desk: lower-cost entry for small teams</h2><p>Zoho Desk lists a Free edition for up to three users. Its official comparison page shows separate Free, Express, Standard, Professional and Enterprise tiers, with prices affected by region, currency and billing. Because the page is localized and does not expose a single universal US price in the view checked, this guide does not quote a paid starting amount. <a href="https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html">Official plan comparison, checked October 29, 2026.</a></p><p>Zoho Desk is worth testing when the team is small or already uses Zoho products. Check department limits, automation, reporting and AI access in the exact edition. A free tier can be a good start, but a paid feature gap may outweigh the initial saving if the team grows.</p>
<h2 id="intercom">4. Intercom: messaging and AI-led service</h2><p>Intercom combines a shared inbox and ticketing with Messenger, a public help center and Fin AI Agent. Its pricing page displayed Essential at US$19 per seat/month with annual billing selected and Fin from US$0.99 per outcome on October 29, 2026. The page also showed a new-customer discount from US$29, so verify the current promotion and selector before budgeting. <a href="https://www.intercom.com/pricing">Official pricing, checked October 29, 2026.</a></p><p>Intercom fits teams that want customer messaging and automated resolution to be central to the support experience. It is a weaker choice if the goal is predictable seat-only pricing: model the expected number of Fin outcomes alongside seats, channels and any add-ons. An outcome charge is not directly comparable with Freshdesk's session meter.</p>
<h2 id="front">5. Front: collaborative inbox for shared ownership</h2><p>Front centers on shared inboxes, assignments, internal comments, templates and rules. Its official pricing page checked October 29, 2026 displayed Starter at US$25, Professional at US$65 and Enterprise at US$105 per seat/month billed annually. <a href="https://front.com/pricing">Official pricing.</a></p><p>Front is worth considering when customer conversations cross departments and the team needs collaboration around a shared inbox. Compare the exact channels, automation and reporting available in the selected tier. It is not a direct fit if your process depends on Freshdesk-specific ticket fields, portal behavior or SLA rules that you have not mapped.</p>
<h2 id="hubspot">6. HubSpot Service Hub: support tied to CRM context</h2><p>HubSpot Service Hub makes more sense when support needs to sit beside customer, sales and account data already maintained in HubSpot. Its official pricing page lists free tools for up to two users, while paid costs depend on seats, feature access and onboarding. The pricing page was checked October 29, 2026. <a href="https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/service">Official Service Hub pricing.</a></p><p>Choose it when the CRM connection solves a real handoff problem. Avoid buying a broader suite just to replace a shared inbox if your team will not use the CRM features. Confirm which service features require paid seats, credits or setup before calculating the cost.</p>
<h2 id="gorgias">7. Gorgias: ecommerce support and order context</h2><p>Gorgias is built around ecommerce support. Its official pricing page describes ticket-volume pricing rather than a simple per-agent model, and it notes that AI Agent charges depend on resolved conversations. The page was checked October 29, 2026. <a href="https://www.gorgias.com/pricing">Official Gorgias pricing.</a></p><p>It is worth a look when a large share of support requests are about orders, returns or store operations. Forecast monthly ticket volume and AI resolutions before comparing cost. It is less relevant for a general service desk that does not need ecommerce-specific context.</p>
<h2 id="salesforce">8. Salesforce Service Cloud: CRM-led service operations</h2><p>Salesforce Service Cloud suits organizations that need service workflows connected to Salesforce CRM and broader business processes. The official pricing page lists Starter Suite from US$25 per user/month, notes that transaction fees apply, and says the starting price can be billed monthly or annually. The page was checked October 29, 2026. <a href="https://www.salesforce.com/service/pricing/">Official Service Cloud pricing.</a></p><p>Do not treat Starter Suite as a like-for-like help-desk quote: it bundles sales, service, marketing, commerce and built-in AI. Larger Service Cloud editions have different costs and capabilities. Implementation, training and add-ons can matter as much as the seat price.</p>
<h2 id="migration">How to leave Freshdesk without losing the support history</h2><p>Freshdesk's <a href="https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158">official ticket export guide</a>, checked October 29, 2026, describes filtered ticket exports and limitations. Do not assume that a ticket-list export includes every conversation, archived ticket, attachment, custom field, report or automation. Inventory the records and configuration you must keep, export a sample and inspect the result before selecting a destination.</p><ol><li>List tickets, comments, attachments, users, organizations, custom fields, macros, rules, knowledge articles and reports that must move.</li><li>Ask the destination vendor which record types and fields it can import and what needs manual rebuilding.</li><li>Run a sample import that includes an old ticket, a reopened ticket and an attachment-heavy case.</li><li>Compare record counts, timestamps, customer profiles and tags before switching email forwarding.</li><li>Keep the source system and a rollback plan available until agents have tested the new workflow.</li></ol><p>If Help Scout is your target, its official Import2 guide lists Freshdesk as a supported source on Standard, Plus and Pro. If you choose another platform, confirm the import route and any plan restrictions directly with that vendor.</p>
<h2 id="choose">How to choose the right replacement</h2><ol><li>Write down the one or two Freshdesk limits that actually cause work or cost.</li><li>Choose the product model that fits: ticketing, shared inbox, messaging, CRM or ecommerce support.</li><li>Calculate cost for your real seats and likely AI or ticket volume.</li><li>Verify data export, import, retention and permissions with official documentation.</li><li>Test representative tickets before cancelling Freshdesk or moving the public support address.</li></ol><p>For a deeper look at the current product, see the <a href="/freshdesk/">Freshdesk hub</a>, <a href="/freshdesk/review/">review</a>, <a href="/freshdesk/pricing/">pricing guide</a> and <a href="/freshdesk/how-to-use/">setup guide</a>. If Freshdesk still looks like the best fit, use those pages to check the plan and workflow before renewing.</p>
<h2 id="faq">Freshdesk alternatives FAQ</h2><h3>What is the closest alternative to Freshdesk?</h3><p>Zendesk is the closest broad help-desk comparison; Zoho Desk is worth checking for a smaller budget; Help Scout suits email-first teams. The right choice depends on channels, routing, AI usage and migration needs.</p><h3>Is there a free Freshdesk alternative?</h3><p>Help Scout lists a free plan for up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Zoho Desk lists a free edition for up to three users, and HubSpot Service Hub lists free tools for up to two users. Confirm current limits on official pages.</p><h3>Can I move Freshdesk tickets to Help Scout?</h3><p>Help Scout's official import tool lists Freshdesk as a supported source on Standard, Plus and Pro. Test a sample and map fields before switching email forwarding or retiring the old help desk.</p><h3>What should I compare besides price?</h3><p>Compare ticket fields, routing, SLA controls, portal and knowledge base, AI usage meters, reporting, export coverage, integrations and migration effort. A lower base price may not reduce total cost.</p>
