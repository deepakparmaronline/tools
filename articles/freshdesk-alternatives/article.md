# 7 Freshdesk Alternatives for Different Support Teams

If Freshdesk no longer fits your support workflow, start by identifying what needs to change. [Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/) is a direct help desk comparison, [Zoho Desk](https://www.zoho.com/desk/) has a free edition for three users, and [Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/) suits smaller teams that prefer a shared inbox. [Front](https://front.com/) focuses on collaborative conversations, [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) puts messaging and AI at the center, and [Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/) is designed for ecommerce support. The right choice depends on channels, seats, usage fees and the data you must move.

**Short answer:** Compare Zendesk for a full help desk, Zoho Desk for a lower-cost starting point, and Help Scout for email-first support. Choose Front, Intercom or Gorgias only when their collaboration, messaging or ecommerce focus matches your actual workflow.

## When it makes sense to keep Freshdesk

Freshdesk may still be the right choice if your team mainly works through email, needs a shared ticket queue, and uses the customer portal, knowledge base and reporting in its current plan. Its US pricing page checked October 29, 2026 lists Growth at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month when billed annually. The page also lists the first 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions included once per account on those plans, with additional sessions priced separately. See the [Freshdesk pricing guide](/freshdesk/pricing/) for the plan details.

One distinction matters before you switch: Freshdesk is the email-first help desk, while [Freshdesk Omni](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk-omni/) is the separate multichannel product. If you are missing chat, social or other channel support, check whether you need a different plan or the separate Omni product rather than assuming every channel is part of the Freshdesk plan you already have.

## Why teams compare alternatives

### Plan limits do not match the workflow

A team may need custom dashboards, advanced routing, multiple service-level policies, skill-based assignment or stronger administration controls. Compare the exact feature table for your current plan with the plan required by each alternative. A feature shown on a vendor's website is not necessarily included in the entry tier.

### AI changes the cost model

Freshdesk's listed AI Agent allowance is limited, and extra sessions are charged in packs. Other tools may charge per seat, per ticket, per resolution or for a mix of these. Estimate a typical month of ticket volume and likely AI usage before calling one option cheaper.

### The team needs a different kind of support workspace

Freshdesk is built around a help desk and ticket queue. Help Scout emphasizes a shared inbox and knowledge content; Front emphasizes collaboration around customer conversations; Intercom is suited to messaging-led product support; and Gorgias focuses on ecommerce workflows. These are not interchangeable feature sets. Pick the model that matches how requests arrive and how agents solve them.

### Migration or administration is becoming hard

Changing tools does not remove the need to maintain ticket categories, assignment rules, permissions, saved replies and reports. A switch can be worthwhile when the destination simplifies a specific workflow, but only after you know which historical data can be moved and which automations need to be rebuilt.

## 7 Freshdesk alternatives in detail

### 1. Zendesk: for structured support operations

[Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/) is the closest broad help desk comparison on this list. Its official US pricing page checked October 29, 2026 lists Support Team at US$19, Suite Team at US$55 and Suite Professional at US$115 per agent/month billed yearly. Support Team focuses on email ticketing, routing, customer context, analytics and automation. Suite plans add broader channel and knowledge capabilities.

**Why consider it:** Zendesk is worth evaluating when your support operation needs clear ownership, more routing controls, reporting and a larger integration ecosystem. It is also useful when you want to compare a core ticketing plan with a broader service suite.

**Trade-off:** The entry Support plan is not the same scope as Suite. AI, channel coverage and add-ons can change the total bill. Do not compare the US$19 entry price with Freshdesk Growth as if every feature is equivalent.

**Skip it if:** you mainly need a simple shared inbox or cannot justify the administration and plan cost. Read the [Zendesk hub](/zendesk/), [Zendesk review](/zendesk/review/) and [Zendesk vs Freshdesk comparison](/compare/zendesk-vs-freshdesk/) before deciding.

### 2. Zoho Desk: for small teams and Zoho users

[Zoho Desk](https://www.zoho.com/desk/) offers a Free Edition for three user licenses. Its official pricing page also describes a 15-day trial and paid editions that add features such as social support, web forms, knowledge base, custom reporting, telephony, Zia AI and skill-based assignment. Availability depends on the edition.

**Why consider it:** The free edition can suit a very small team that needs basic email ticketing. Zoho Desk is also worth a look if the business already uses Zoho CRM or other Zoho products and wants its support process connected to that ecosystem.

**Pricing note:** The official page uses a region and currency selector. The page view checked for this article showed INR pricing, so a current US-dollar paid starting price is not quoted here. Select your region and billing term on the [official pricing page](https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing.html) before comparing totals.

**Trade-off:** Check which edition includes the exact channels, departments, automation and reporting controls you need. A free tier is useful only if its user and feature limits fit your actual workload.

**Skip it if:** a required workflow is available only in a higher edition that does not fit your budget or if your team wants a very different interface and operating model.

### 3. Help Scout: for human-led email support

[Help Scout](https://www.helpscout.com/) is built around shared conversations, inboxes, customer history and its Docs knowledge base. Its pricing page checked October 29, 2026 lists a free plan for up to five users with one inbox and one Docs site. Paid plans are displayed from US$25 per user/month, with higher tiers adding more team, workflow and administration features.

**Why consider it:** It fits a support team that wants agents to work in a familiar conversation view and publish useful help content without managing a large help desk configuration. The free plan can also be a low-risk way for a small team to see whether the shared-inbox model is enough.

**Trade-off:** Confirm inbox counts, routing, service-level rules, reporting and user limits before switching. Help Scout also lists Envoy, an AI add-on priced at US$0.75 per resolution, so estimate AI usage separately if you plan to use it.

**Skip it if:** your team needs complex multichannel routing, advanced enterprise controls or a support model driven by specialized workflows rather than shared conversations.

### 4. Front: for collaboration around customer conversations

[Front](https://front.com/) focuses on shared inboxes and team collaboration. Its pricing page checked October 29, 2026 displayed Starter at US$25, Professional at US$65 and Enterprise at US$105 per seat/month billed annually. Its plan scope changes across collaboration, rules, channels and reporting, so compare the feature table with the work your team needs to do.

**Why consider it:** Front is a strong candidate when requests are handled by several departments and agents need to discuss ownership or context around a customer conversation. It can suit teams that want a shared communication workspace rather than a ticket queue that is managed only by a support department.

**Trade-off:** Seat costs add up as more people need access. Check the required plan for rules, integrations, analytics and any channels your team relies on. Do not assume that the Starter plan includes every collaboration or reporting feature.

**Skip it if:** your process depends on help desk features that are not included in your chosen tier or if you need a product centered on ecommerce order management.

### 5. Intercom: for messaging-led and AI-assisted support

[Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/) combines a shared inbox and ticketing with Messenger, a public help center and its Fin AI Agent. The official pricing page checked October 29, 2026 showed Essential at US$19 per seat/month for new customers in its annual-billing display, with US$29 shown as the prior rate; Advanced was US$85 and Expert US$132 per seat/month. Fin is priced at US$0.99 per outcome. Promotions and usage terms can change.

**Why consider it:** Intercom fits software and digital businesses that want support to sit close to their product experience, in-app messaging and proactive customer communication. Its help center and automation options can support a messaging-led workflow.

**Trade-off:** Cost combines seats with outcome-based AI and possible usage charges for other channels. Model both seat count and expected resolutions. The US$0.99 outcome charge is not a flat fee for unlimited AI use.

**Skip it if:** you need predictable seat-only pricing or your support work is mostly traditional email ticketing with little need for in-app messaging.

### 6. Gorgias: for Shopify and ecommerce support

[Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/) is designed around ecommerce customer service. Its current pricing page says the help desk scales from 50 to 5,000 tickets per month and is not priced per agent. The same page describes AI Agent charges when it resolves a conversation, with Voice and SMS available as usage-based add-ons. The exact plan amount depends on the ticket allowance and should be confirmed on the vendor page.

**Why consider it:** Gorgias is worth evaluating when agents need customer-service work closely connected to an online store and its order-related workflow. It is a more focused option than a general-purpose help desk for teams whose main support volume comes from ecommerce.

**Trade-off:** Ticket-volume pricing means the monthly bill follows usage rather than simply multiplying seats. Estimate ticket counts and AI resolution volume. Confirm how tickets and AI outcomes are counted before choosing a plan.

**Skip it if:** your company is a SaaS business, professional service firm or internal support team without an ecommerce-centered workflow.

### 7. LiveAgent: for teams that need chat and phone alongside tickets

[LiveAgent](https://www.liveagent.com/) brings ticketing, live chat, phone support, social channels and a knowledge base into one customer-service workspace. Its official pricing page checked October 29, 2026 advertises a 30-day free trial, no setup fee and no credit card requirement. The product has several per-agent tiers, so confirm the current price and included channels in the official plan selector.

**Why consider it:** It can suit teams that want a traditional help desk with live chat and call-center options in the same product. That makes it worth comparing when a ticket-only plan leaves the team juggling separate tools for chat or phone.

**Trade-off:** Check the exact limits for email addresses, reporting, social channels, phone usage and automation on the plan you choose. A list of supported channels does not mean all of them are included in every tier.

**Skip it if:** your business needs a highly specialized ecommerce or product-led messaging workflow that another platform supports more directly.

## Which alternative fits your support model?

- **Need a broad ticketing platform:** compare Zendesk and Freshdesk feature by feature, including the higher plan you may need.
- **Need a small free starting point:** check Zoho Desk's three-user Free Edition and Help Scout's five-user Free plan, then compare their limits.
- **Want an inbox that feels closer to email:** evaluate Help Scout and Front, but test the routing and reporting requirements that matter to your team.
- **Support a software product through in-app messaging:** consider Intercom and model both seat and AI outcome charges.
- **Run a Shopify store:** evaluate Gorgias against your ticket volume and ecommerce workflow.
- **Need live chat and phone in one help desk:** review LiveAgent's channel and usage limits before committing.

## How to move from Freshdesk without losing support history

Start by listing the records your team must keep: tickets, replies, private notes, attachments, contacts, companies, knowledge articles, custom fields, macros, automations, SLA rules and reports. Do not assume a standard ticket export contains the full conversation or older archived data.

Freshdesk's official [ticket export guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158) explains that a filtered ticket export does not include the entire conversation or archived ticket history. For broader account data, use the documented [account export process](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/237405-how-can-i-export-tickets-including-the-content-of-the-ticket-). The [Freshdesk API documentation](https://developers.freshdesk.com/api/) is another reference for ticket and contact records.

1. Export a small sample before requesting the full dataset.
2. Check whether replies, attachments, timestamps, requesters and custom fields are included.
3. Map fields to the destination and identify data that needs manual handling.
4. Rebuild assignment rules, saved replies, permissions and reports in the new product.
5. Test a sample import with the team that will use it, then compare record counts and customer links.
6. Keep the old help desk available until the team confirms that required history and workflows are usable.

Migration time is part of the decision. A cheaper subscription may not be cheaper overall if it requires extensive manual cleanup or rebuilding. If Freshdesk already handles the workflow well, improving ticket categories and automation may be safer than switching.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best alternative to Freshdesk?

There is no single best choice for every team. Zendesk is a direct broad help desk comparison, Zoho Desk suits small teams and Zoho users, Help Scout suits email-first teams, and the other options fit collaboration, messaging, ecommerce or phone-led workflows.

### Does Freshdesk have a free plan?

Freshdesk's official help article describes a Free Program for two agent seats for six months after the trial. It applies to Freshdesk, not Freshdesk Omni, so it should not be treated as a permanent free plan.

### Which option is closest to Freshdesk?

Zendesk and Zoho Desk are useful first comparisons for a conventional help desk. Compare the actual channels, routing, reporting and plan limits rather than relying on a broad product label.

### Which Freshdesk alternative is best for ecommerce?

Gorgias is designed around ecommerce customer support and is worth evaluating for Shopify-centered workflows. Check its ticket allowance, AI outcome charges and channel add-ons against your real monthly volume.

### Can Freshdesk data be migrated to another help desk?

Some data can be exported, but the available format depends on the export method. Freshdesk says a filtered ticket export does not include the full conversation or archived ticket history, so review the account export guide and test a sample before switching.
