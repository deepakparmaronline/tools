# 8 Freshdesk Alternatives for Support Teams in 2026

Canonical: https://toolboxkart.tech/freshdesk/alternatives/

**Short answer:** Start with Zendesk if you need a broad ticketing replacement, Help Scout if you want a smaller shared inbox, and Zoho Desk if you need a low-cost starting point for a small team. The right Freshdesk alternative depends on whether you need more routing depth, a simpler inbox, CRM context, ecommerce workflows or a different AI billing model.

This list compares eight different product models rather than naming one universal winner. It covers Freshdesk's email-first product, not an assumed Freshdesk Omni bundle. Official prices and feature details were checked October 29, 2026. This is documentation-based research, not a hands-on test.

- **Zendesk. Best for:** teams that want structured ticketing, more routing options and a broad support suite.
- **Help Scout. Best for:** small support teams that work mainly from a shared inbox and knowledge base.
- **Zoho Desk. Best for:** smaller teams or companies already using Zoho products.

## First decide why you want to leave Freshdesk

A price increase, a missing feature and a poor workflow are different problems. If the issue is the number of agents, calculate the total seat cost. If it is AI usage, compare how each vendor counts sessions, outcomes or resolutions. If it is reporting, check the exact plan that includes the dashboard or SLA control. If the issue is portability, test export and import before choosing a destination.

Freshdesk's public pricing page lists Growth at US$19, Pro at US$55 and Enterprise at US$89 per agent/month billed annually. Freshworks' official AI add-on guide says new customers on these paid plans receive a one-time complimentary 500-session allowance. Extra packs cost US$49 per 100 and are valid for the current billing cycle. Pro and Enterprise list Freddy AI Copilot at US$29 per agent/month as an add-on. These details were checked October 29, 2026 on the [official pricing page](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/) and [official AI add-on guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011515).

The public pricing page shows a 14-day Enterprise trial and does not list a Free plan. Some Freshdesk support documentation still refers to a Free program, so confirm whether it is available to new accounts before treating it as a long-term option. The [official support-email guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/37541-set-up-your-support-email) was checked October 29, 2026.

## 1. Zendesk: closest broad help-desk comparison

Zendesk is the first alternative to evaluate when the team still wants conventional ticketing with clear routing, customer context, automation and reporting. Its pricing page lists Support Team at US$19 and Suite Team at US$55 per agent/month paid yearly, with Suite Professional at US$115. These prices and plan details were checked October 29, 2026 on the [official Zendesk pricing page](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/).

Choose Zendesk when the plan covers the channels, workflows and reporting you need. It is not automatically cheaper than Freshdesk, and Suite features can change the total. Compare equivalent channels and plan limits rather than comparing only the lowest number. For the product-side review, see the [Zendesk hub](/zendesk/) and [Zendesk review](/zendesk/review/).

**Why it may beat Freshdesk:** the published Suite tiers add channels and support controls that may fit a larger operation. **Tradeoff:** the plan you need can cost more than the entry price, and a migration still needs field mapping. **Skip it if:** your team only needs a shared inbox and a small set of assignment rules.

## 2. Help Scout: shared inbox with a free entry plan

Help Scout is worth a close look if the team mainly answers email and values shared conversations, a knowledge base and workflows more than advanced ticket-routing controls. Its pricing page lists a Free plan for up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Standard was displayed at US$25 per user/month on October 29, 2026; the page includes a Monthly/Annual selector, so verify the selected billing period. [Official pricing, checked October 29, 2026.](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/)

Help Scout's official import guide lists Freshdesk as a supported source on Standard, Plus and Pro. The guide says the import can bring emails or tickets with customer profiles and tags, but it does not import users or inbox settings, knowledge base articles, saved replies, ticket rules, automations, custom fields or custom statuses. Review the [official import guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1495-import-emails-or-tickets-into-help-scout), checked October 29, 2026, and test a sample before switching.

**Strength:** a small team can start with the free plan and keep conversation history close to the shared inbox. **Tradeoff:** complex routing and field-driven workflows may need redesign, and the Free plan has a 100-contact monthly limit. **Skip it if:** your support operation depends on deep ticket states, advanced SLA logic or a broad multichannel console.

## 3. Zoho Desk: lower-cost entry for small teams

Zoho Desk lists a Free edition for up to three users. Its official comparison page shows Free, Express, Standard, Professional and Enterprise tiers, with prices affected by region, currency and billing. The view checked October 29, 2026 did not expose one universal US price, so this guide does not invent a paid starting amount. [Official plan comparison, checked October 29, 2026.](https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html)

Zoho Desk is worth evaluating when the team is small or already uses Zoho products. Check department limits, automation, reporting and AI access in the exact edition. The Free tier is a useful starting point, but a paid feature gap may outweigh the initial saving if the team grows.

**Advantage:** the free edition gives a small team a way to start without buying a full seat package. **Tradeoff:** the best paid tier depends on localized pricing and the features your team actually uses. **Skip it if:** your organization does not want the Zoho ecosystem or needs a workflow that has not been verified in the target edition.

## 4. Intercom: messaging and AI-led service

Intercom combines a shared inbox and ticketing with Messenger, a public help center and Fin AI Agent. Its pricing page displayed Essential at US$19 per seat/month with annual billing selected and Fin from US$0.99 per outcome on October 29, 2026. The page also showed a new-customer discount from US$29, so verify the current promotion and selector before budgeting. [Official pricing, checked October 29, 2026.](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)

Intercom fits teams that want customer messaging and automated resolution to be central to support. An outcome charge is not directly comparable with Freshdesk's session meter. Forecast seat costs and expected Fin outcomes together, then confirm which features and channels are included in the selected plan.

**Best reason to move:** messaging and AI are central to the customer experience rather than a side feature. **Tradeoff:** outcome-based charges can make cost depend on resolution volume. **Skip it if:** the main requirement is a conventional ticket queue with predictable seat-only pricing.

## 5. Front: collaborative inbox for shared ownership

Front centers on shared inboxes, assignments, internal comments, templates and rules. Its official pricing page checked October 29, 2026 displayed Starter at US$25, Professional at US$65 and Enterprise at US$105 per seat/month billed annually. [Official pricing.](https://front.com/pricing)

Front is worth considering when customer conversations cross departments and the team needs collaboration around a shared inbox. Compare the exact channels, automation and reporting available in the selected tier. It is not a direct fit if your process depends on Freshdesk-specific ticket fields, portal behavior or SLA rules that you have not mapped.

**Strength:** shared ownership can suit sales, account management and support teams that work on the same conversation. **Tradeoff:** the seat price alone does not prove the required ticket controls are included. **Skip it if:** your workflow needs a traditional service desk with specific ticket-state and SLA behavior.

## 6. HubSpot Service Hub: support tied to CRM context

HubSpot Service Hub makes more sense when support needs to sit beside customer, sales and account data already maintained in HubSpot. Its official pricing page lists free tools for up to two users, while paid costs depend on seats, feature access and onboarding. The page was checked October 29, 2026. [Official Service Hub pricing.](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/service)

Choose it when the CRM connection solves a real handoff problem, such as an agent needing the account owner and recent sales context. Confirm which service features require paid seats, credits or setup before calculating cost.

**Strength:** support records can sit in the same CRM context as sales and account activity. **Tradeoff:** the total may include broader CRM features and onboarding that a simple help desk does not need. **Skip it if:** you will not use the CRM functions and only want a shared inbox.

## 7. Gorgias: ecommerce support and order context

Gorgias is built around ecommerce support. Its official pricing page describes ticket-volume pricing rather than a simple per-agent model, and AI Agent charges can add another usage meter. The page was checked October 29, 2026. [Official Gorgias pricing.](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing)

It is worth a look when a large share of support requests are about orders, returns or store operations. Forecast monthly ticket volume and AI resolutions before comparing cost. It is less relevant for a general service desk that does not need ecommerce-specific context.

**Advantage:** an ecommerce team can evaluate order-related support in the product model built for that work. **Tradeoff:** volume and AI charges need a forecast, not just a seat count. **Skip it if:** your main work is B2B technical support or internal service requests.

## 8. Salesforce Service Cloud: CRM-led service operations

Salesforce Service Cloud suits organizations that need service workflows connected to Salesforce CRM and broader business processes. The official pricing page lists Starter Suite from US$25 per user/month, notes that transaction fees apply, and says the starting price can be billed monthly or annually. The page was checked October 29, 2026. [Official Service Cloud pricing.](https://www.salesforce.com/service/pricing/)

Do not treat Starter Suite as a like-for-like help-desk quote: it bundles sales, service, marketing, commerce and built-in AI. Larger editions have different costs and capabilities. Implementation, training and add-ons can matter as much as the seat price.

**Strength:** service can share records and process context with the broader Salesforce setup. **Tradeoff:** configuration and total cost can be excessive for a simple support queue. **Skip it if:** you do not use Salesforce CRM or need a lightweight help desk with little administration.

## How to leave Freshdesk without losing the support history

Freshdesk's [official ticket export guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/225158), checked October 29, 2026, describes filtered ticket exports and limitations. Do not assume that a ticket-list export includes every conversation, archived ticket, attachment, custom field, report or automation. Inventory the records and configuration you must keep, then export a sample and inspect the result before selecting a destination.

Help Scout's [official import guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1495-import-emails-or-tickets-into-help-scout), checked October 29, 2026, lists Freshdesk as a supported source on Standard, Plus and Pro. It says users and inbox settings, knowledge base articles, saved replies, macros, ticket rules, automations, custom fields and custom statuses need separate handling. Ask the destination vendor which record types and fields it can import. Compare record counts, timestamps, customer profiles, tags and attachments before switching email forwarding. Keep the source system and a rollback plan until agents have checked the new workflow.

## How to choose the right replacement

1. Write down the one or two Freshdesk limits that actually cause work or cost.
2. Choose the product model that fits: ticketing, shared inbox, messaging, CRM or ecommerce support.
3. Calculate cost for your real seats and likely AI or ticket volume, including annual commitment.
4. Verify data export, import, retention and permissions with official documentation.
5. Test representative tickets and important integrations before cancelling Freshdesk or moving the public support address.

If Freshdesk still fits the workflow, keeping it may be less risky than moving for a small price difference. ## Freshdesk alternatives FAQ

### What is the closest alternative to Freshdesk?

Zendesk is the closest broad help-desk comparison; Zoho Desk is worth checking for a smaller budget; Help Scout suits email-first teams. The right choice depends on channels, routing, AI usage and migration needs.

### Is there a free Freshdesk alternative?

Help Scout lists a free plan for up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. Zoho Desk lists a free edition for up to three users, and HubSpot Service Hub lists free tools for up to two users. Freshdesk's public pricing page shows a 14-day trial but does not list a Free plan; some support docs still reference a Free program, so confirm new-account eligibility.

### Can I move Freshdesk tickets to Help Scout?

Help Scout's official import tool lists Freshdesk as a supported source on Standard, Plus and Pro. Test a sample and map fields before switching email forwarding or retiring the old help desk.

### Are the first 500 Freshdesk AI sessions monthly?

No. Freshworks says the 500 sessions are a one-time complimentary allowance for new customers on Growth, Pro or Enterprise. Extra packs cost US$49 per 100 and are valid for the current billing cycle. See the [official AI add-on guide](https://support.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/50000011515), checked October 29, 2026.

### What should I compare besides price?

Compare ticket fields, routing, SLA controls, portal and knowledge base, AI usage meters, reporting, export coverage, integrations and migration effort. A lower base price may not reduce total cost.

### What should I export before switching?

Inventory tickets, comments, attachments, contacts, organizations, custom fields, macros, rules, knowledge articles, reports and user permissions. Test the exported records and confirm what the destination can import before changing email forwarding.
