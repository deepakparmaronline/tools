## Alternatives and their official pricing
- Zendesk: https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/
  - Support Team from $19 per agent/month and Suite tier comparison. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Zoho Desk: https://www.zoho.com/desk/pricing-comparison.html
  - Free edition for three users and paid tier structure; prices may be localized. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Help Scout: https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/
  - Free plan for up to five users and Standard from $25/user/month. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Help Scout free plan details: https://docs.helpscout.com/article/1597-free-plan
  - 100 contacts per month, one shared inbox and five users. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Intercom: https://www.intercom.com/pricing
  - Essential from $29/seat/month on annual billing; Fin from $0.99 per outcome. Checked October 28, 2026.
- HubSpot Service Hub: https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/service
  - Free tools for up to two users; paid seat and credit arrangements vary by tier. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Gorgias: https://www.gorgias.com/pricing/choose-your-plan
  - Starter at $10/month for 50 billable tickets; higher plans scale by ticket allowance. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Front: https://front.com/pricing
  - Starter $25/seat/month annually, Professional $65, Enterprise $105; plan limits differ. Checked October 28, 2026.
- Front trial and plan notes: https://help.front.com/en/articles/2178
  - 14-day Professional trial and stated limits. Checked October 28, 2026.
- LiveAgent: https://www.liveagent.com/pricing/
  - Small Business from $15/agent/month annually or $19 monthly; higher tiers and feature differences. Checked October 28, 2026.
