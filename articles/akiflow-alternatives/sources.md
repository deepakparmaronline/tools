## Research limits and conflicts

Akiflow homepage now advertises automatic time blocking, conflicting with older comparisons describing it as strictly manual. The article avoids that stale absolute claim. Motion pricing extraction shows individual/team and billing toggles without establishing the selected state, so a comparable numeric quote is omitted. Todoist paid currency prices were not exposed. Reclaim has stale promotional wording; paid billing context and attendee costs are not assumed. A universal Akiflow export/import route was not verified.
