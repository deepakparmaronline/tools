# Manychat Guide: Features, Channels and Fit

Manychat is a chat marketing platform for creators, brands and teams that want to turn social messages into useful conversations. It automates replies to comments and direct messages, then helps a team collect leads, share links and move a conversation toward a sale or a human reply.

## What Manychat does

A trigger starts an automation. The Flow Builder connects messages, buttons, questions, conditions and actions. Triggers can include an Instagram comment, a story reply, a direct message or a keyword.

For example, a creator can ask people to comment a word under a Reel. Manychat can detect the comment, post a public reply and send a private message with a link. The same flow can collect an email address or add a tag. The [Instagram comments trigger guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17741897-instagram-post-and-reel-comments-trigger) explains the post, keyword and reply settings.

Manychat is mainly a chat marketing and messaging automation product. It is not a full ticketing help desk for teams that need complex case queues and service-level reporting.

## Channels and account requirements

Free and Essential can connect two channels from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Messenger and Telegram. Pro can connect up to three eligible channels, including WhatsApp, SMS and Email. Business and Advanced allow all eligible channels. Availability depends on plan and region, so check the [official plan comparison](https://manychat.com/pricing/) before planning a campaign.

For Instagram automations, the account must be a Professional account, either Business or Creator. The [connection guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17741886-how-to-connect-instagram-to-manychat) explains the supported connection options. The [troubleshooting guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17741923-instagram-automation-troubleshooting) covers permissions and message access.

## Understand Active Contacts

Manychat counts a contact as active when a message is sent or received through Manychat during the billing month, including through automations, AI, broadcasts or the Inbox. A person who only follows the account is not automatically an Active Contact.

The monthly allowance ranges from 25 on Free to 25,000 on Advanced. Paid plans can charge for contacts above the included allowance. The count resets with the billing cycle, even on an annual subscription. Read the [Active Contacts guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742213-active-contacts), checked on November 23, 2026, before a campaign or viral post increases message volume.

## Who should consider Manychat?

Manychat can fit a creator sharing lead magnets, a local business answering repeated Instagram questions, or an ecommerce brand sending product links after a comment. It can also help an agency build repeatable flows for client accounts.

It is less suitable as the only system for a team that needs advanced ticket ownership, a long service history across departments or a full help desk migration. WhatsApp templates and some service messages have separate usage charges. Contact imports also have plan and consent rules.

## Integrations and data movement

Manychat connects to Zapier on paid plans. The [official integration guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17741916-zapier-integration) describes ways to send collected lead details to other apps.

For exports, Manychat documents Data Collection CSV, Google Sheets actions and External Requests. Email and SMS imports are limited to certain paid plans, require support approval and require consent. Review the [export guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742236-how-to-export-contacts-data) and [import guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742252-how-to-import-contacts-for-email-and-sms-campaigns) before planning a migration.

## Common questions

### Does Manychat have a free plan?

Yes. Free includes 25 Active Contacts per month, one user, one Inbox seat and up to four live automations. It does not include WhatsApp, SMS or Email. Checked on November 23, 2026 in the [Free plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742189-free-plan).

### Can Manychat send an automatic Instagram DM after a comment?

Yes. The Instagram Comments Reply trigger can watch a selected post or Reel, match keywords or any comment, and start a private reply flow. The first private reply node has content restrictions. See the [official setup guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17741897-instagram-post-and-reel-comments-trigger).

### Is Manychat a customer support ticketing system?

No. It is mainly a chat marketing and messaging automation platform. Teams that need complex ticket queues should compare dedicated support software.
