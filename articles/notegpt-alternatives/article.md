# 6 NoteGPT Alternatives for Video Notes, PDFs and Study

Choose a NoteGPT alternative according to what happens after the summary. Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM, suits a collection of sources you want to question together. Glasp fits highlights and YouTube notes in the browser, while Recall helps build a library you revisit. Scholarcy focuses on research papers; Knowt turns material into study practice; ScreenApp handles recordings and transcripts. These replace different parts of NoteGPT's learning workflow. None should be assumed to replace its entire creation suite. Start with one familiar source, check the generated claims, and confirm the input limits before paying or migrating a library.

## NoteGPT alternatives at a glance

| Alternative | Useful replacement | Cost or free allowance | Main constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini Notebook | A notebook of research sources | Standard access with 50 sources per notebook | YouTube import uses public captioned transcripts |
| Glasp | Browser highlights and video summaries | Free tier; Pro $15 monthly or $150 yearly | Private highlights require a paid plan |

Prices and material limits were checked on October 8, 2026. USD prices can differ by region, taxes, purchase channel and promotion. This is an official-source comparison, with suggested evaluation steps rather than claimed hands-on results.

## What NoteGPT does, and when to keep it

This article concerns NoteGPT at notegpt.io. Its current website spans summaries, transcription, research and creation tools. YouTube, PDF and audio workflows sit alongside functions for producing other media. A replacement for video notes may leave those other uses uncovered. [NoteGPT overview](https://notegpt.io/)

The pricing page distinguishes basic usage from Premium Credits. Feature choice and processing requirements matter, so a plan name alone does not establish what a semester of lectures will cost. Promotional rates and billing states were displayed together during research; this guide does not turn them into an unsupported monthly price. [NoteGPT pricing](https://notegpt.io/pricing)

Keep NoteGPT if its inputs, study outputs and allowances fit your routine. Its own help describes reviewing and editing results, then copying text, downloading transcripts or exporting subtitles. If that already provides the material you need in a familiar workflow, moving to several separate services can add work. [NoteGPT help](https://help.notegpt.io/how-to-use-notegpt)

A reason to switch should be concrete: a source library is hard to revisit, recordings exceed a verified allowance, or you want a different study format. No summary tool should be treated as a substitute for checking an important claim against the source.

## How to compare the replacements

The shortlist covers source organization, input handling, review format and practical cost. There is no universal ranking because a student reviewing lecture cards has different needs from a researcher screening papers.

Use an evaluation sheet with four rows: original source, transcript or extracted text, generated summary, and your corrected notes. The replacement should make the relevant layers easy to identify. A polished summary is less useful when you cannot tell whether a statement came from the source or the model.

For a first comparison, choose one explanation with a number, an exception and a conclusion. Check all three against the original. Then ask how you would find the note a month later and move it elsewhere. These are proposed checks, not reported experiments.

## 1. Gemini Notebook: a defined collection of sources

Google renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook in July 2026. It remains a standalone research product, so older references to NotebookLM may point to the same service. [Google's rename announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/)

It is worth evaluating when your work begins with several related sources rather than a single video. A notebook can hold documents and other supported inputs so you can work with the collection. Standard access currently allows 50 sources per notebook, with higher tiers increasing limits. [Google plan limits](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16213268?hl=en)

**Cost:** start with standard access. Paid access depends on the eligible Google subscription or organizational arrangement; do not assume a consumer upgrade automatically describes your school or employer's account.

**Input tradeoff:** Google documents public YouTube videos with captions and imports only their text transcript. It is therefore a poor substitute for understanding a diagram or demonstration that is never explained aloud. Local audio is transcribed on import, with supported formats and language restrictions. [Source requirements](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270?hl=en)

**Skip it if:** the job is mainly an immediate summary beside a video, or your essential sources fail its import restrictions.

Keep a source collection small enough to understand. Ask a precise question and check the relevant original passage. Even a source-based workflow needs an evidence check.

## 2. Glasp: keep highlights beside browsing

Glasp fits a learner who wants to save selected passages and work with YouTube transcripts while browsing. Its video tool and highlighting workflow are closer to capture during reading or watching than to a full replacement for NoteGPT's workspace. [Glasp YouTube tool](https://glasp.co/youtube-summary)

The distinction between its two summary modes matters. Basic summaries send a transcript and prompt to a chosen AI assistant in another tab. Advanced summaries are generated by Glasp beside the video and saved to the account. The experience and allowances differ.

**Cost:** the free tier includes three basic YouTube summaries per day and three advanced summaries per seven days. Advanced allowances are shared with article and PDF summaries. Pro is $15 per month billed monthly or $150 per year, equivalent to $12.50 per month under an annual commitment. [Glasp pricing and mode definitions](https://glasp.co/pricing)

**Tradeoff:** highlights are public by default; private highlights require paid access. Check visibility before collecting material you do not want shared. Basic summary access also does not provide an unlimited paid subscription to whichever external assistant opens.

**Skip it if:** you need a private institutional research workspace by default, or you mainly want recording capture rather than browser notes.

The useful question is whether the saved highlight still helps you understand the source later. Save enough context and source information to avoid turning a library into isolated quotations.

## 3. Recall: a library you plan to return to

Recall is relevant when the accumulation of articles, videos, PDFs and notes matters as much as generating the next summary. Its official site describes a knowledge library and Markdown export. The older getrecall.ai domain now redirects to recall.it; this is separate from similarly named meeting technology. [Recall overview](https://www.recall.it/)

An example fit is a learner collecting several months of material about one technical subject. The decision is whether connections and later retrieval improve that workflow enough to justify maintaining a library.

**Cost:** Free includes 10 AI summaries per month, with unlimited content saving and personal note writing. Plus is displayed at $10 per month billed yearly, a $120 annual commitment, and adds automatic organization, library chat, quizzes and spaced repetition. [Recall pricing](https://www.recall.it/pricing)

**Tradeoff:** writing notes and linking them manually on Free is different from the automatic organization and review features on Plus. A vendor's unlimited summary label also does not establish unlimited processing of every file type or size.

**Skip it if:** you only summarize a few isolated videos and never revisit them, or you need a specialized academic citation-management workflow.

Markdown export gives you a useful portability route for readable content. It does not prove that every graph relationship, quiz state or review schedule will survive in another application. Inspect a sample export before committing an entire library.

## 4. Scholarcy: screen and organize research papers

Scholarcy is a candidate for readers whose main input is academic material. It creates structured flashcards from texts and describes keeping references, figures and tables alongside saved paper summaries. Its research organization and export functions are more relevant here than a general video-summary ranking. [Scholarcy overview](https://www.scholarcy.com/)

A practical use case is deciding which papers deserve a complete reading before a literature review. The tool can support screening; it does not establish that a summarized finding applies outside the paper's methods or sample.

**Cost:** the Free Article Summarizer lists a limit of 10 summaries and exports flashcards one at a time. The page does not specify a reset period. Paid plans add saved libraries, enhanced summaries and batch export, with a one-week trial described for the monthly offer. Numeric paid amounts did not render and are not verified. [Scholarcy plans](https://www.scholarcy.com/pricing)

**Tradeoff:** Scholarcy's structured paper cards are not automatically the same object as term-and-definition cards for exam practice. Verify the output you need rather than assuming every product's flashcard label means identical review features.

**Skip it if:** YouTube browsing or live lecture capture is the primary workflow.

Check methods, sample details and limitations against the full paper before using its results. Keeping a reference beside a summary is useful, but it does not validate an interpretation.

## 5. Knowt: move from notes into study practice

Knowt fits a student who wants flashcards and practice modes rather than a library organized around summaries. Its official site describes creating study material from files and lectures, together with free modes for reviewing cards. [Knowt study tools](https://knowt.com/)

A useful scenario is a course chapter with definitions you must recall without looking. Generate a small set, correct it, then assess whether the study mode suits your preparation. Card generation alone does not demonstrate learning.

**Cost:** Basic is free. Student Ultra is $24.99 billed monthly, or the page's explicit $149.99 upfront annual total. The annual page also shows a monthly equivalent that does not reconcile with that total, so use the stated charge and verify checkout. AI summaries and other premium tools appear in Ultra. [Knowt plans](https://knowt.com/plans)

**Tradeoff:** free access to study modes does not mean unlimited AI processing of lecture files. Confirm your account's generation allowance before making it the sole replacement for a heavy NoteGPT workflow.

**Skip it if:** you want a research library with careful paper organization, or your intended output is mostly summaries for work rather than study questions.

Inspect cards for overly broad prompts, missing exceptions and invented definitions. A short incorrect answer becomes harder to spot once you start repeatedly practicing it.

## 6. ScreenApp: recordings first

ScreenApp is relevant when your source material is audio or video recordings and the next step is a transcript, summary or question about that recording. Its product focuses on recording capture and analysis. This is a partial replacement for NoteGPT's recording workflow. [ScreenApp overview](https://screenapp.io/)

Consider it for a learner with recorded explanations who needs to locate a specific statement. Evaluate the transcript against the recording before building notes from it. Names, quantities and specialized vocabulary deserve particular attention.

**Cost:** Free lists two transcriptions and three uploads or imports to try, with recordings up to 45 minutes. Those transcription attempts are not advertised as a monthly renewing allowance. Pro is $30 monthly or $228 billed annually, with 50 transcriptions per month and recordings up to two hours. Its seven-day Pro trial requires a card. [ScreenApp pricing](https://screenapp.io/pricing)

**Tradeoff:** duration limits can matter more than the number of files. A single lecture exceeding the cap can require a different plan or another workflow, even if you have unused transcriptions.

**Skip it if:** your essential requirement is PDF-paper screening or a dedicated flashcard curriculum.

Check the file and recording you will actually use, including its length and language. Do not infer accurate transcription of your material from a general product promise.

## Preserve the source when switching

Move one topic first. Keep the original files or source URLs, a transcript where available, your corrected notes and the date of the source. Label generated summaries separately so later readers can distinguish them from the original.

NoteGPT's help documents copying content, downloading transcripts and exporting subtitles. It does not establish a complete automatic workspace migration to these six products. Flashcards, mind maps, chat history and review state need separate checks. [NoteGPT export guidance](https://help.notegpt.io/how-to-use-notegpt)

Import original sources where practical. Feeding a compressed summary into another summarizer can remove qualifications without showing what was lost. If you migrate corrected notes, preserve their connection to the original.

When a PDF needs OCR, page editing or markup rather than summarization, that is a separate software decision. The [UPDF alternatives guide](/alternatives/updf-alternatives/) compares those document workflows.

Before cancelling, open the exported material outside NoteGPT and confirm it remains readable. Verify your replacement's limits with the typical lecture or document, not only a short demonstration.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a free NoteGPT alternative?

Several options have free starting points, including Gemini Notebook, Glasp, Recall, Scholarcy, Knowt and ScreenApp. Their allowances cover different actions. A free note-writing feature is not the same as unlimited transcription or AI summaries.

### Can Gemini Notebook summarize any YouTube video?

Google's documented import requires a public video with captions and uses its transcript. It does not import the entire visual video. Check whether the spoken content contains the information you need.

### Which alternative fits research papers?

Scholarcy is the paper-focused choice in this shortlist. Gemini Notebook is also worth evaluating for a collection of sources. Read the original methods and limitations before relying on a generated conclusion.

### Which option supports later study practice?

Knowt focuses on cards and study modes. Recall's paid Plus plan includes quizzes and spaced repetition. Choose after checking the content and review process, rather than assuming generated questions are correct.

### Are Glasp highlights private on Free?

Its official pricing page says highlights are public by default and private highlights require paid access. Check visibility settings before saving personal or restricted material.

### Can I import my whole NoteGPT workspace?

A complete direct migration to these products was not verified. Start with documented content or transcript export and inspect each destination's import formats. Do not assume review history or generated study objects transfer.

## Make one source pass the comparison

Choose the alternative matching your main input and the output you actually revisit. Use one source you understand to check a number, an exception and a conclusion, then inspect its export and plan limits. Keep NoteGPT if it already handles that process well. A switch is useful when the replacement improves the complete learning workflow, not merely the appearance of its first summary.
