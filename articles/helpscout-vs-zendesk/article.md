# Help Scout vs Zendesk: Pricing and Features

## Quick verdict

Choose Help Scout if your support team is small or midsize, most work starts in email, and you want a shared inbox, Docs knowledge base, and Beacon without managing a large ticketing system. Choose Zendesk if you need a more structured service operation with deeper routing, more channels, advanced ticket workflows, and room for complex support teams.

Zendesk's Support Team plan starts below Help Scout Standard, but it is not the same feature bundle as Zendesk Suite Team. Support Team is focused on email ticketing and core support essentials. Suite Team adds AI agents, a knowledge base, omnichannel routing, messaging, live chat, and telephony. Compare the Suite price with Help Scout if those channels matter.

## Pricing: compare the right plans

Help Scout lists Free, Standard at $25, Plus at $45, and Pro at $75 per user/month. Free supports five users, one inbox, one Docs site, and 100 contacts per month. Standard supports up to 25 users, two inboxes, two Docs sites, 150 basic workflows, and one basic SLA. Plus supports up to 50 users, five inboxes, three Docs sites, 500 advanced workflows, two advanced SLAs, and round-robin routing. Pro has a ten-seat minimum, ten inboxes, five Docs sites, unlimited advanced workflows and SLAs, and balanced routing.

Zendesk lists Support Team at $19 per agent/month paid yearly, Suite Team at $55, and Suite Professional at $115. The Support Team plan includes email and ticketing, ticket routing, prebuilt analytics, pre-written responses, customer context, and automations. Suite Team adds AI agents, a knowledge base, omnichannel routing, messaging, live chat, and telephony. Suite Professional adds skills-based routing and an IVR phone tree.

The practical comparison depends on your channel needs. If email and a knowledge base are enough, Help Scout's Standard plan is a simple starting point. If you need messaging, live chat, telephony, and more formal ticket routing, compare it with Zendesk Suite Team rather than the lower Support Team price. If you need advanced routing and operations controls, Suite Professional is the more relevant Zendesk tier.

## Inbox and ticket workflow

Help Scout keeps the customer conversation at the centre of the screen. Agents can add an internal note, assign the conversation, apply a tag, use a saved reply, or create a workflow that handles a repeated task. Customer profiles and company information help an agent see earlier interactions without switching between several records.

Zendesk is designed for a more formal ticket operation. The pricing page describes ticket routing, prebuilt analytics, automations, triggers, and customer context on the entry Support Team plan. Suite Team adds omnichannel routing, messaging, live chat, and telephony. This is a stronger structure for teams that need to route different types of work across channels and departments.

The choice is not that one product has automation and the other does not. Both support automation. The difference is how much structure the team needs and which features are included at the plan level. A team that only needs a shared inbox may find Zendesk too much to configure. A team with several queues, formal escalation paths, and a large agent group may find Help Scout too limited.

## Knowledge base and self-service

Help Scout includes Docs, its knowledge base product, and Beacon, which can bring support content and contact options into a website or app. Every plan includes a Docs site, although the number of sites and article limits vary by tier. Beacon can make it easier for a customer to find an answer before opening a conversation.

Zendesk Suite Team includes a knowledge base. Its service platform also supports messaging, live chat, and telephony. The current pricing page positions Suite Team as the plan for teams that want to unify support channels and automate resolutions with AI agents.

If your help center is already well organised, compare how each product handles article structure, search, custom domains, permissions, and content migration. Do not assume that moving tickets automatically moves the knowledge base. Help Scout's standard import guide specifically says that knowledge base and help center articles are not part of the standard ticket import.

## AI and usage costs

Help Scout's pricing table lists AI Assist on paid plans. AI Drafts and AI Summarize are listed for Plus and Pro. Envoy is a separate AI-agent suite, with Scout as the built-in agent, and the public product page lists $0.75 per resolution. Help Scout also lists three months of free resolutions for a new account. The Envoy page marks some capabilities, including email handling and learning from past conversations, as coming soon.

Zendesk includes AI agents in its Suite plans and lists a set of automated resolutions that varies by plan and agent count. The public pricing page shows five automated resolutions per agent/month for Support Team and Suite Team, and ten for Suite Professional, within its listed allowance. Additional AI capabilities and add-ons may affect the total.

For either product, estimate actual volume before buying. A per-seat price does not include every AI or channel cost. Track the number of agent seats, AI resolutions, messages, and channels you expect to use.

## Migration and data portability

Help Scout's import guide lists Zendesk as a supported source. It can import emails or tickets with attached customer profiles and tags. The standard import does not bring over users, inbox settings, knowledge base articles, saved replies, macros, ticket rules, automations, custom fields, ticket types, or custom statuses. Help Scout says the Docs API can support a custom knowledge-base transfer and the Inbox API can support other custom imports.

Help Scout's export guide says selected reports can be exported as CSV or XLSX, but those report exports exclude conversation thread contents. Other customer, user, and conversation data is available through the Inbox API, while Docs articles are available through the Docs API. A customer-list CSV can be requested, but it contains names and email addresses rather than conversation history.

Before switching either direction, map the records and fields that matter. Run a sample import, check attachments and customer identity, recreate unsupported workflows, and decide when email forwarding will move. Keep the old account available until the new team has checked a sample of migrated conversations.

## Which platform should you choose?

Choose **Help Scout** when:
- Email is the main support channel.
- Agents need a shared view of conversations and company context.
- You want Docs and Beacon in the same product family.
- Your team can work with plan-based workflow and routing limits.
- You value a free entry plan for a small team.

Choose **Zendesk** when:
- You need a more formal ticket queue across several channels.
- You need deeper routing, skills, or IVR features.
- You have a larger support team with complex reporting and administration needs.
- You are prepared to configure a more structured service operation.

If you need a traditional ticketing tool but Zendesk Suite feels too large, compare [Freshdesk vs Help Scout](/compare/freshdesk-vs-help-scout/). If the main issue is collaboration around shared email, also review [Front's pricing](https://front.com/pricing) before moving to a full ticketing suite.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Help Scout cheaper than Zendesk?

Help Scout has a free plan and Standard is listed at $25 per user/month. Zendesk Support Team is listed at $19 per agent/month paid yearly, while Suite Team is $55. The lower Zendesk price is for a more limited plan, so compare the features you need rather than only the starting price.

### Is Zendesk better for large support teams?

Zendesk is the stronger shortlist option when a team needs advanced routing, multiple channels, formal ticket workflows, and more detailed service operations. The right plan depends on the channels and routing features required.

### Does Help Scout include a knowledge base?

Yes. Help Scout Docs is included in every plan, with site and article limits that vary by tier. Zendesk includes a knowledge base in Suite Team and higher plans.

### Can I migrate Zendesk tickets to Help Scout?

Help Scout lists Zendesk as a supported import source. The standard import can move tickets or conversations with customer profiles and tags, but not users, inbox settings, help articles, macros, automations, custom fields, ticket types, or custom statuses.

### Which is better for a small email support team?

Help Scout is often the simpler fit when email and self-service content are the main needs. Zendesk may be the better choice when the team already needs formal ticket routing or expects to add more channels soon.
