# Manychat Pricing: Plans, Limits and Fees

Manychat has a Free plan and four paid plans: Essential, Pro, Business and Advanced. The main pricing factor is the number of Active Contacts who engage through Manychat in a billing month, along with the channels, team seats and automation features your business needs.

## What is included in the Free plan?

Free costs US$0 and includes 25 Active Contacts per month, one user, one Inbox seat and up to four live automations. You can connect two channels from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Messenger and Telegram. The plan includes a basic automation builder and templates, but a first message can show Manychat branding.

Free does not include WhatsApp, SMS or Email. It is useful for learning the builder or testing a small Instagram flow. It is not a safe choice for a campaign that may generate more than 25 engaged contacts in a month. See the [Free plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742189-free-plan), checked on November 23, 2026.

## Essential: small audiences and unlimited automations

Essential costs US$17 per month or US$14 per month when billed yearly. The annual option is US$168 paid for the year. It includes 250 Active Contacts, two users and one Inbox seat. You can connect two channels from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Messenger and Telegram.

Essential removes the Free plan's four-live-automation limit and allows unlimited automations. It also adds lead capture, tags and email support. It does not add WhatsApp, SMS or Email channels. Manychat lists an extra-contact rate of US$0.10 on monthly billing or US$0.08 on annual billing. Details were checked on November 23, 2026 in the [Essential plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742191-essential-plan).

## Pro: more channels, AI and broadcasts

Pro costs US$39 per month or US$29 per month with annual billing, paid as US$348 for the year. It includes 2,500 Active Contacts, three users and two Inbox seats. The account can connect up to three eligible channels, including WhatsApp, SMS and Email.

Pro adds advanced automations, broadcasts, Manychat AI features, Inbox labels and rules, and API access. It is the first plan to consider if WhatsApp is a key channel. Manychat lists an additional Inbox seat at US$25. Extra Active Contacts are US$0.05 on monthly billing or US$0.038 on annual billing. Check the [Pro plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742197-pro-plan), checked on November 23, 2026.

## Business and Advanced: team operations and higher volume

Business costs US$99 per month or US$69 per month billed yearly, with an annual total of US$828. It includes 7,500 Active Contacts, five users and three Inbox seats. You can connect all eligible channels. The plan adds team Inbox controls and lead routing. Extra Active Contacts are US$0.025 monthly or US$0.018 annually. The [Business plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742201-business-plan) was checked on November 23, 2026.

Advanced costs US$199 per month or US$139 per month billed yearly, with an annual total of US$1,668. It includes 25,000 Active Contacts, ten users and five Inbox seats, with room to add more seats. It is aimed at larger teams that need high-volume messaging and API-based workflows. The [Advanced plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742209-advanced-plan) was checked on November 23, 2026.

Manychat's pricing page shows an Advanced extra-contact rate, while its Active Contacts help article describes that rate as custom. Because those official pages do not fully agree, confirm the exact Advanced overage in your account before budgeting it. Do not rely on a third-party estimate.

## How Active Contacts affect the bill

An Active Contact is someone who interacts with your business through Manychat during the current billing month. A contact counts when messages are sent or received through automations, AI, broadcasts or the Inbox. The same person counts once for the month, regardless of the number of messages.

The count resets at the start of each billing cycle, including on an annual subscription. Paid plans can charge for contacts above the included allowance. Manychat also lets an account set an overage threshold. Review the [Active Contacts guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742213-active-contacts), checked on November 23, 2026, before a promotion or viral post increases message volume.

## WhatsApp message fees are separate

The subscription price is not the full WhatsApp cost. Manychat charges for delivered template messages based on the message category and the recipient's market. Since October 1, 2026, service messages delivered during an open customer service window are also chargeable after the monthly allowance.

Each WhatsApp Business phone number includes 1,000 free service messages per calendar month. Messages sent by end users to the business are free. The allowance resets monthly and does not roll over. The exact rate depends on the recipient's country and the template type. Read the [Manychat WhatsApp pricing guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742132-whatsapp-pricing-guide), checked on November 23, 2026, before estimating a broadcast budget.

## Which plan should you choose?

- Choose Free to learn the builder or test a small flow with no more than 25 Active Contacts.
- Choose Essential if you need more Instagram or TikTok automation but do not need WhatsApp, SMS or Email.
- Choose Pro if you need WhatsApp, more Active Contacts, AI features or API access.
- Choose Business if several people share the Inbox and need lead routing across channels.
- Choose Advanced when the 7,500-contact Business allowance or its team capacity is too small.

Do not upgrade only because a plan has more features. Estimate monthly Active Contacts, channel count, team users, Inbox seats and WhatsApp message volume first. Then compare the annual commitment with the monthly price.

## Common pricing questions

### Is Manychat free?

Yes. Free costs US$0 and includes 25 Active Contacts per month, two eligible channels, one user and up to four live automations. Checked on November 23, 2026 on the [Free plan guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742189-free-plan).

### Does Manychat charge for WhatsApp messages?

Yes. Template messages are charged by category and destination market. Since October 1, 2026, service messages are also charged after the first 1,000 free service messages per WhatsApp Business phone number each month. See the [official WhatsApp pricing guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742132-whatsapp-pricing-guide).

### Does an annual plan include a yearly Active Contact allowance?

No. Active Contacts are counted each billing month. The allowance resets monthly even when the subscription is billed annually. See the [Active Contacts guide](https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17742213-active-contacts).
