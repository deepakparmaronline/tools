# 5 Pearl Alternatives for Dental Imaging Workflows

If you need a Pearl alternative for dental X-ray analysis, start with Overjet and Videa for chairside imaging workflows, Denti.AI for imaging bundled with clinical documentation, and Diagnocat for practices evaluating 2D and CBCT reporting together. Align X-ray Insights is another imaging candidate, subject to regional availability. The right choice depends on your existing imaging software, the exact module available in your country, and how clinicians review its output. This comparison concerns Pearl dental AI at hellopearl.com, especially Second Opinion. It does not rank diagnostic accuracy or recommend patient treatment.

## Compare the workflow before the brand

Pearl sells several products. Replacing Second Opinion's radiograph annotations is a different purchase from replacing Practice Intelligence or a revenue-cycle module. Make the scope of your current Pearl contract the first line in every competing quote.

## When keeping Pearl makes sense

[Second Opinion](https://hellopearl.com/products/second-opinion) provides radiograph overlays, measurements, patient-facing reports, and image-quality checks. Pearl directs buyers to confirm compatibility with their specific software. A familiar product that already works in your operatories can be preferable to retraining staff and changing the review process.

Keep Pearl on the shortlist if the integration is dependable, clinicians understand the annotations, and your contracted modules cover the work you need. If the gap is reporting or training, first ask whether a configuration change or a different Pearl module addresses it. Pearl's [product directory](https://hellopearl.com/products) includes offerings beyond Second Opinion; a competitor's larger bundle is not automatically a better replacement.

Consider switching when a vendor can demonstrate the exact integration you are missing, a relevant reporting workflow, or a clearer total cost. Treat these as purchasing criteria, not evidence that Pearl performs poorly. We could not verify a current public Pearl price, so this article does not claim any candidate is cheaper than your contract.

## 1. Overjet for a broader imaging and operations shortlist

[Overjet](https://www.overjet.com/) presents Vision AI for radiograph analysis and patient explanations, alongside separate offerings such as IRIS AI imaging and revenue-cycle tools. It is a relevant Pearl candidate when your purchasing project includes both clinical imaging and operational work.

For example, a group comparing chairside overlays and insurance workflows can ask Overjet for a proposal showing those modules separately. That makes it easier to compare the imaging component with Pearl Second Opinion and the administrative component with the corresponding Pearl product.

**Pricing:** Public production pricing was not verified. Ask for a quote specifying locations, modules, onboarding, support, and contract duration. A demo or invitation to try the software does not establish an ongoing free plan.

**Tradeoff:** A broader platform creates more procurement decisions. Overjet's [integration guidance](https://www.overjet.com/blog/guide-to-dental-software-integrations) is a useful starting point, but the vendor should demonstrate your exact systems and versions. Skip a suite-level purchase if all you need is a narrow imaging function and the proposal requires unwanted components.

## 2. Videa Clinical Assist for integration-led purchasing

[Videa Clinical Assist](https://videa.ai/platform/clinical-assist), from the company previously branded VideaHealth, analyzes dental radiographs and presents annotated findings. Its official workflow emphasizes connecting to existing practice management and imaging systems. This makes it a sensible candidate when staying inside the staff's usual software matters more than adding another independent dashboard.

The [Videa Learning Center](https://help.videa.ai/) also documents Dentrix and Ascend Detect AI products powered by Videa. Ask your existing software provider whether an integrated offering is available under your current agreement before buying a separate subscription.

**Pricing:** No current public price was verified. Request a quote for the named product and delivery route, because an embedded partner offering and a direct platform purchase should not be assumed to have identical terms.

**Tradeoff:** Patient education views and regulated detection functions are different. Videa explicitly states that Patient View is for education and is not FDA-cleared. Require a demonstration of the clinician review view as well as the presentation screen. Skip a recommendation based only on attractive patient visuals if the review and documentation workflow remains unclear.

## 3. Denti.AI for imaging bundled with documentation

[Denti.AI](https://www.denti.ai/) combines imaging-related functions with voice periodontal charting, clinical notes, and AutoChart. It suits practices evaluating several documentation tasks together. A practice that already pays separately for note drafting and charting should compare the entire proposed bundle with its current stack.

**Pricing:** The [OneStop Clinical pricing page](https://www.denti.ai/pricing) displays $333 per month as an annual equivalent, billed at **$3,990 per year**, alongside a $399 monthly rate. The annual invoice is the relevant commitment. It includes up to seven installs at one location and unlimited users. Imaging and AutoChart depend on the supported PMS; fair use applies. Multiple locations require a tailored discussion.

**Tradeoff:** Unlimited users does not mean unlimited locations, unrestricted usage, or universal software compatibility. Skip the bundle if your practice only wants radiograph review and would not use its documentation tools.

The [FDA database entry K230144](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K230144) identifies Denti.AI Detect specifically. That record should not be generalized to every feature sold under the Denti.AI brand.

## 4. Diagnocat for 2D and CBCT reporting evaluation

[Diagnocat](https://diagnocat.com/en) describes analysis and structured reports for intraoral, panoramic, and CBCT images, plus related 3D reporting functions. It is a relevant candidate when the purchasing problem includes more than chairside 2D overlays.

A practice handling referrals could ask the vendor to demonstrate how a report is reviewed, corrected, saved, and shared in its existing referral process. That is a concrete comparison with Pearl rather than a contest over the number of conditions advertised.

**Pricing:** A usable numeric price was not verified. The [FAQ](https://diagnocat.com/en/faq) describes monthly and annual subscriptions, with service and report allowances affecting the purchase. It also says unused reports do not transfer to the next subscription period. Obtain the actual allowance, renewal, retention, and overage terms in writing.

**Tradeoff:** Diagnocat explicitly says availability and regulatory status vary by region. Its global page is not proof that every advertised module can be used in your country. Skip it if local eligibility or integration cannot be confirmed. CBCT capability also adds little value to a practice whose purchasing scope only covers routine 2D review.

## 5. Align X-ray Insights as a regional imaging candidate

[Align X-ray Insights](https://www.alignxrayinsights.com/) is the current destination of the dentalXrai website. The product describes radiograph analysis with colored annotations and an interactive tooth chart. It is worth investigating for practices seeking an imaging-specific comparison rather than a larger clinical and administrative bundle.

The site identifies automatic patient-data retrieval as a Pro-plan feature. Include this distinction in a demo: importing an image manually and having the right patient appear automatically are different workflows.

**Pricing:** A free trial is advertised, but the [pricing page](https://www.alignxrayinsights.com/pricing) returned a notice about expanding into more regions rather than a usable price offer during research. We therefore cannot verify a starting price, trial allowance, or local purchase availability.

**Tradeoff:** This remains a conditional shortlist entry. Confirm that the vendor can sell and support the relevant module in your region before investing time in a technical evaluation. Skip it when eligibility is unresolved; the official landing page alone is not enough to approve a purchase.

## Build a comparable dental AI quote

Price comparisons become misleading when one proposal covers a single imaging module and another includes charting, notes, analytics, or revenue-cycle work. Give each vendor the same written specification.

- **Scope:** Name the radiograph types, clinician-review functions, reporting needs, and optional modules you actually require.
- **Installation:** List practice locations, operatories, users, software versions, and whether the proposal includes integration work.
- **Usage:** Ask what counts as an analysis, report, reprocessed image, or fair-use event. Obtain overage rules and the treatment of unused allowances.
- **Commitment:** Compare annual invoices, month-to-month terms, renewal increases, cancellation windows, and implementation charges.
- **Support:** Identify who fixes connector failures: the AI vendor, imaging provider, PMS provider, or your IT contractor.

Separate essential requirements from convenient extras. A missing mandatory integration should eliminate an option even if its dashboard looks better. A bundle that adds three unused modules should not win solely because its feature list is longer.

## Evaluate integrations and regulatory scope carefully

A supported brand logo is not a complete compatibility statement. Ask vendors to demonstrate the same software edition and version your practice uses, including the route from image capture to analysis to documented clinician review. Check whether reports return to the patient record or require a separate export.

For US purchasing, request the relevant clearance documentation and intended-use labeling for the particular device. The [FDA explains](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/how-study-and-market-your-device) that establishment registration and device listing do not themselves mean clearance or approval. For other countries, obtain the applicable local documentation instead of treating an FDA reference as worldwide authorization.

Ask how the clinician accepts, dismisses, or corrects an annotation and how those actions appear in the record. Have qualified clinical staff assess suitability within the documented intended use. This procurement article cannot establish that one system detects disease more accurately than another, and percentages from different vendor studies are not a controlled comparison.

## Plan the exit before signing

Use a vendor-approved demonstration with appropriate data handling, then document the workflow you are replacing. Keep a record of the current configuration and assign responsibility for staff training, permissions, and connector setup.

Before switching, ask both vendors what can be exported: original images, annotated images, reports, audit information, and any saved measurements. Do not assume a PDF preserves editable annotations or that a new vendor can import the old vendor's analysis. Ask about access after cancellation and whether archived reports remain in your own patient-record system.

Have the practice's responsible privacy and IT staff review the proposed processing arrangements, access controls, retention terms, and any required agreements. A marketing compliance statement should not substitute for reviewing the actual contract. Define a rollback point if the integration fails, and avoid cancelling the existing service before the replacement workflow is accepted.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest Pearl Second Opinion alternative?

Overjet and Videa belong on an initial imaging shortlist. Which is closer depends on the specific integration, review workflow, and modules in your Pearl agreement. Denti.AI is also relevant when charting and documentation are part of the purchase.

### Is there a free Pearl dental AI alternative?

We did not verify a permanent free production plan among these candidates. A demonstration, sample-image tool, or trial is not the same as a licensed service for ongoing practice use. Request the trial's limits and permitted use before relying on it.

### Which option is useful for CBCT reporting?

Diagnocat explicitly describes CBCT reporting, making it a candidate for that requirement. Confirm the local availability and permitted scope of the module. Pearl also lists a separate 3D offering, so compare the relevant 3D products rather than assuming Second Opinion represents every Pearl capability.

### Can a PMS replace Pearl without separate AI software?

A practice management system may offer an integrated AI product from a specialist vendor. Videa's support material documents Dentrix and Ascend Detect AI. Ask your provider which product powers the feature, what license is needed, and which functions the integration exposes.

### Does FDA clearance mean every module is cleared?

No. Review the named device and its intended use. Denti.AI Detect's database entry is product-specific, and Videa separately identifies Patient View as an educational feature that is not FDA-cleared. Verify the relevant documentation for each proposed function and jurisdiction.

## Choose the shortlist that matches the purchase

For an imaging-first comparison, request demonstrations from Overjet and Videa against your current Pearl workflow. Add Denti.AI when notes and charting are part of the requirement, and Diagnocat when structured 2D and CBCT reporting matters. Treat Align X-ray Insights as a candidate only after regional availability is confirmed.

Keep Pearl if it meets those requirements at an acceptable total cost. The next useful step is a comparable written quote and a demonstrated integration, followed by clinical and IT review. A longer list of advertised features is not enough reason to switch.
