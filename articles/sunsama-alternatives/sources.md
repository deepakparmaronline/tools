## Research limits and conflicts

Sunsama is not exclusively manual: account documentation includes auto-scheduling and auto-rescheduling settings. It supports Apple accounts. CSV/JSON export is verified for the primary workspace, but direct imports of all fields into the alternatives are not. Ellie pricing advertises both billing selectors; the displayed $9.99 monthly offer is used, annual price omitted. Motion selected billing/team state and current Reclaim paid terms remain qualified.
