## Evidence handling

Reviewed current CallPage alternatives results from Tomba, Prospeo, Novocall and SpotSaaS. Several lists conflate lead-data enrichment with callback replacement, use different price vintages or recommend LiveCall independently. Official LiveCall now routes to CallPage; Convolo redirects to Brightcall. Those findings changed the shortlist. Vendor comparisons were discovery only. Search: https://www.google.com/search?q=CallPage+alternatives

LiveCall is excluded because its official domain directs to CallPage. Novocall's current homepage/pricing could not be verified and is not recommended from old comparison posts. Convolo redirects to Brightcall; use the current Speed to Lead product and do not reuse old Convolo prices. Leadoo's pricing page was observed but subsequently returned HTTP 500; no exact current base price or callback bundle entitlement is claimed. CloudTalk showed euro prices with a country selector, so no universal USD rate is used. Callback24's global and Polish plans differ; free global limits are quoted but final paid regional rates require confirmation. Callback Tracker's US/Canada package is not generalized to India or other supported international destinations. CallPage price cards and table expose different billing figures, so no unqualified annual/monthly conversion is made. No universal import, number portability or compliance guarantee is claimed.
