## Product identity and scope

Manychat is a social media and messaging automation platform for creators, brands and teams. The current product supports Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, WhatsApp, SMS and Email, with channel access controlled by plan and region. It should not be described as a full help desk or ticketing system.

The distinctive documented workflow is Instagram comment-to-DM automation. A business can choose a specific post or Reel, all posts and Reels, or the next post or Reel. It can use included or excluded keywords, or any comment, then add a public reply and a private reply flow. The official guide documents restrictions on the first private reply node: https://help.manychat.com/en/articles/17741897-instagram-post-and-reel-comments-trigger

## Pricing conflicts and safeguards

- The Advanced plan guide lists extra-contact rates of US$0.004 monthly and US$0.0028 annual. The Active Contacts article describes Advanced overage pricing as custom. The pricing draft flags this conflict and does not publish an Advanced overage amount.
- Plan availability varies by region. Paid accounts created before March 2, 2026 are being moved to the new plan model gradually. The current pricing page is the reference; older accounts may see different eligibility.
- The USD pricing page says prices do not include tax and that the billing country determines the price shown before payment. Do not convert it to INR or imply every country pays the same amount.
- WhatsApp usage fees are separate from the subscription. The destination market and template category determine message charges. Service message billing changed on October 1, 2026.
- Export documentation describes Data Collection CSV, Google Sheets actions and External Requests rather than a single one-click full-account export. Do not claim a complete one-step migration.
- Email and SMS imports require certain paid plans, support approval and consent. Telegram imports require Pro, a Telegram ID and a prior subscription to the same bot.
- Do not claim that the platform guarantees sales, follower growth, inbox response times or automation results.

Verified price and scope notes:

- Chatfuel Business S is US$18 per month billed yearly and includes US$20 of AI credits per month. Its official page lists Reels and post comment auto-replies, WhatsApp campaigns, lead qualification and meeting booking. A free trial is shown; the reviewed plan list does not show a permanent free tier.
- WATI Growth is US$59 per month billed annually with three users and a seven-day trial. Template message charges are additional. Its plan page lists WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Webchat, broadcasts, automations and team Inbox features. Pro is US$119 per month billed annually with five included users.
- Interakt lists Starter at US$12 per month for Instagram only and Growth at US$55 per month for WhatsApp and Instagram. The Starter price is marked for Indian destination numbers, so it must be labelled as a regional price. WhatsApp template message costs vary by destination market.
- respond.io lists Starter at US$99 monthly or US$79 per month billed yearly, with a seven-day trial. Growth adds broadcasts, workflows, AI agents, advanced reports, Zapier and Make integrations, and developer API.
- SleekFlow lists Essential at US$49 per month, Pro at US$109 and Premium at US$279. Essential starts with 500 monthly active contacts and three seats. Pro adds AI agents, broadcasts, Flow Builder and API access. The page says Essential availability is rolling out by country.
- Tidio has a Free plan with 50 human conversations and 100 Flow visitors per month, plus a one-time 50 Lyro AI conversation quota. Starter is US$33 per month billed annually with 50 AI and human conversations. Growth starts at US$63 per month billed annually with 100 conversations. Its live chat, ticketing and social channels make it a partial support alternative, not a like-for-like comment-to-DM replacement.
