# Help Scout: Shared Inbox and Support Guide

## Quick answer

Help Scout is a customer support platform built around a shared inbox, a knowledge base called Docs, and the Beacon help widget. It suits small and midsize teams that want customer conversations to feel like email while giving agents shared ownership, saved replies, workflows, customer context, and self-service content.

It is less suitable when a support operation depends on deep ticket customization, several brands, complex routing, or a large set of imported macros and rules. Help Scout's built-in migration tool can bring conversations, customer profiles, and tags from supported systems, but it does not move every part of an old help desk.

## What Help Scout does

**Inbox** brings email, live chat, text, social conversations, and customer context into a shared workspace. Teammates can assign a conversation, add an internal note, use a saved reply, apply a tag, or create a workflow that handles a repeatable task. Views help agents focus on waiting, assigned, overdue, or tagged conversations.

**Docs** is the knowledge base. Teams can publish help articles in collections and categories, then use those articles to answer common questions before a customer contacts support. A Docs site is included in every plan, but the number of sites and articles depends on the plan.

**Beacon** adds a support entry point to a website or product. Depending on the configuration, it can surface Docs content, offer live chat, and collect a message. It is useful when a customer should be able to search for an answer without leaving the page.

**Workflows and SLAs** help teams manage repeatable work. Workflows can tag, assign, change status, or notify teammates when conditions are met. The number and type of workflows and SLA policies increase with the paid plan.

**AI tools** need to be separated by product. The pricing table lists AI Assist on paid plans and AI Drafts and AI Summarize on Plus and Pro. Envoy is the separate AI-agent suite, with Scout as its built-in agent. Envoy is listed at $0.75 per resolution and includes three months of free resolutions for a new account. Some capabilities on the Envoy page, including email handling and learning from past conversations, are still labelled as coming soon, so do not budget for them as live features.

## Who should consider it?

Help Scout is a sensible shortlist option for a SaaS company, professional service firm, education team, or small support department that mainly handles email and needs a shared record of customer conversations. It can also suit a team that wants a knowledge base and an on-site support widget without buying a separate product for each job.

It is a weaker fit for a large operation that needs detailed ticket types, many custom statuses, strict skill-based routing, multiple brands, or extensive automation imported from another platform. Those teams should compare Zendesk, Freshdesk, and other full help desk products before moving.

A product update dated October 1, 2026, describes a redesigned conversation sidebar that brings status, assignee, SLA, topics, sentiment, company information, history, custom fields, and apps into one place. This is useful context when evaluating the current interface, but it does not remove the migration limits listed in Help Scout's import guide.

## Common questions

### Is Help Scout a help desk or a shared inbox?

It is both a shared inbox and a customer support platform. The Inbox is designed around conversations, while workflows, SLAs, Docs, Beacon, and customer profiles add help desk and self-service features.

### Does Help Scout have a free plan?

Yes. The current pricing page lists a free plan for up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site, and 100 contacts per month. Check the plan table for the limits on saved replies, tags, articles, and reporting history.

### Can Help Scout import tickets from another help desk?

The standard import tool supports several providers, including Freshdesk, Zendesk, Intercom, Gorgias, HubSpot Service Hub, and Zoho Desk. It imports conversations or tickets with customer profiles and tags, but not every setting or content type.

### Is Envoy included in the Help Scout seat price?

No. Envoy is a separate AI-agent product priced at $0.75 per resolution. Help Scout lists three months of free resolutions for a new account; confirm the current offer and usage rules before rollout.
