# Help Scout pricing

Canonical: https://toolboxkart.tech/helpscout/pricing/

**Short answer:** Help Scout lists a Free plan and three paid plans. The current pricing page displays Standard at US$25, Plus at US$45 and Pro at US$75 per user/month. It also has a monthly and annual billing selector. Budget for seats, extra inboxes, extra Docs sites and Envoy resolutions, not only the first price shown.

These are the prices and limits displayed on the official Help Scout pricing page checked November 11, 2026. The page has a monthly and annual selector and shows an annual saving marker. The displayed amount can change when the billing mode changes. Confirm the selected mode, currency, tax and minimum seat count before approving a budget. Do not compare a yearly-billed competitor price with a monthly Help Scout quote without making the billing periods clear.

## What the Free plan includes

Free is useful for a small team that wants to try a shared inbox, but its contact allowance matters. The plan lists up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. It also has a small allowance for articles, tags and saved replies. The 100-contact limit is not the same as 100 messages. A customer who sends several messages is still one contact for the plan limit. If the team reaches the monthly allowance, the Free plan may stop replies until the next cycle or until the account upgrades.

Before you move a public support address to the Free plan, check the last three months of unique contacts. If your monthly count is already close to 100, use a paid plan estimate. Also check whether one Docs site is enough for your help content and whether all five users need agent access. A free plan can be a good pilot, but it is not a safe long-term budget if the contact count is growing.

## Extra costs to include

- **Additional inboxes:** the pricing page lists an extra inbox at US$10 per month on paid plans. The final amount depends on billing mode.
- **Additional Docs sites:** the page lists an extra Docs site at US$20 per month. Check whether a separate site is needed or whether one site with clear collections is enough.
- **Envoy AI agent:** the public page displays US$0.75 per resolution. A resolution is not a seat, a contact or a message. Estimate the expected number of resolved conversations and confirm how Help Scout counts them.
- **Seat growth:** paid plans are priced per user. A team that adds agents, temporary support staff or managers should include those seats in the forecast.
- **Channels and integrations:** SMS, WhatsApp, social channels and connected apps can have plan or provider requirements. Confirm the exact channel and any third-party charge before launch.

## Which plan should you choose?

### Choose Free for a controlled pilot

Free is a reasonable way to learn the Inbox and Docs model when the team has fewer than five users and comfortably stays under 100 contacts a month. Keep the pilot narrow. Use one inbox, one Docs site and a few saved replies. If the support address receives more than 100 unique contacts, do not treat Free as the production plan.

### Choose Standard for a simple support team

Standard is the first paid step for a team that needs more contacts, another inbox and a basic SLA. It has a 25-user limit and two inboxes in the public table. If the team has several departments, check whether two inboxes are enough before choosing this plan. A third inbox adds a separate monthly charge.

### Choose Plus when routing and service rules matter

Plus raises the user and inbox limits and adds advanced workflows, more SLA capacity, round-robin routing and a secure customer portal. It is worth comparing when the team has a queue that needs more deliberate ownership or a customer-facing portal. Do not pay for the tier only because the feature list is longer. Identify the workflow that will use the extra controls.

### Choose Pro for administration and scale

Pro lists a 10-user minimum, SSO/SAML, HIPAA support, more inboxes and higher workflow and SLA limits. A small team may not need it. A larger team should still check whether the plan's security and reporting features meet its own compliance requirements. A vendor feature label is not a substitute for a formal security review.

## A simple seat-cost check

Use the displayed per-user monthly rates as a starting point, not as an invoice quote. Ten Standard seats at the displayed rate create a US$250 monthly equivalent before taxes and add-ons. Ten Plus seats create US$450, and ten Pro seats create US$750. The actual invoice depends on the billing selector, seat count, minimums, extra inboxes, Docs sites and AI use. Write down each item before comparing Help Scout with another help desk.

For a fair comparison, use the same assumptions for every vendor: active agents, light users, monthly contacts, ticket volume, AI resolutions, help-centre sites, channels and reporting needs. A product with a lower seat price may cost more once AI usage, messaging and implementation are added.

## Ways to control the bill

- Review the number of inboxes before creating another department queue. Use tags and saved views when ownership does not need a separate inbox.
- Keep one Docs site unless the team has a real need for a separate brand, language or permission boundary.
- Give agent seats only to people who need to reply or manage conversations. Confirm whether a light-user option fits other roles.
- Measure Envoy resolutions with a small pilot before you enable it across every channel.
- Check the annual selector and renewal terms before accepting a discount. A lower monthly equivalent can require a longer commitment.

## Common pricing questions

### Does Help Scout have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan lists up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site and 100 contacts per month. It has smaller article, tag and saved-reply allowances than paid plans.

### How much does Help Scout Standard cost?

The pricing page displayed Standard at US$25 per user/month on November 11, 2026. The page has a monthly and annual selector, so confirm the selected billing mode and currency before purchase.

### How much does Envoy cost?

The public pricing page displays Envoy at US$0.75 per resolution. Confirm the current definition of a resolution and estimate volume before enabling it across all support channels.

### Does Help Scout charge for extra inboxes?

Yes. The pricing page lists an additional inbox at US$10 per month on paid plans. Billing mode and current terms can affect the final amount.
