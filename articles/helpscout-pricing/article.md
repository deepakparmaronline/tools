# Help Scout Pricing: Plans, Limits and Costs

## Quick price summary

Help Scout has a Free plan and three paid plans. The current pricing page displays Standard at $25, Plus at $45, and Pro at $75 per user/month. It also has a Monthly/Annual selector and advertises a 16% saving on annual billing. Treat those figures as the displayed monthly equivalent, then confirm the selected billing term, tax, seat count, and add-ons at checkout.

| Plan | Price shown | Team and inbox limits | Main limits to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Up to 5 users, 1 inbox, 1 Docs site | 100 contacts/month, 10 saved replies, 10 tags, 10 Docs articles, 30-day report history |
| Standard | $25/user/month | Up to 25 users, 2 inboxes, 2 Docs sites | 150 basic workflows and 1 basic SLA |
| Plus | $45/user/month | Up to 50 users, 5 inboxes, 3 Docs sites | 500 advanced workflows, 2 advanced SLAs, round-robin routing, 25 light users |
| Pro | $75/user/month | 10-seat minimum, 10 inboxes, 5 Docs sites | Unlimited advanced workflows and SLAs, balanced routing, 50 light users |

Paid plans list unlimited contacts. The number of Docs sites, inboxes, workflows, SLAs, light users, and routing features changes by tier. The plan table should be checked before purchase because a feature that looks like a core product capability may be limited to a higher plan.

## What the Free plan includes

Free is useful for a very small team that needs one shared support inbox and a basic help center. It supports up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site, and 100 contacts per month. The detailed pricing table also lists ten saved replies, ten tags, ten Docs articles, one Beacon, and 30 days of report history.

The contact allowance is the key limit. A team that receives more than 100 new contacts in a month should not assume it can keep using the Free plan without a change. It also has fewer reporting and automation options than the paid plans. Use it to evaluate the workflow or run a small support operation, not as a promise of unlimited free support.

## Standard: for a small paid team

Standard is the first paid tier. The current pricing page lists $25 per user/month. It supports up to 25 users, two inboxes, two Docs sites, 150 basic workflows, and one basic SLA. It is the sensible starting point when the team has outgrown the Free contact allowance or needs more than one inbox.

Standard can suit a support team with straightforward assignment and tagging rules. Before choosing it, check whether your workflows need advanced conditions or whether you need round-robin assignment. Those needs may push the team to Plus.

## Plus: for more routing and AI assistance

Plus is listed at $45 per user/month. The plan supports up to 50 users, five inboxes, three Docs sites, 500 advanced workflows, two advanced SLAs, round-robin routing, and 25 light users. The pricing table lists AI Assist, AI Drafts, and AI Summarize for Plus.

Choose Plus when a team needs more structured routing, additional inboxes, and more workflow capacity. It is also the first tier to consider if AI drafting and conversation summaries are important to agent productivity. Do not confuse those agent-assist features with Envoy, which is a separate AI-agent product with usage-based pricing.

## Pro: for larger teams

Pro is listed at $75 per user/month and has a ten-seat minimum. It includes ten inboxes, five Docs sites, unlimited advanced workflows and SLAs, balanced routing, and 50 light users. It is aimed at teams that need more administration and workflow capacity than a small support desk.

Pro may be unnecessary if the team has one or two inboxes and simple assignment rules. Compare the additional cost with the actual use of routing, SLA, security, and administration features rather than upgrading because the plan sounds more complete.

## Add-ons and extra costs

| Item | Public price or rule |
| --- | --- |
| Extra inbox | $10/month on annual billing or $12/month on monthly billing |
| Extra Docs site | $20/month |
| Envoy AI agent | $0.75 per resolution |
| Envoy trial | Three months of free resolutions for a new account |
| Additional migration volume | Imports over 500,000 records are $500 per 500,000 records, including the first 500,000 |

The Envoy price is usage-based. Estimate the number of conversations the agent may resolve, then set a monthly cap and monitor the actual resolution count. The product page also says teams can set spending caps and receive a discount when they prepay for resolutions. Confirm the current offer and billing rules before adding Envoy.

## Simple cost examples

A five-person team on Standard has a displayed seat cost of 5 × $25, or $125 per month before tax and add-ons, when using the annual price view. A ten-person team on Plus has a displayed seat cost of 10 × $45, or $450 per month before tax and add-ons. These examples do not include extra inboxes, extra Docs sites, Envoy resolutions, or any negotiated terms.

The cheapest plan is not always the lowest-cost plan for the workflow. If a team needs more than two inboxes or advanced routing, compare the full Plus price with the cost of a different help desk. If the team needs to export a large history, include migration work in the budget as well.

## Which plan should you choose?

- Choose **Free** if you have five or fewer users, one inbox, and fewer than 100 contacts per month.
- Choose **Standard** if you need a paid support workspace with two inboxes and basic workflows.
- Choose **Plus** if you need round-robin routing, more advanced workflows, more SLAs, or AI Drafts and Summarize.
- Choose **Pro** if you need the ten-seat plan, more inboxes, unlimited advanced workflows and SLAs, and the higher administration limits.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Help Scout have a free plan?

Yes. Free supports up to five users, one inbox, one Docs site, and 100 contacts per month. It also has limits on saved replies, tags, Docs articles, Beacon, and report history.

### Are Help Scout prices per user or per inbox?

The main plans are priced per user per month. The pricing page also lists extra inboxes as an add-on, so include both seats and inbox count in your budget.

### Is Envoy included in Standard, Plus, or Pro?

Envoy is a separate AI-agent product priced at $0.75 per resolution. The product page lists three months of free resolutions for a new account. AI Assist, AI Drafts, and AI Summarize are separate plan features.

### What costs extra in Help Scout?

Extra inboxes, extra Docs sites, and Envoy resolutions can add to the plan price. Large imports over 500,000 records also have a published fee. Check the current pricing and import pages before approving a migration.
