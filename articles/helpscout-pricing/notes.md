# Editorial notes — Help Scout pricing

- Prices and plan limits checked on 2026-11-14 from the official pricing page.
- The page has a Monthly/Annual selector and advertises 16% annual savings; article tells readers to confirm the selected billing term.
- Free plan limits include 5 users, 1 inbox, 1 Docs site, 100 contacts/month, 10 saved replies, 10 tags, 10 Docs articles, 1 Beacon, and 30-day report history.
- Envoy is separate at $0.75 per resolution and lists three months of free resolutions for new accounts.
- Extra inbox, extra Docs site, and large-import costs are included.
- No Semrush refresh or hands-on pricing checkout.
