# 6 Survicate Alternatives for Surveys and Customer Feedback

For Survicate alternatives, start with Refiner for contextual SaaS and mobile surveys, or Qualaroo for targeted website and in-app nudges. Typeform suits a deliberate form-based feedback journey; SurveyMonkey and QuestionPro suit broader questionnaires and research work. SurveySparrow is worth evaluating for conversational surveys and a separately scoped customer experience programme. Keep Survicate if its multichannel collection, targeting and feedback analysis already fit your team. Compare the actual population and billing unit: unlimited responses can still come with MAU, pageview or feature limits, while a low survey price may exclude the channel you need.

## Decide where feedback should be collected

An in-app question after an action and a survey link sent to an email list reach different people at different moments. A switch should preserve the collection context you need, rather than simply reproduce the questions in another builder.

These six options cover contextual feedback, intentional form completion and broader survey programmes. The shortlist is based on current official product and pricing information checked on October 14, 2026. Recommendations are editorial inferences; no product trials, completion-rate experiments or customer outcomes are claimed.

## Why staying with Survicate can make sense

[Survicate](https://survicate.com/) presents multichannel surveys alongside feedback analysis and a Research Hub. If your team already has appropriate targeting rules and a clear process for acting on feedback, a switch can disrupt collection continuity without improving the decisions you make.

The [current pricing page](https://survicate.com/pricing/) shows Growth starting at $114 per month billed annually, with a displayed starting scope of 250 survey responses and 1,000 data points per month. Pro and Enterprise begin at displayed $349 and $569 monthly figures respectively and use custom pools/scopes. They are not equivalent to Growth's starting response allowance. The feature comparison also includes Starter, retention limits and plan-specific logic/integration provisions.

The separate response and data-point limits matter when budgeting surveys plus Research Hub use. Confirm each allowance and overage/reset rule rather than counting all feedback activity as the same event. The pricing comparison has several selectable tiers; a maximum shown in a matrix is not necessarily the allowance purchased at a card's starting price.

The page offers a 10-day trial with up to 25 survey responses and 100 Research Hub data points. That trial description is not evidence of an unlimited permanent free plan. Older alternatives pages quote different starting plans and prices; they are not used as proof of today's offer here.

Stay if your existing channels, attribute/event targeting and analysis are useful and the scoped quote is acceptable. Consider alternatives for a concrete change: product-native feedback around usage, a richer research questionnaire, a different commercial unit or a coordinated CX programme. Compare the required Survicate plan before deciding a rival is the only way to get the feature.

## Refiner for contextual SaaS and mobile surveys

[Refiner](https://refiner.io/) focuses on in-app and mobile feedback for product teams. It describes web surveys, native SDKs for iOS/Android and frameworks such as React Native/Flutter, email surveys and shareable pages. Its targeting uses user traits, pages and events, while responses can connect to customer-data and product-analytics tools.

Shortlist it when the important question is about a specific user's experience inside your product. For example, an onboarding team might ask a short effort question after the first completed workflow, while excluding staff and people who already responded. That is a proposed evaluation scenario, not an observed response-rate result.

The [pricing page](https://refiner.io/pricing/) uses monthly active users and says all paid plans include unlimited survey responses. For in-product surveys, its MAU definition generally counts people logged into the app over the last 30 days. For other survey types, it describes unique respondents in a 30-day window. The reviewed dynamic controls did not expose a reliable numeric price, so an old $99 figure is not presented as a verified current quote.

Essentials covers survey features, trait segmentation and basic integrations; Growth adds tracked events, multilingual surveys and deeper data integrations/API/webhooks. Published-survey and environment limits also differ. An unlimited-response claim therefore does not mean unlimited audience, environments or every integration.

The potential benefit over your Survicate setup is the fit of product context and data-stack integration. The tradeoff is paying for a different population. A large application with modest feedback participation can behave very differently under MAU and response billing. Skip Refiner if your main requirement is a verified low-priced mass questionnaire to an external list, or if you have not confirmed which plan supplies the event trigger and destination integration.

## Qualaroo for targeted website and in-app nudges

[Qualaroo](https://qualaroo.com/) presents Nudges for websites, products and mobile apps alongside email/link surveys. Its targeting includes identity, behaviour and custom properties, and its feature descriptions include branching, NPS/CSAT/CES and mobile SDKs. This is an actual contextual-feedback candidate, rather than merely a form link with a different design.

Evaluate it when you want a short question at a relevant point in a visit. A proposed checkout-friction survey, for example, should target the intended visitors without interrupting completed purchases. Test the targeting and suppression rules with both eligible and excluded users, then verify the response reaches the intended follow-up tool.

[Qualaroo pricing](https://qualaroo.com/pricing/) lists Business at $49.99 per month billed annually, $599.88 per year, for 5,000 email sends and 100,000 pageviews per month. It includes unlimited responses and nudges. That is not unlimited traffic or sending capacity. The page also describes Free as up to 50 responses, including 500 email sends and 10,000 pageviews; it does not clearly label those free allowances as a monthly reset, so they are not described as 50 free responses every month here.

The advantage to investigate is a nudge-oriented workflow with response volume treated differently from Survicate. The limitation is the pageview/send scope and integration requirements. Validate how your full site traffic is counted, not only the number of people who see one question.

Skip it if you expect “unlimited responses” to remove all usage planning. Also confirm the exact mobile SDK, authentication and feedback-to-CRM path for your application. Marketing claims about higher participation are not independent completion-rate evidence in this comparison.

## Typeform for an intentional form-based journey

[Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/) is a form-led candidate with conditional logic, integrations and a reader-facing question flow. It fits feedback that people intentionally open, such as a post-project questionnaire or interview screener. That is a different collection model from automatically triggering a small in-app widget after a product event.

Choose it for evaluation when the presentation and logic of a complete questionnaire matter. A proposed screener could branch based on a respondent's role and end early for people outside the study. Check the actual mobile form, accessibility of the required inputs and the export fields, rather than assume a polished template produces better feedback.

The [current pricing page](https://www.typeform.com/pricing) prominently shows Basic at US$39 per month or $28 per month on annual billing, with 100 responses per month and one user. Plus lists 1,000 responses, three users and branding removal. However, another comparison section contains different Basic price figures. Treat the prominent cards as the displayed offer and confirm the checkout quote before purchasing; this article does not call the conflicting lower amount an assured price.

The potential benefit over your existing setup is the fit for a designed questionnaire and its logic. The tradeoff is collection context and quota. Unlimited forms/questions are not unlimited submissions, and embedding a form does not establish equivalent product-event targeting or a native mobile SDK workflow.

Skip Typeform as a wholesale Survicate replacement if you need recurring in-product feedback with identified users and suppression rules that have not been demonstrated. It can still replace the intentional survey-link part of the programme. Ask which integrations and automations require higher plans or separate add-ons.

## SurveyMonkey for collaborative questionnaires and analysis

[SurveyMonkey](https://www.surveymonkey.com/) offers survey creation and analysis for broader feedback and research programmes. The [team-plan comparison](https://www.surveymonkey.com/pricing/) includes shared assets, user permissions, logic and analytical functions. More advanced logic, multilingual surveys and cross-tabulation have plan dependencies.

Shortlist it when several colleagues need to create, review and analyse a structured questionnaire. A useful proposed evaluation is a feedback study with two segments, a branching section and a team member who should analyse results without editing the live survey. Confirm that the user role permits the actual work instead of sharing a single login.

The pricing page served regional information, including rupee-denominated overage language, while numeric plan prices were not reliably exposed in the reviewed response. This comparison therefore does not invent a universal US starting price. It does verify that team plans require at least three users, Team Advantage lists 50,000 responses per year and Team Premier 100,000. Paid response limits are counted across surveys and users, and Audience responses are purchased separately.

Basic is a limited free plan. The official FAQ says its viewable response limit is shown at signup/login and can be smaller than the number collected, so no guessed 25- or 40-response allowance is stated here. Check export and data access before relying on a free production collection.

The advantage to investigate is team questionnaire/analysis work. The tradeoff is the team minimum, pooled quota and scope of product-triggered collection. Skip it if your goal is simply a one-person contextual app question at a verified low monthly price. SurveyMonkey Audience is also a separate way to obtain participants, not a built-in unlimited research sample included with an ordinary subscription.

## QuestionPro for broader survey and research requirements

[QuestionPro](https://www.questionpro.com/) separates survey software, research, customer experience and employee experience offerings. That separation is useful when the requirement has expanded beyond an occasional NPS prompt into a designed research programme.

The [survey pricing page](https://www.questionpro.com/pricing/) lists Essentials Free with one user and 200 responses per survey. Advanced is $99 per user per month billed annually, with one user and 25,000 responses per year. Team Edition is $83 per user per month annually with a minimum of five users and a 100,000 annual response allowance. The lower per-user Team figure is not a cheaper one-user offer. Research Suite and CX require separate pricing discussions.

Evaluate it if you need a broader questionnaire or a path into specialist research methods. A proposed study should use the question types and logic you actually require, then demonstrate the analytical output and raw export. If the requirement is a CX follow-up programme, ask for that scope rather than assuming the Advanced survey licence contains the whole CX suite.

The potential benefit over Survicate is the fit of the research/questionnaire offering. The limitation is product and package selection. “QuestionPro supports this method” does not mean the method is in the free survey tier. Similarly, the 200-response free allowance is per survey, while paid quoted figures use annual pools; the units should not be compared without context.

Skip it if you only need a narrowly targeted mobile prompt and have not verified that collection surface. Start with a representative survey, confirm collector behaviour and data export, and obtain the correct suite quote for any specialist research or customer-experience requirements.

## SurveySparrow for conversational surveys and CX scope

[SurveySparrow](https://surveysparrow.com/) positions its platform around customer feedback and experience. Its [pricing page](https://surveysparrow.com/pricing/) separates Surveys, CX Suite for NPS/CSAT/CES, Brand, Research and other offerings. That distinction matters: a basic survey subscription is not automatically the whole customer-experience programme shown in a demonstration.

Shortlist it when you want to evaluate a conversational survey format alongside workflow and customer-experience functions. A useful sample is a post-service survey with a low-score follow-up that reaches a named owner. Ask whether the workflow and required connector belong to the proposed plan, and verify that a repeated customer does not receive unwanted duplicates.

The official page describes a permanent Free plan with 75 responses per quarter, three active surveys and 10 questions per survey, after an optional 14-day trial of features. Paid Surveys billing is quarterly or yearly. The numeric paid prices were not visible reliably in the reviewed page, so third-party $19 starting figures are not adopted. Business lists collaboration, custom branding, workflows and webhooks; Enterprise has additional customization and support scope.

The potential advantage is the fit of the survey and feedback programme you demonstrate. The tradeoff is navigating several offerings and quotas. A quarterly free response pool cannot be described as 75 new responses each month, and trial access to advanced functions does not prove they remain in Free.

Skip it if you require a verified paid price before any sales discussion, or if the intended in-app/mobile delivery has not been established in the quoted scope. No higher response or completion rate is claimed based on its conversational positioning.

## Keep feedback comparable during migration

Inventory questions, answer scales, language versions, targeting, exclusions, repeat-survey rules, user identifiers, follow-up destinations and historical exports. Preserve the context alongside the score: the same question asked after onboarding and in an annual email can answer different decisions.

Use a proposed collection pilot with an eligible new user, an excluded existing respondent and a person whose identity changes after login. Verify both display and suppression, then confirm that the user identifier and survey context reach the exported response and the follow-up system. Test a partial response separately if your reporting relies on it.

Do not run two equivalent popups over the same eligible audience without a plan. That can change who responds and annoy the people you want to hear from. Keep a record of collection changes when comparing historical trends rather than treating every movement in the score as a product improvement.

For email or shared links, the [UTM builder](/marketing/utm-builder) can help keep campaign labels consistent when you distribute surveys. It does not identify respondents automatically or replace a survey platform's attribution and privacy controls. Verify how links, identifiers and exported fields actually work in the destination tool.

Export a representative dataset before cancelling the incumbent. Confirm timestamps, user traits, question versions, text answers and score fields, and ask what historical data the new platform can import. A spreadsheet export does not transfer targeting rules, live SDK installation or every dashboard definition. Compare the full cost, including installation, translations, response/traffic pools, integrations and ongoing ownership of follow-ups.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which alternatives are closest for in-product feedback?

Refiner and Qualaroo are strong initial candidates because their official scope includes contextual app/web feedback. Verify event targeting, identified-user handling, suppression and the exact mobile SDK. Other survey platforms belong on the shortlist when their collection model matches the work.

### Which tools have a verified free option?

Qualaroo describes up to 50 free responses with email/pageview limits. QuestionPro Essentials lists 200 responses per survey. SurveySparrow lists 75 responses per quarter, three active surveys and 10 questions each. SurveyMonkey Basic is free but its visible-response limit needs account confirmation. These are different scopes.

### Is Survicate's 25-response offer a permanent free plan?

The current pricing page presents 25 responses within a 10-day feature trial. That does not establish a permanent free production allowance. Confirm any separate free/legacy entitlement directly rather than rely on an older comparison.

### Does unlimited response pricing remove all usage limits?

No. Refiner also uses MAU and plan limits; Qualaroo Business limits pageviews and email sends. Price the real audience and collection surface, not only the number of completed surveys.

### Can Typeform replace every Survicate workflow?

Do not assume so. It is relevant for deliberate forms and survey links, with response/user quotas and plan dependencies. An embedded form alone does not prove the same product-event, identified-user or native-mobile workflow.

### Will our NPS history remain comparable after switching?

Preserve question wording, scale, audience, timing and identity rules, and document changes. Export the old responses and compare definitions before combining datasets. A platform migration by itself does not explain a changed score.

## Pick a collection workflow before a pricing winner

Start with two tools matching the channel and decision. Refiner/Qualaroo suit contextual feedback, Typeform suits intentional forms, SurveyMonkey/QuestionPro suit broader questionnaires, and SurveySparrow needs an explicit survey or CX scope. Keep Survicate when its current programme works. Verify a small collection and export, obtain a quote using the correct unit, then migrate the workflow rather than only copy its questions.
