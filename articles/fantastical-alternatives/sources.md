## Research limits and conflicts

Flexibits pricing rendered numeric amounts as placeholders, so no subscription amount is asserted. Readdle's current homepage puts some functions in its core offer while an older help page describes them as PRO and quotes $19.99/year; an official lifetime announcement quotes $59.99 US with future major-update exclusions. These conflicting pages do not verify today's checkout price or feature entitlement. Omit numerical Readdle pricing and tell readers to inspect their app's offer. Calendar accounts and task integrations can differ by platform and managed-account permissions; no full client-to-client settings or booking-template transfer is claimed.
