# Help Scout pricing source ledger

**Checked:** November 18, 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)

| Claim | Official source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plan prices, annual discount, seat and included Inbox/Docs limits | [Pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) | Monthly view: Standard $25, Plus $45, Pro $75 per user. Annual option shows 16% discount. Included table: Free 5 users/1 Inbox/1 Docs; Standard 25/2/2; Plus 50/5/3; Pro unlimited users (min. 10)/10/5. The page has a separate Inbox maximums sentence that conflicts with included counts; report included values and flag the conflict. |
| Free contacts, saved replies, tags, article and reporting limits | [Pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) | Free: 100 contacts per month, 10 saved replies, 10 tags, 10 Docs articles and 30 days of report history. |
| Additional Inbox and Docs fees | [Billing and Plans Guide](https://docs.helpscout.com/article/596-billing-and-plans-guide) | Extra Inbox $10/month with annual billing or $12 monthly. Additional Docs sites are listed at $20/site/month; no separate monthly rate was verified for Docs. |
| Envoy fee, trial and cap | [Pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) | $0.75 per resolution. Three months of unlimited resolutions; monthly spending cap can disable Envoy for the remainder of cycle. |
| AI resolution definition | [Pricing FAQ](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) | One conversation resolved by Scout without human assistance. Escalation, KB search, follow-up questions or asking for more help do not count. One resolution per conversation. |
| Standard, Plus, Pro feature differences | [Pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) | Standard basic workflows; Plus advanced workflows, routing, customer portal and listed integrations; Pro unlimited workflows/SLAs, routing, SSO/SAML, HIPAA and light users. |
| Trial availability | [Pricing page](https://www.helpscout.com/pricing/) | Standard/Plus trial 15 days without card; Pro requires demo. |

This draft page is desk research. It does not use a customer account or a vendor quote.
