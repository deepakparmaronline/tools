# Editorial notes: Fiverr pricing

- Primary keyword: Fiverr pricing.
- Fiverr is a marketplace, not a conventional subscription SaaS product, so the table separates buyer fees, seller share, Gig minimums, eligible hourly-contract charges and payout costs instead of inventing plan tiers.
- Official Payment Terms list 5.5% buyer service fee plus US$3.50 on purchases under US$200; eligible hourly contracts have a separate US$5 initiation fee and 5.5% of hourly rate.
- Seller earnings are 80% of completed order value. Payout-provider fees and taxes are separate.
- All current details checked 2026-10-31; no hands-on test claimed.
