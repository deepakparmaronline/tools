Why Add a Watermark to Your Images?

Sharing your creative work online always comes with the risk of unauthorized use. Using an Add Watermark to Image tool allows you to overlay a visible logo, brand name, or copyright text directly onto your photos. This acts as a powerful deterrent against image theft, ensures proper attribution when your images are shared on social media, and helps build consistent brand awareness across the web.

How to Watermark Photos Online

Our browser-based tool makes it simple to protect your assets without downloading heavy software. Simply upload your base image, choose between a text or image watermark, and adjust the settings. You can easily tweak the opacity to create a subtle watermark that doesn't distract from the photo, or use the "Tile" position to repeat the text across the entire image for maximum protection. Best of all, your images are processed entirely on your own device—guaranteeing complete privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos safe when I upload them?
Absolutely. This tool operates 100% locally within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your images are never uploaded to a server, stored, or viewed by anyone else. Your privacy is fully guaranteed.
Can I use both text and image logos as watermarks?
Yes! You can select either a "Text Watermark" to quickly type your brand name or copyright symbol, or switch to an "Image Logo" to upload your own transparent PNG brand logo.
Will adding a watermark reduce my image quality?
No, the tool maintains the original resolution of your base image. When you click download, the canvas generates a high-quality file that matches your original photo's dimensions perfectly.
