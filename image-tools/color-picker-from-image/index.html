Why Use an Image Color Picker?

Whether you're a web developer, digital artist, or graphic designer, accurately matching colors from an inspiration photo or a client's logo is essential. Our Color Picker from Image tool removes the guesswork by letting you extract the precise color pixels straight from your files. Instead of relying on your eyes to match a shade, you get mathematical precision in standard formats.

How to Extract Colors from an Image

Using this tool is entirely seamless. Simply click the upload box to select a photo from your device. Once the image appears on the interactive canvas, move your cursor or tap anywhere on the picture to sample that exact pixel. The tool instantly decodes the pixel data and presents you with the matching HEX code (ideal for CSS and web design) and RGB value (standard for digital imaging software). You can copy the values directly to your clipboard with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find the HEX code from an image?
Simply upload your image to our tool, click or tap the specific area of the picture you want to sample, and the exact HEX code will be displayed below for you to instantly copy and use in your designs.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No, our color picker tool works completely locally within your web browser. Your images are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring your privacy and intellectual property are fully protected.
What color formats can I extract?
This tool directly reads the pixel data to extract the exact RGB (Red, Green, Blue) values from your image, and then automatically converts them into standard HTML HEX codes for easy copying.
