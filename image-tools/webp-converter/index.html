Why Should You Convert Images to WebP?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web. Using WebP, webmasters and developers can create smaller, richer images that make the web faster.

1. Massive File Size Reduction

Compared to traditional formats, WebP offers incredible compression. According to Google's data, WebP lossless images are 26% smaller than PNGs. WebP lossy images are 25-34% smaller than comparable JPEG images at an equivalent SSIM quality index.

2. Supercharge Your SEO and Core Web Vitals

Page load speed is a direct ranking factor in Google's algorithm. Large JPG and PNG files slow down your Largest Contentful Paint (LCP). By running your images through our WebP converter, you instantly reduce the payload of your web pages, leading to faster load times, lower bounce rates, and higher rankings on search engines.

3. Retain Transparency (Alpha Channel)

Unlike JPGs which force a solid background, WebP supports an alpha channel (transparency). This means you can convert your transparent PNG files to WebP, retain the transparent background for your graphic designs, and still enjoy file sizes that are up to 3 times smaller.
