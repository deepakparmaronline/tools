What is Article schema?

Article structured data describes an article and its important properties, such as headline, author, image and publication dates. Blog posts and news stories can use more specific article types where appropriate.

How to generate Article JSON-LD

Enter the information shown on your article. Use the real author and publisher details, add a representative image and generate the JSON-LD.

Article structured data tips

Keep dates accurate, use a clear headline and make sure the author and publisher information matches the page. Structured data should not contain misleading information.

FAQ

Should I use Article, BlogPosting or NewsArticle?
Choose the type that best describes the content. BlogPosting is a more specific type for blog posts; NewsArticle is for news content.
Does Article schema improve rankings?
It can help search engines understand the content, but it does not guarantee higher rankings.
