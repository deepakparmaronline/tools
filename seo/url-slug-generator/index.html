Why Use a URL Slug Generator?

A URL slug is the unique part of a URL that identifies a specific page on your website. Using an SEO-friendly URL slug is crucial because it provides both search engines and users with a clear indication of what the page is about. Our URL slug generator automatically converts your titles into lowercase, removes special characters, and replaces spaces with hyphens, creating clean, readable URLs that perform better in search results.

Best Practices for URL Slugs

To rank well, keep your slugs short, relevant, and descriptive. Avoid long strings of numbers or irrelevant parameters. By using our slugify tool, you ensure that your URLs are clean and keyword-optimized, which is a fundamental aspect of on-page SEO.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug?
A URL slug is the part of a URL that appears after the domain name. For example, in example.com/blog/url-slug-guide, "url-slug-guide" is the slug.
Why should I use hyphens in URLs?
Search engines like Google read hyphens as separators, making it easier for them to identify separate words in your URL. This improves keyword relevance and overall readability.
Can I change my URL slugs later?
Yes, but be careful. If you change a slug for an existing page, remember to set up a 301 redirect from the old URL to the new one to avoid breaking existing links and losing SEO authority.
