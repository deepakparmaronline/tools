What is an internal link?

An internal link points from one page on a website to another page on the same website. Strong internal linking helps users move around the site and helps crawlers discover important URLs.

How to check internal links

Paste HTML and enter the site's base URL. The tool separates links by hostname and lists the internal links it finds.

Internal linking SEO tips

Link important pages from relevant content, use descriptive anchor text, avoid unnecessary link repetition and check that important pages are not isolated.
