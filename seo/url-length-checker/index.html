Why check URL length?

Short, readable URLs are easier for people to understand and share. Very long URLs can also contain unnecessary parameters or deep paths that deserve a technical SEO review.

What is a good URL length?

There is no single Google character limit that makes a URL rank or fail. Use the shortest descriptive URL that fits the site's structure. This tool uses practical ranges as guidance, not a search-engine rule.

URL SEO best practices

Keep URLs descriptive, avoid unnecessary parameters, use readable words and keep the structure stable after publishing.
