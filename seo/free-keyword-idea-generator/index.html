          Frequently Asked Questions

          What is the best free keyword research tool?
          While major platforms like Ahrefs or SEMrush offer limited free trials, a dedicated Free Keyword Idea Generator like Tool Box Kart is often best for immediate content ideation. It doesn't restrict your searches or hide long-tail variations behind a paywall, giving you pure semantic ideas instantly.
          How do I generate long-tail keywords?
          Simply type a broad, 1-2 word phrase (like "shoes" or "software") into our Long Tail Keyword Generator. The tool will automatically append modifiers like "best," "for beginners," "how to," and "alternatives" to create a list of easy-to-rank, highly targeted phrases.
          What are buyer intent keywords?
          Buyer intent (or transactional) keywords are terms people search when they are ready to spend money. Examples include words like "buy," "cheap," "discount," "best," and "review." Our SEO Keyword Finder automatically filters and builds these specific variations for you.
