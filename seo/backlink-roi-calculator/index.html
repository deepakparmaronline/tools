Free Backlink ROI Calculator

Compare the value your link building campaign generated against what it cost, and get a clear ROI percentage.

What this calculator measures

Enter the total value your backlink campaign generated and what the campaign cost to run. The tool returns your return on investment as a percentage, using the standard ROI formula: value gained minus cost, divided by cost.

How to estimate value gained

If you track revenue by channel, use the additional revenue attributable to the organic traffic those links helped drive. If revenue tracking is not set up, estimate value using the equivalent cost of buying that same amount of traffic through paid ads, or assign a fixed value per lead or signup the traffic produced.

How to estimate campaign cost

Add up everything spent to earn the links: content production, outreach tool subscriptions, freelancer or agency fees, and any paid guest post or sponsorship costs. Leaving out smaller costs like tools and freelancer time will overstate your real ROI.

Frequently asked questions

How is backlink ROI calculated?
Backlink ROI is calculated as the value gained from the campaign minus its cost, divided by the cost, shown as a percentage: (value gained minus cost) divided by cost, times 100.
What counts as value gained from backlinks?
Common ways to estimate value include the revenue attributable to increased organic traffic, the equivalent cost of buying that same traffic through ads, or a fixed value you assign per qualified lead the traffic produces.
What should be included in campaign cost?
Include everything spent to acquire the links: outreach tool subscriptions, content creation, freelancer or agency fees, and any paid placements or sponsorships.
Is a positive ROI enough to judge a campaign as successful?
ROI is a useful summary number, but also look at link quality, relevance and long-term ranking impact, since some value from backlinks compounds well after the campaign period ends.
