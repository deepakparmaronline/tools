Free Noindex Checker

Check a robots directive for the noindex instruction before you publish or audit a page.

What is noindex?

Noindex tells search engines not to include a page in their search index when the directive is supported and discovered.

How to check for noindex

Paste the robots meta content from your page. The tool reports whether noindex is present along with other common directives.

Noindex SEO checks

Use noindex intentionally. During an audit, compare it with the page's purpose and make sure valuable landing pages, articles and category pages are not blocked from indexing by mistake.
