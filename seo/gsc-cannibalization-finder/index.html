What is keyword cannibalization?

Keyword cannibalization is a practical SEO term for situations where multiple pages compete for the same search topic or intent. Multiple URLs appearing for one query does not automatically mean there is a problem.

How this GSC checker finds candidates

The tool groups your Search Console rows by query, then looks for queries associated with more than one page URL. It ranks the URL with the most impressions as the primary candidate for review.

Why manual review is important

Two pages can legitimately appear for one query. Before merging or redirecting anything, compare intent, content purpose, conversions, links and the actual search results.

What to do when you find a real conflict

Choose the page that best matches the search intent, strengthen its topical coverage and internal links, and decide whether another URL should target a different intent or be consolidated.

FAQ

No. It is only a signal that deserves review.
