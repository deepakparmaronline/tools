What is a readability checker?

A readability checker estimates how easy a piece of text is to read. It can help writers review sentence length, word choice and overall reading difficulty.

What is the Flesch Reading Ease score?

Flesch Reading Ease uses sentence length and syllable estimates. Higher scores generally indicate easier text. The score is a guide, not a quality judgment.

Why readability matters for content

Clear writing helps readers understand information faster. For SEO content, readability should support the audience and topic rather than replacing search intent, expertise or useful information.

How to improve content readability

Break long sentences, use useful headings, remove unnecessary words and choose familiar language when it fits the audience. Keep technical terms when they are needed for accuracy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a readability score?
A readability score is a way to estimate how easy text is to read. This tool uses Flesch Reading Ease plus basic word and sentence metrics.
Is Flesch Reading Ease an SEO ranking factor?
No. A readability score is not a direct Google ranking factor. It can still help writers make content easier for their intended audience.
