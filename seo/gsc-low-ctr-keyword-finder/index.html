What is a low CTR keyword?

A low CTR query receives impressions but a relatively small share of those impressions turns into clicks. Low CTR is not automatically a problem because position, intent and SERP features all affect click behavior.

How to use this finder

Paste a GSC export, set a minimum impression count and choose a CTR threshold. The tool sorts matching rows so you can start with queries that have enough visibility to matter.

What should you check next?

Look at average position first. A page ranking near the bottom of page one may need a different approach from a page ranking in the top three. Then review the title, description, intent match and SERP features.

Do not chase CTR blindly

A clickbait title can raise clicks while hurting satisfaction. The goal is a better match between the searcher, the snippet and the page, not a high CTR at any cost.
