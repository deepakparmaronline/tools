Why Use a UTM Link Builder?

If you are running digital marketing campaigns, you need to know exactly where your traffic is coming from. A UTM Link Builder (or Campaign URL Builder) allows you to add specific parameters to your URLs. This enables Google Analytics to track detailed data, such as which specific ad, email, or social post drove the conversion. Without these tags, your referral data gets messy, and you lose insights into campaign ROI.

How to Create Effective UTM Links

Use consistent naming conventions for your campaign URLs. Always lowercase your parameters to ensure data consistency in your analytics reports. Our UTM generator automates the process, ensuring your tracking strings are perfectly formatted and ready to use in your social media, email, or PPC ads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a UTM parameter?
UTM stands for Urchin Tracking Module. These are simple tags (source, medium, name, term, content) added to a URL to track campaign performance.
What is the difference between source and medium?
Source is where the traffic is coming from (e.g., google, newsletter). Medium is the type of traffic (e.g., cpc, email, organic).
Can I use UTMs for internal links?
No. You should only use UTM parameters for external links that point back to your website. Using them internally can reset your referral data and skew your analytics.
