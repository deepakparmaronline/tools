Free Person Schema Generator

Create Person JSON-LD structured data for authors, consultants, professionals and other people represented by a webpage.

What is Person schema?

Person structured data describes an individual and useful properties such as their name, profile URL, image, job title and public social profiles.

How to use the Person schema generator

Enter information that is publicly shown on the person's profile or author page. Generate the JSON-LD and add it to the relevant page.

Person schema best practices

Use a real profile, accurate job title and genuine profile links. Avoid adding private information or social accounts that do not belong to the person.

FAQ

Can authors use Person schema?
Yes. An author profile can use Person structured data when the page represents that person.
Can I add social profile links?
Yes, where the profiles are public and genuinely belong to the person.
