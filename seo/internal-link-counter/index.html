Free Internal Link Counter

Count internal and external links in a block of HTML and see the link totals at a glance.

Why count internal links?

Internal links are part of site architecture. Counting them can reveal pages with very few links or templates that add an unusually large number of links.

How to count internal links

Paste HTML and enter the site's hostname. The tool counts links by hostname and reports internal and external totals.

SEO use cases

Use the counter during content audits, template reviews and internal linking checks. A high link count is not automatically good; relevance and useful navigation matter more.
