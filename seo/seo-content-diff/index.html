Free SEO Content Diff Checker

Compare old and new page content to see what was added or removed during an SEO update, content refresh or migration.

Why use a content diff checker for SEO?

Small content changes can matter during an SEO refresh. A diff makes it easier to review what an editor changed instead of comparing two long documents by eye.

Useful for content audits and migrations

Use the tool before and after a content update, when reviewing a migration, or when checking whether important sections were removed from a page. It can also help document what changed between two versions.

Browser-only comparison

The tool compares the text locally in your browser. No API, account or server-side content processing is required.

FAQ

Why compare old and new SEO content?
A content diff helps you see what changed during a page refresh, migration or editorial update.
Can this tool compare HTML?
Yes. You can paste plain text or HTML. For HTML, the comparison is based on the text content after basic tag removal.
Does the content leave my browser?
No. The comparison is performed in your browser and does not require an API or server upload.
