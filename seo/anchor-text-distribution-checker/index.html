What is anchor text distribution?

Anchor text distribution describes the mix of phrases used in links. It is useful for reviewing internal linking patterns and spotting templates that repeat the same anchor excessively.

How to check anchor distribution

Paste page HTML and run the tool. It groups identical visible anchor text and sorts the results by frequency.

SEO best practices

Do not chase an artificial percentage for exact-match anchors. Use varied, natural wording that accurately describes the destination and supports the reader's next step.

What a natural anchor mix looks like

Backlink profiles that grow naturally tend to be dominated by branded terms and plain URLs, since that is how most people actually link to a site in normal writing. Exact-match keyword anchors usually make up a small slice of the total, with partial-match and descriptive phrases filling in the rest.

FAQ

Does Google require a specific anchor text ratio?
No. There is no universal ideal anchor-text percentage. As a rough reference, many SEOs suggest keeping exact-match anchors to a small share, often cited around 5 to 10 percent, with branded and natural phrases making up most of the rest.
What is a healthy anchor text distribution?
A healthy distribution is dominated by branded terms and naked URLs, with a smaller share of exact-match and partial-match keyword anchors, and some generic phrases like click here mixed in.
Can this tool check a live backlink profile?
No. It analyzes the anchor text found in HTML you paste in, such as a page's internal links or a backlink export converted to HTML. For a full external backlink profile, use a backlink data source and export the anchor text column.
What should I do if one anchor phrase dominates?
If one exact-match phrase makes up a large share of your anchors, diversify future links using branded, partial-match and natural descriptive phrases so the overall mix looks less manipulated.
