What is Organization schema?

Organization structured data describes an organization, its website, logo and selected business information in a machine-readable format.

How to use this generator

Enter the public information that is true for your organization. Add social profile URLs where relevant, generate the JSON-LD and place it on the appropriate page.

Organization schema tips

Use your real organization name, official URL and a logo that represents the organization. Do not add social profiles or contact details that are not publicly associated with the business.

FAQ

Where should Organization schema go?
It can be added to a relevant organization or home page, provided the information describes the organization on that page.
Is Organization schema a ranking factor?
Structured data is primarily a way to help search engines understand entities and page content. It does not guarantee rankings.
