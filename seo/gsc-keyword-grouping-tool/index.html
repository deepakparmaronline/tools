What does keyword grouping mean?

Keyword grouping is the process of putting closely related searches into one working topic. For SEO, this can help you decide whether a page should target a group of phrases or whether separate pages make more sense.

Use real GSC queries

Paste the query column from your Search Console export. The tool compares meaningful terms and creates transparent groups. You can then review each group against the search results and your existing URLs.

Do not publish one page for every keyword

Related searches often belong to the same page. Grouping helps reduce unnecessary pages, but the final decision should consider intent, page type, competition and what Google already shows.

Why first-party query data helps

GSC shows queries that generated impressions for your property. Grouping those terms can uncover topic families that are easy to miss in a raw spreadsheet.
