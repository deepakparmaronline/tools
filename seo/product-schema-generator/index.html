What is Product schema?

Product structured data describes a product page and important information such as name, image, brand, SKU and offers. It helps search engines understand ecommerce product information.

How to generate Product JSON-LD

Enter the product information exactly as shown on the page. Add current offer details if the product has a purchasable offer, then generate and copy the JSON-LD.

Product schema best practices

Keep price, currency and availability accurate. Do not mark up information that is missing from the page or no longer true.

FAQ

Can I use Product schema for services?
Product structured data is intended for products. Use a more suitable schema type for a service or other page.
Does Product schema guarantee product rich results?
No. Search engines decide eligibility and appearance based on many signals.
