What is pagination SEO?

Pagination is the way a site splits a large collection, such as products or articles, across multiple pages. Each page should have a clear purpose and consistent internal linking.

How to check pagination

Paste the relevant HTML. This tool looks for canonical, rel next and rel prev link elements and counts link tags.

Pagination best practices

Do not rely on old pagination markup as a guaranteed search feature. Make each paginated page useful, crawlable and internally linked. Use canonical URLs that reflect your chosen indexation strategy.

FAQ

Does rel next and prev guarantee indexing?
No. Search engines use many signals and can change how they handle pagination.
