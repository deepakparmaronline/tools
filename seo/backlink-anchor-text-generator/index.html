              What this anchor text generator does

              Enter one target keyword and this tool builds six categories of backlink anchor text around it: exact match, partial match, branded, generic, naked URL and long-tail descriptive phrases. Use the mix when you are doing outreach, guest posting or planning internal links, instead of sending the same exact-match phrase to every site.

              Why anchor text variety matters

              Search engines use anchor text as a relevance signal, but a backlink profile where almost every link uses the exact same keyword phrase looks unnatural and has historically been associated with over-optimization penalties. Real sites get linked to with their brand name, a plain URL, or a generic phrase like "read more" far more often than with a perfect keyword match.

              How to use the anchor variations

              Keep exact match anchors as a small share of your total links. Lean on branded and naked URL anchors for the bulk of your profile, and mix in partial match and long-tail phrases for guest posts and editorial mentions where a more natural sentence is expected.

              FAQ

              How much exact match anchor text is safe?
              Most experienced link builders keep exact match anchors to a small share of the total link profile, often cited around 5 to 10 percent, and lean on branded and natural phrases for the rest.
              What is the difference between exact and partial match anchor text?
              Exact match anchor text uses your target keyword word for word. Partial match uses part of the keyword inside a longer, more natural phrase, which reads less aggressively to both readers and search engines.
              Why include branded anchor text at all?
              Branded anchors, like a company or site name, make up the largest share of a natural link profile because that is how people actually link to sites in normal writing and citations.
              Does this tool check my existing backlink profile?
              No. This tool only generates anchor text ideas from a keyword you provide. To see your current anchor distribution from real backlinks, use a backlink data source such as Google Search Console or a third-party backlink index.
