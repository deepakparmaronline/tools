Free Crawl Budget Calculator

Estimate how many crawls per day would be needed to revisit a site on your target cycle.

What is crawl budget?

Crawl budget is a practical way to think about how much crawling search-engine crawlers can spend on a site. Crawl behavior depends on many signals, including site size, server capacity, freshness and search-engine systems.

How to use this calculator

Enter the number of URLs, your target recrawl cycle and an optional crawl rate. The calculator gives a simple planning estimate. It is not a Google crawl-budget forecast.

Crawl budget SEO tips

Fix duplicate URL variations, redirect chains, broken links, poor internal linking and server problems before trying to force more crawling.

FAQ

Does this predict Googlebot exactly?
No. It is a planning calculator, not a Googlebot measurement tool.
