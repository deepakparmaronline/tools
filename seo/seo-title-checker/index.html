How long should an SEO title be?

There is no universal character limit that guarantees a title will display exactly as written. Search results use available space and can rewrite titles. Keep titles concise, descriptive and focused on the page rather than chasing a fixed character count.

What makes a good SEO title?

  • Describe the page clearly.
  • Use the main topic naturally.
  • Match the searcher's intent.
  • Make the result useful and distinct.
  • Avoid repeating the same keyword.

SEO title checker FAQ

Is 60 characters the SEO title limit?

No. Character count is only a rough editing aid. Search snippets are rendered by available pixel space and can change.

Can a long title hurt SEO?

A long title can be truncated or rewritten, but there is no simple character-count penalty. Clarity and relevance matter more than hitting an exact number.

Does this tool send my title to a server?

No. This checker processes the title in your browser.
