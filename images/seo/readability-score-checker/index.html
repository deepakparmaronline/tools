Optimizing Content for Readability

Does readability score affect SEO? ▾

Directly? No. Google doesn't rank you just because you have a score of 70. However, readability impacts Dwell Time and Bounce Rate. If users can't understand your content easily, they leave. This signals to Google that your page isn't helpful.

What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score? ▾

For standard web content, aim for a score between 60 and 70. This is easily understood by 13-15 year olds.

- 90-100: Very Easy (5th Grade)
- 60-70: Standard (8th/9th Grade)
- 0-30: Very Difficult (Academic/Legal)

How can I improve my readability score? ▾
  • Shorten your sentences (keep them under 20 words).
  • Use simpler words (e.g., use "help" instead of "facilitate").
  • Break up large paragraphs.
  • Use transition words like "however," "therefore," and "because."
