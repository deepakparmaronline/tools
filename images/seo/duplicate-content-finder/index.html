Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good similarity score? ▾

For rewritten content, aim for less than 20-30% similarity. If the score is above 50%, search engines might consider it "Duplicate Content" and canonicalize the original version.

How does the Internet Check work? ▾

Since scanning the entire web requires expensive enterprise APIs, our tool uses a "Smart Sentence" technique. It isolates unique, complex sentences from your text and creates specific Google Search queries. If exact matches appear in Google, your content is likely plagiarized.
