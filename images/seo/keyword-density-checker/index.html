Understanding Keyword Density

What is Keyword Density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a keyword or phrase appears on a web page compared to the total number of words on the page.

How to use this density checker?

Simply paste your content into the box and click "Analyze". Our tool will calculate the word frequency and show you the density percentage for each word.

What is the ideal keyword density for SEO?

Most SEO experts recommend a density of 1% to 2%. Going above this might lead to "Keyword Stuffing" penalties from Google.

Does it count stop words?

Our tool automatically filters out common stop words (like 'the', 'is', 'at') to give you a more accurate keyword analysis.

Why is word frequency important?

By checking word frequency, you can ensure that you are not repeating certain words too often and that your main topic is clearly reflected in your text.

Can I check keyword density for competitors?

Yes, you can copy the text from any competitor's page and paste it here to see which keywords they are targeting.
