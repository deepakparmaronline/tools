# ToolboxKart — Current Rival UX / Design Research

Internal product-design notes. Never render this document or excerpts from it on public pages.

Research date: 2026-09-07

## Sites reviewed

- TinyWow — https://tinywow.com/ and https://tinywow.com/tools
  - Strong: search-first discovery, large category cards, popular-tool surfacing, low-friction promise.
  - Avoid: promotional density and upsell blocks dominating utility pages.
- SmallSEOTools — https://smallseotools.com/
  - Strong: broad discoverability, category grouping, tool-first intent.
  - Avoid: very dense directory presentation and excessive visual competition around the working task.
- CodeBeautify — https://www.codebeautify.net/json-viewer and https://codebeautify.org/
  - Strong: developer tools expose the working editor/actions immediately; output controls are obvious.
  - Avoid: legacy-density feeling and too many adjacent actions for simple tasks.
- RapidTables — https://www.rapidtables.com/
  - Strong: fast, dense utility directory and very broad technical coverage.
  - Avoid: navigation density as the primary visual identity.
- Omni Calculator / comparable specialized calculator sites
  - Strong: specialized inputs, explanatory context, formulas and useful secondary results.
  - Avoid: turning every simple utility into an unnecessarily long reading experience.
- Calculator.net / comparable classic calculator sites
  - Strong: familiar calculator conventions and clear labels.
  - Avoid: ad-heavy or dated visual patterns.
- Diffchecker — https://www.diffchecker.com/
  - Strong: side-by-side working layout, clear options such as ignore whitespace/case.
- Current focused tool sites surfaced during research (ToolboxGenie, SolveKit, Starlight Tools, Eng Bench, etc.)
  - Strong: browser-privacy messaging, explicit formulas, optional advanced inputs, copy/download actions.

## New ToolboxKart design direction

This build intentionally does not copy a rival visual theme. It combines the strongest interaction principles into a distinct ToolboxKart product UI:

1. Light neutral canvas with white working surfaces.
2. Indigo primary action color with teal secondary accent.
3. Large search-led homepage rather than a giant navigation wall.
4. Compact category cards that scale to new fields without redesigning the homepage.
5. Tool pages put the input workspace and result panel before supporting content.
6. Result cards prioritize the number/output users came for.
7. Explanations, formulas, limitations and FAQs come after the working area.
8. No signup friction in the Phase 1 interface.
9. Browser-local processing is stated only when it is true.
10. Internal engineering/AI/competitor notes are never public copy.

## Future design rule

Before a major visual redesign, review current leaders again. Do not assume this 2026 benchmark remains current, and do not blindly imitate any competitor.
