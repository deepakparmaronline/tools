Why Use Our Compound Interest Calculator?

Compound interest is the 8th wonder of the world. Our calculator helps you understand your financial growth by showing how your investments multiply over time. By calculating the future value of your money, you can plan your retirement and savings goals with confidence.

What is the formula for compound interest?
The formula is A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is principal, r is the annual rate, n is compounding frequency, and t is time.
