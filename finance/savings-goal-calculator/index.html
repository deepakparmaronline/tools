Reach Your Financial Targets Quickly

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a savings goal calculator work?
A savings goal calculator takes your final target amount and subtracts what you have already saved. It then divides the remaining amount by your timeline. If you add an interest rate, it uses a financial formula to calculate how much the interest will help you, thereby reducing your required monthly deposit.
Can I use this as a sinking fund calculator?
Yes! A sinking fund is just a way to save up for a specific expense over time (like car repairs or a holiday). This money saving calculator works perfectly for tracking sinking funds. Just enter the amount you need and the months left until you need it.
What if I can't afford the monthly savings amount?
If the monthly amount suggested by the savings target calculator is too high for your budget, you have two options. First, you can extend your timeline by increasing the number of months. Second, you can look for ways to cut back on expenses to free up more cash to save.
