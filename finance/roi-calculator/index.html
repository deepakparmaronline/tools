Understanding Your Return on Investment

Using our free ROI calculator online is the fastest way to evaluate the efficiency of an investment. Whether you are trading stocks, running marketing campaigns, or buying real estate, knowing your profit margin is critical. The core ROI formula subtracts your initial cost from your final value, then divides it by the initial cost to give you a clear percentage.

Tracking your annualized return and overall investment yield helps you compare different financial assets. Smart investors always check their return on capital before scaling up their portfolios.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROI percentage?
A "good" ROI depends on the industry and risk level. Generally, an annualized investment return of 7% to 10% is considered standard for the stock market, while high-risk business ventures often target a 15% to 20% ROI.
How do I calculate ROI manually?
To calculate it manually, take the final value of the investment, subtract the initial cost of the investment to get your net profit. Then, divide that net profit by the initial cost and multiply by 100 to get your ROI percentage.
Can my ROI be negative?
Yes. If your final return value is less than your initial investment amount, you have incurred a loss. Our financial calculator will display this as a negative percentage, indicating a negative return on investment.
