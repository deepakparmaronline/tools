Frequently Asked Questions About Fixed Deposits

How does the compounding frequency affect my FD maturity amount?
The more frequently interest is compounded, the higher your ultimate returns will be. For example, monthly compounding will generate slightly more interest than quarterly or yearly compounding for the exact same principal and time frame.
Is the interest earned on Fixed Deposits taxable?
Yes, the interest income earned from a fixed deposit is fully taxable according to your progressive income tax slab. Additionally, if your annual interest income exceeds ₹40,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens), banks will deduct TDS (Tax Deducted at Source).
What is a tax-saving Fixed Deposit?
A tax-saving FD is a special financial instrument that comes with a mandatory 5-year lock-in period. It allows you to claim tax deductions up to ₹1,50,000 under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. However, premature withdrawals are strictly prohibited on these accounts.
