Frequently Asked Questions

How does a split bill calculator work?
An expense split calculator takes the base bill amount, adds any optional percentages like taxes or tips to get a grand total, and then divides that final number equally by the number of people in your group.
Can I use this tool to split rent and utilities?
Yes! While it is popular for restaurant bills, you can use this split the bill tool to divide monthly rent, groceries, or internet bills evenly among your flatmates. Simply leave the tax and tip fields blank if they don't apply.
Does the tip calculate before or after tax?
In this group expense tracker, both the tip and the tax are calculated based on the original base bill amount entered. This is the standard and most transparent way to calculate extras without paying a "tip on a tax."
