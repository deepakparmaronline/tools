How to Maximize Your Wealth with a SIP

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly in mutual funds. It is one of the most effective strategies for long-term wealth creation because it instills financial discipline and leverages the power of compounding over time.

Our free calculator helps you estimate the future value of your monthly mutual fund investments. By consistently investing through market ups and downs, you benefit from rupee cost averaging, which reduces the impact of short-term market volatility and boosts your long term capital gains.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SIP?
SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It is a method of investing a fixed sum regularly (usually monthly) in a mutual fund scheme, rather than investing a large amount all at once.
How does the SIP calculator work?
The calculator uses the compound interest formula adapted for regular periodic contributions. It calculates your future wealth based on your monthly investment, estimated annual return rate, and the duration of your investment.
Can I increase my SIP amount later?
Yes! Many platforms offer a "Step-up SIP" feature, allowing you to increase your monthly contribution annually as your income grows, which dramatically accelerates your wealth creation.
