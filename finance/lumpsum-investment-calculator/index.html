How to Maximize Your Mutual Fund Returns

A lump sum investment is an excellent way to grow idle cash. Whether you've received a bonus, an inheritance, or simply saved up a large amount, parking it in equity or debt instruments can accelerate your wealth creation.

Our tool uses the standard compound interest formula to estimate the future value of your one-time investment based on your expected rate of return and time horizon. Remember, holding investments for longer periods helps balance market volatility and maximizes the benefits of long term capital gains.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a lumpsum investment?
A lumpsum investment is a one-time, bulk investment into a mutual fund or fixed-income asset, as opposed to staggered investments like a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP).
How are lumpsum returns calculated?
The calculator uses the compound interest formula: Total Value = P(1 + r/100)^n, where 'P' is your initial investment, 'r' is the annual expected rate of return, and 'n' is the number of years.
Is Lumpsum better than SIP?
It depends on the market. If the market is down, a lumpsum investment often yields better returns. However, an SIP is better for averaging out market volatility over time.
