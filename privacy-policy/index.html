Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

This policy explains what Tool Box Kart (toolboxkart.tech) collects when you use this site, and what we do with it. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We do not require an account to use any tool, so we do not collect names, emails, or passwords through signup. When you visit the site, our server automatically logs standard technical details, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit. This is normal for any website and is used to keep the site running and secure.

Your files and tool data

Most tools on this site, including image and file converters, run entirely in your browser. Files you upload to these tools are processed on your own device and are not sent to or stored on our server.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies to keep basic site functions working correctly, and may use cookies for aggregated, anonymous analytics to understand which tools are used the most. These do not identify you personally. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; doing so will not stop the tools themselves from working, since they run locally in your browser.

Third-party services

We use Google Fonts to display text on this site, which involves a request from your browser to Google's servers when you load a page. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Children's privacy

Tool Box Kart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

If you have a question about what data we hold about your visit, or want something removed, email us at privacy@toolboxkart.tech and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@toolboxkart.tech.
